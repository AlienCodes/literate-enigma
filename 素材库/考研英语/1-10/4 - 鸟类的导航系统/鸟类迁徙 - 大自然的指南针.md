**第一段**



------

Twice each year, billions of birds **migrate** between **hemispheres**—a feat of navigation that has long **puzzle**d researchers. How do they **locate** distant destinations with such **fidelity**? After years of study, **ornithologist**s have found that a key part of the answer lies in an **internal** sense **attuned to** Earth's **magnetic field**—a discovery that has earned widespread **acclaim**.

------

每年两次，数十亿只鸟类在**半球**之间**迁徙**——这一导航壮举长期以来 **令** 研究者 **困惑** 不已。它们是如何以如此高的**精确度**定位遥远的目的地的？经过长期研究，**鸟类学家**发现，答案的关键在于一种与地球**磁场**相协调的**内在**感知——这一发现赢得了广泛的**赞誉**。



------

**第二段**

The **primary** breakthrough came from **physicist** Klaus Schulten at the University of Illinois. In the 1970s, he proposed that birds **spot** magnetic signals through chemical reactions in their eyes. The mechanism is **straightforward**: when a **beam** of light enters the eye, it triggers changes in proteins called **cryptochromes**—and **crucially**, Earth's **magnetic** field subtly alters these reactions in ways the brain can **scan**. Just as **radar** picks up invisible radio waves to **pinpoint** a target, the bird's eye **picks up** invisible magnetic signals to pinpoint a direction—effectively **functioning as** a built-in **coherent** compass. Guided by this internal **beacon**, birds can follow its **bearing** and navigate **unerringly** toward their final **destination**. The **analogy** may be simple, yet the science behind it is profound.

**第三段**

Studies at Oxford provided **vivid** proof of this internal compass. Researchers placed European **robin**s in chambers designed to **shield** them from **external interference** while generating **controlled** magnetic fields inside. **Under normal conditions**, birds oriented themselves with ease. But when these fields were **disabled**, the birds themselves became navigationally **disabled**—losing their sense of **axis**, unable to orient themselves. Furthermore, **interference** from radio **broadcasts** also disrupted navigation, causing birds to **lose their bearings**—a clear **expression** of just how sensitive birds' navigation mechanisms are to changes in magnetic fields.

牛津大学的研究为这一内置指南针提供了**生动的**证据。研究人员将欧洲 **知更鸟** 放置在专门设计的房间里——这些房间既能**屏蔽 ** **外界干扰**，又能在内部生成 **可控的** 磁场。**在正常条件下**，鸟类能够轻松确定方向。但当这些磁场被**关闭**时，鸟类自身也变得"导航**残疾**"——失去了对**轴向**的感知，无法确定自身方位。除此之外，来自无线电**广播**的**干扰**也会扰乱导航，使鸟类 **失去方向感**——这清楚地**表明**了鸟类的导航机制对磁场的变化是多么敏感。



**第四段**

> Further research shows that birds **are far from** alone. Scientists have made **giant** strides in their long-term studies, extending this discovery to creatures across land, sea, and sky. **Lobster**s, for example, **crawl** along the seafloor using similar **instincts**. Sea turtles begin to **steer** their course from the **instant** they hatch; their **vision** of the world includes **raw** geomagnetic data—a hidden dimension beyond the human **gaze**. **As such**, the **sphere** of magnetically guided creatures is **apparently** far vaster than once imagined.

------



此外，更多研究表明，鸟类**远非**孤例。科研人员在长期研究中取得了**巨大的**进步，将这一发现扩展到了陆地、海洋和天空中的各类生物。例如，龙虾利用类似的**本能**在海底**爬行**。海龟从孵化的那一**刻**起就开始**掌舵**前行；它们对世界的**视野**包含**原始**地磁数据——一个人类**目光**无法触及的隐藏维度。**由此看来**，受磁场引导的生物**领域\**\**显然**远比曾经想象的更为广阔。

**第五段**

Research into magnetic navigation continues at a **terrific** **rate**. Each year, scientists refine their **method**s and uncover **new details**. One particularly revealing approach involves **exposure** studies conducted at **dusk**, when migratory flocks prepare to **embark on** **objectives** thousands of miles away. During these critical moments, researchers **assess** the birds' **motion** and orientation, gathering data that would **otherwise** remain hidden.

To quantify their findings, scientists rely on precise **metrics** that track navigational **accuracy** across varying **altitude** and latitude. These **measurement**s reveal how animals **concentrate** on magnetic signals despite environmental noise—and how their **tolerance** for interference shifts from one location to another. Equally valuable is the **feedback** from **tagged** birds, which helps refine models of **origin** and destination, **piece by piece** painting an ever-clearer picture of how these creatures find their way.

**At the heart of it** all lies a simple yet powerful truth: magnetic signals **pierce** through clouds, **linger** across continents, and **consistently** guide travelers along invisible pathways. Understanding this natural **rhythm** in full may **demand** decades more work—but with every discovery, we **draw closer to** seeing the world as these **traveler**s do.
