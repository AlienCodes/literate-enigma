This ability manifests remarkably early. Consider a two-year-old who has never heard the word "walked." It is **probable** that she will **generate** the correct past tense spontaneously—without instruction, without correction. No **rehearsal** is required. **Indeed,** from just a handful of examples, she can **generalize** patterns and apply them to novel situations, making her speech **intelligible** to any listener. The brain, it seems, is wired to **amplify** sparse data into complete grammar.

Powerful as this innate machinery is, it is not **indifferent** to the environment. Children raised in **sociable** households—where **communication** flows freely and words fill the air—acquire vocabulary faster. Caregivers who respond with **sympathetic** engagement—**gestures**, eye contact, patience—**reinforce** learning at every turn. All of these factors accelerate the process. Yet while nurture certainly plays its part, the most **integral** element remains our biological instinct—the very **foundation** for mastering one's native language.

> **This evolutionary perspective has gained traction.** Some researchers **incline** toward the view that this ability evolved for survival. In any **community**, the capacity to **register** **verbal** cues provides clear advantages—and so does the ability to produce them. A child who can **announce** needs, ask questions, and **resume** communication after any **halt** integrates **naturally** into **the social world**. **And all of this happens effortlessly.** Language learning has become an **instinctive reflex**—it unfolds spontaneously alongside all other activities, with no need to **suspend** daily life simply to study it.
>
> **Not everyone agrees, of course.** Some critics have tried to **refute** outright the idea that grammar is hardwired; others remain **neutral**, seeking **balance** between nature and nurture. But every **draft** of the argument returns to the same evidence: children worldwide stop making certain **grammatical errors** at roughly the same age—regardless of the language they speak or the culture in which they are raised. This indicates that language development follows a built-in **biological clock**—one encoded in the brain, not shaped by the environment.

> **What, then, should adults do?** The answer is surprisingly simple: **nurture the relationship.** When the **relation** between caregiver and child is warm and responsive, language flourishes naturally. When that bond is strained, language delay becomes more likely—but early intervention can serve as a **remedy**. Such intervention does not require professionals with formal **qualification**—what it requires is a richer **verbal** environment: more talking, more listening, more interaction.
>
> This is because language does not need to be taught—it needs only to be awakened. Every **murmur** from an infant, every **gesture** toward a toy, signals that their linguistic ability is stirring to life. Adults need not **bother** with instruction—what children need is far simpler: love, **compassion**, and a willingness to talk and listen.
>
> **Perhaps this is why** language may be humanity's greatest **treasure**—a treasure unlike any other. Monuments crumble; empires fall to **ruin**. Yet language endures, passed down from generation to generation, continually handed to each new **successor**. And so, in every child who listens and every parent who speaks, the oldest human gift is born anew.

## **每个孩子都会破解的代码**

即使没有系统的**学费式教学**、**百科全书**或**手稿**，每一个健康的婴儿最终都会熟练掌握至少一门语言。从**不识字**的新生儿到能够**口头表达**的孩子，这一飞跃式转变，堪称**社会学**中最引人入胜的谜题之一。

在哈佛大学发展研究实验室，研究人员花费了数十年时间，考察这一过程究竟如何**运作**。他们关于早期**语言学**发展的研究成果揭示了一条核心**原则**：儿童并不是通过单纯的**模仿**或机械的**重复**来学习语言的。相反，他们会从自己无意间**听到的**言语中，提取出**隐性的**规则——这些规则往往是成年人也难以完整**复述**出来的。

设想一个两岁的孩子，从未听过“walked”这个词。她却极有**可能**会自发地**生成**正确的过去时形式，根本不需要任何**排练式练习**。她能够从极其有限的语言输入中进行**概括归纳**，使自己的话语对任何听者而言都是**可理解的**。大脑似乎天生就具备一种能力，能够将稀疏的数据**放大**为完整的语法体系。

这种能力并非对环境**无动于衷**。在**善于交往的**家庭中长大的孩子，如果**交流**自由顺畅，词汇习得速度会明显更快。照料者若以**富有同理心的**关注作出回应——包括**手势**、目光接触和耐心——便会不断**强化**学习过程。然而，核心机制仍然是人类生物学中**不可或缺的**组成部分：一种只需极少刺激即可被激活的**内在基础**。

一些研究者**倾向于**认为，这种能力是在进化过程中为生存服务而形成的。在任何一个**群体**中，能够迅速**捕捉**并理解**语言性**线索，都会带来明显优势。一个能够**表达**需求、提出问题，并在任何**中断**之后迅速**继续**交流的孩子，更容易融入社会结构之中。学习语言无需刻意**中止**其他活动，它始终在持续发生。

批评者曾试图**反驳**语法是“先天内置”的观点；也有人保持**中立**立场，寻求先天与后天之间的**平衡**。但无论这一争论的每一版**论稿**如何变化，证据始终指向同一事实：世界各地的儿童，都会在大致相同的年龄阶段停止犯某些语言错误。

语言或许是人类最珍贵的**财富**。不同于终将走向**废墟**的物质结构，它在每一代**继承者**身上不断重生。婴儿的每一声**呢喃**、每一个指向玩具的**手势**，都在宣告语法的觉醒。
