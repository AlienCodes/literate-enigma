## **Einstein: Prophet of the Cosmos**

​	Albert Einstein was born in 1879 in Ulm, **Germany**. **From an early age**, he showed a **creative** mind and a deep love for **geometry**. During his school years, teachers found his **temperament** difficult—he displayed no **obedience** to **conservative** methods of **rote learning**. His **bold** questioning would later **characterize** his entire career.

​	**As an adult**, Einstein secured a **junior** position at the Swiss Patent Office and, in his spare time, published four **paper**s in 1905—one of which caused an **uproar** in physics. His theory of **relativity** revealed a profound **relationship** between mass and energy, overturning the **classical** framework of physics. Many scientists **scoff**ed, dismissing his claims as **idle boasts**. However, after extensive discussion and **validation** at several major scientific **conference**s, many people's initial **skepticism** **<u>gave way to</u>** **gradual** acceptance.

​	His **personality** was **amiable** yet stubborn—a combination that made him a warm companion but a difficult **partner**. In 1903, he married his first **spouse**, Mileva Marić, but **over the course of** their sixteen-year **marriage**, they **part**ed **ways**, divorcing in 1919. That same year, he remarried Elsa, who **stood by** him through turbulent years with **unwavering** **loyalty**, never once abandoning him even as fame **brought troubles and chaos into** their lives. Einstein showed little **vanity**; his wild hair and **rumpled** **outfit** became **iconic** rather than embarrassing. Colleagues would often **tease** him about his **appearance**, but he never seemed to mind—**on the contrary**, he even felt rather **amused**.

​	Einstein's career **reached its peak** in 1921, when he was **award**ed **the Nobel Prize**. By then, he was treated like **royalty**—a **statesman** of science who traveled the world as an **ambassador** of intellect. His **eloquent** speeches on peace and **democracy** inspired millions, and even **religious** leaders sought his views on faith.

​	After the Nazi **regime** seized power, **hatred** toward Jews and hostile **propaganda** escalated **relentlessly**. Einstein became a target—the Nazi government **confiscated** his property, **tarnish**ed his reputation, and left him no choice but to **depart** Germany. He later **fled to** the United States and was offered a position at the **prestigious** Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, where he eventually swore the **oath** of **allegiance**, becoming an American citizen. Though he found **refuge**, the **terror** of **warfare** still **haunt**ed him daily.

​	Later, Einstein's advice to Roosevelt helped **fuel** the Manhattan Project, though **deep down** he strongly opposed it. The **nuclear** **weapon**s it produced would ultimately kill **hundreds of thousands of** people—even though they **brought the war to an early end**. This left him living in **regret** for the rest of his life. In his later life, he devoted all his time and energy to seeking a unified theory of the **universe**, exploring the **mechanisms** that would **illustrate** nature's deepest secrets. Yet **regrettably**, decades of **labor** did not **pay off** as he had expected.

​	Einstein **passed away** in 1955, and his brain was preserved for **future generations** to study. Today, **marble** monuments and bronze **statues** of him stand to **commemorate** his contributions to the world. The theories he conceived continue to **boost** the **advancement** of physics and **recur** in discussions of **space and time**. **To this day**, he remains one of the greatest scientists of all time—**for** his mind alone **reshape**d humanity's **understanding** of the entire **universe**.

## **爱因斯坦 - 宇宙的先知**

**第一段**

​	阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦于1879年出生于 **德国** 乌尔姆。**自幼**，他便展现出**富有创造力的**头脑，以及对**几何学**深深的热爱。在他上学的时候，老师们觉得他的**性情**难以管教——他对**死记硬背**这种**保守的**教学方法毫不**服从**。他那**大胆**质疑的精神，日后**成为**他整个职业生涯的**标志性特征**。

**第二段**

​	**成年后**，爱因斯坦在瑞士专利局获得了一个**初级**职位，并在工作之余于1905年发表了四篇**论文**——其中一篇在物理学界引起了巨大**轰动**。他的**相对论**揭示了质量与能量之间深刻的**关系**，颠覆了物理学的**经典**框架。许多科学家**嗤之以鼻**，认为他的说法不过是 **虚张声势的吹嘘**。然而，经过了数次大型科学会议的讨论和**验证**之后，很多人从最初的 **质疑** **逐渐** **转变为** 接受。

**第三段**

​	他的**性格**既**和蔼可亲**又固执己见——这种(性格)组合使他成为一个温暖的伙伴，却是一个难以相处的**伴侣**。1903年，他与第一任**配偶**米列娃·玛丽奇结婚，但**在**十六年**婚姻**的**历程中**，他们最终**分道扬镳**，于1919年离婚。同年，他再婚娶了艾尔莎，她在动荡的岁月里以**坚定不移的** **忠诚** **守护在**他身旁，即使名声**给**他们的生活**带来了麻烦和混乱**，也从未离弃过他。爱因斯坦几乎没有**虚荣心**；他那一头乱发和**皱巴巴的** **衣着**非但不令人尴尬，反而成了**标志性**形象。同事们常常拿他的**外表**来**打趣**，但他似乎从不介意——**相反**，他甚至觉得**蛮有趣的**。

**第四段**

​	爱因斯坦的职业生涯在1921年**达到巅峰**，那一年他被**授予**了**诺贝尔奖**。那个时候，他被奉若**王室**成员——一位科学界的**政治家**，以智慧**大使**的身份周游世界。他那关于和平与**民主**的**雄辩**演讲激励了千千万万的人，就连**宗教**领袖也来征询他对信仰的看法。

**第五段**

​	纳粹**政权**夺取权力后，针对犹太人的**仇恨**与敌意**宣传** **不断**升级。爱因斯坦成了被针对的目标——纳粹政府**没收**了他的财产，**玷污**了他的名声，逼得他不得不**离开**德国。他后来**逃往**了美国，并受雇于**久负盛名的**普林斯顿高等研究院，在那里他最终宣誓**效忠**(美国)(入籍前的一种仪式)，成为了美国公民。尽管他获得了**庇护**，但是**战争**的**恐惧**依然日日**萦绕在**他心头。

**第六段**

​	后来，爱因斯坦给罗斯福的建议**助推**了曼哈顿计划，但**其实他的内心**非常反对这样做。这项计划所制造的**核** **武器**最终杀死了**几十万**人——尽管**这些武器提前结束了战争**。这让他的余生一直活在**悔恨**之中。在他的晚年，他将所有的时间和精力致力于寻求一个关于**宇宙**的统一理论，去探索能够**阐明**自然最深层奥秘的**机制**。然而 **遗憾的是**，几十年的**心血**并未  **得到**  他所期待的  **回报**。

**第七段**

​	爱因斯坦于1955年**逝世**，他的大脑被保留了下来，供**后代**研究。如今，**大理石**纪念碑和他的青铜**雕像**矗立着，**纪念**他为这个世界所做出的贡献。他所构想出的理论持续**推动**着物理学的**进步**，并在关于**时空**的讨论中**反复出现**。**直到今天**，他依然是有史以来最伟大的科学家之一——**因为**他一个人的思维，**重塑**了人类对于整个**宇宙**的**认知**。
