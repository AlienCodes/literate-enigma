Without **predator**s, this land became a paradise for elk. 没有了**捕食者**(食肉动物)，这片土地成了麋鹿的天堂。

But **with it came a cost**: the park's ecosystem fell into **disorder**.. 但是**代价也会随之而来**：公园的**生态系统**陷入了一片**混乱**。

They **gnawed at** the **bark** **without restraint**, leaving trunks bare. 它们**肆无忌惮地 啃食 树皮**，使**树干**裸露。

As a result, many trees died. 很多树木因此而死亡。
