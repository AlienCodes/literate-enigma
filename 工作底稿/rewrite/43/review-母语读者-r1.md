## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜2｜−1】P5 结构：“**Whoever** fired the arrow is an **enigma**, and any **motive** is **conjecture**. **Curators** in Bolzano now keep Ötzi. His 61 **tattoos** … **acupuncture**. He has a name…” 问题：凶手之谜与结尾之间插入两句博物馆与文身，打断推进；且结尾 He 紧跟文身句尚可，但若前移凶手句则会指向 whoever。改法（重排，0 词）：“**Curators** in Bolzano now keep Ötzi, whose 61 **tattoos** on joints and **spine** were perhaps an early form of **acupuncture**. **Whoever** fired the arrow is an **enigma**, and any **motive** is **conjecture**. Ötzi has a name, a last meal and a cause of death, while his **anonymous** **killer** has none.”（His→whose、He→Ötzi，净 0 词）
2. 【可改｜2｜−0.5】原文：“The **assumption** was that a **contemporary** **climber** had died on an **alpine** **slope**, since glaciers sometimes yield frozen remains.” 问题：since 给出的理由解释的是“为何会有尸体”，不是“为何以为是现代人”，因果错位。可接受（P1 无余词）；若改：“…, as glaciers sometimes yield the remains of lost climbers.”（+2 词，需他处抵消）。
3. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“a **contemporary** **climber**” contemporary 易读成“同时代的”（与谁同时代？）。改法：“a **modern-day** **climber**”（0 词；速查表同步）
4. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“The **shooter** probably caught him **unaware**.” 惯用语是 catch sb unawares（副词），unaware 为美式口语变体。改法：“…caught him **unawares**.”（0 词）
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“Known as Ötzi, he was a natural **mummy** who lived about 5,300 years ago” 木乃伊不会“生活”。改法：“Known as Ötzi, the natural **mummy** was a man who lived about 5,300 years ago”（+1 词）；同段抵消：“Treating it as a recent death, rescuers…” → “Assuming a recent death, rescuers…”（−2 词）。P2 净 −1。
6. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“he had **dined** on … a **hearty**, **nourishing** meal reportedly **consumed** within two hours” dined 与 consumed、hearty 与 nourishing 两组叠用；加粗占名额，接受。
7. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“They concluded that another person killed him.” 箭从背后射入，此句近乎同义反复，节奏上是空拍；接受（P4 无余词，删则需补）。
8. 【可改｜9｜−0.5】中文“射出那支箭的**究竟是谁**(whoever)是个**谜团**(enigma)”拗口。改法：“**究竟是谁**(whoever)射出了那支箭，至今是个**谜团**(enigma)”。
9. 【可改｜9｜−0.5】中文“他曾**进食**(dined)干野山羊肉”生硬。改法：“他曾**吃过**(dined)一顿干野山羊肉……”或“他的饭食(dined)有……”。
