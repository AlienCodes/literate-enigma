# EN: The Virus That Saved a Life
# ZH: 病毒盟友：噬菌体

## 英文

In late 2015 Tom Patterson, a 69-year-old UC San Diego psychiatry professor, fell ill on holiday in Egypt with his wife, Steffanie Strathdee. Flown home via Frankfurt, he carried Acinetobacter baumannii, which no available **antibiotic** could touch. When a drain slipped, **bacteria** **spilled** into his **abdomen** and bloodstream. Septic shock and a **coma** followed.

Patterson's rescue rested on a discovery nearly a century old. In 1917 Félix d'Hérelle, at the Institut Pasteur, studied samples from a dysentery patient and found a virus that infects bacteria. He called it a **bacteriophage**, **literally** a "bacteria-eater", and proposed using it to treat infections. Frederick Twort had independently reported a similar agent in 1915.

With penicillin and other antibiotics, the West largely abandoned phage therapy after the Second World War. It persisted in the Soviet Union, especially Georgia. George Eliava founded an institute in Tbilisi in 1923, and d'Hérelle worked there in 1933–35. Eliava was executed in 1937, yet phages were sold even over the counter.

Strathdee sought phage researchers. Teams at Texas A&M and the US Navy, which had collected phages from sources including **sewage**, found matching phages. In March 2016 Patterson became the first person in the US given intravenous phages for a **systemic** multidrug-resistant infection. He woke about three days later and was **discharged** on 12 August.

Antibiotic resistance has become a growing global **threat**, and it has revived interest in phage therapy, an approach the West largely set aside. The **bacteria-eaters** discovered in 1917 are being studied again. Patterson's treatment drew on a Navy collection whose phages had been gathered from sources as humble as sewage.

## 中文

2015年末，69岁的加州大学圣迭戈分校精神病学教授汤姆·帕特森与妻子斯特凡妮·斯特拉斯迪在埃及度假时病倒。他经法兰克福被送回国，体内的鲍曼不动杆菌已对所有现有**抗生素**(antibiotic)无动于衷。一根引流管滑脱后，**细菌**(bacteria)**涌入**(spilled)他的**腹腔**(abdomen)和血液。随后出现感染性休克和**昏迷**(coma)。

救帕特森的，是一项近百年前的发现。1917年，巴斯德研究所的费利克斯·德赫雷尔研究一名痢疾病人的样本，发现了一种感染细菌的病毒。他称之为**噬菌体**(bacteriophage)，**字面意思**(literally)是"吃细菌者"，并提出用它治疗感染。弗雷德里克·特沃特早在1915年已独立报道过类似的病原。

随着青霉素等抗生素出现，二战后西方基本放弃了噬菌体疗法。它在苏联延续下来，尤其是在格鲁吉亚。1923年，乔治·埃利亚瓦在第比利斯创办研究所，德赫雷尔于1933至1935年在那里工作。埃利亚瓦于1937年被处决，但噬菌体后来甚至可以在柜台直接买到。

斯特拉斯迪四处寻找噬菌体研究者。德州农工大学和美国海军的团队找到了匹配的噬菌体，海军的噬菌体采自包括**污水**(sewage)在内的多种来源。2016年3月，帕特森成为美国首位因**全身性**(systemic)多重耐药感染而静脉注射噬菌体的病人。约三天后他苏醒过来，8月12日**出院**(discharged)。

抗生素耐药已成为日益严重的全球**威胁**(threat)，这重新唤起了人们对噬菌体疗法的兴趣，而西方曾将它搁置一旁。1917年发现的**吃细菌者**(bacteria-eaters)再次受到研究。帕特森的治疗所依靠的海军样本库，其噬菌体就采自污水这样寻常的来源。
