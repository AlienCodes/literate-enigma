## 总体判断
- 开头：埃及染病、所有抗生素无效、引流管滑脱、昏迷——四句把读者拽进来，好。
- P2 “Help came from a **century-old** idea.” 转场利落。
- 弱点在 P3：两处因果不通（penicillin／early preparations／outcast 三件事挤在一句；Eliava 被枪决 “yet” 鸡尾酒照卖，转折没有落点）。
- 结尾 “Ninety-nine years separated d'Hérelle's discovery from Patterson's first treatment.” 算术成立（1917→2016），但紧跟在全文最好的一句 “…a remedy the West had **forsaken** deserves a second look” 之后，以一个数字收尾，像注脚，把力度泄掉了。

## 必改
1. 【该改｜2｜−1】结尾。建议以 “deserves a second look” 收束，把 99 年移进 P4 的转折点：
   - P4：“In March 2016, ninety-nine years after d'Hérelle's discovery, he became the first US patient…”（+6 词）
   - P5：删末句 “Ninety-nine years separated d'Hérelle's discovery from Patterson's first treatment.”（−9 词）
   P5 收在 “…but it suggests a remedy the West had **forsaken** deserves a second look.” 事实不变，力度明显更强。
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】P3 首句 “After the Second World War, penicillin became medicine's **cornerstone**, early preparations proved less reliable, and phages became a Western **outcast**.” 三个分句并列，early preparations 指谁不明（读者会以为是青霉素），因果顺序也颠倒。改法：“Early phage preparations proved less reliable, and after the Second World War, with penicillin as medicine's **cornerstone**, phages became a Western **outcast**.”（+1 词）
3. 【该改｜2｜−1】P3 末句 “Eliava was shot in Stalin's **purge** of 1937, yet phage **cocktails** were even sold **over the counter**.” yet 与 even 都找不到对照对象，而且没说在哪里卖。改法：“Though Eliava was shot in Stalin's **purge** of 1937, Georgian phage **cocktails** were later even sold **over the counter**.”（+2 词；Georgian 由上文 Soviet Georgia 推出，later 只表示在 1937 年之后，不新增事实）
4. 【该改｜6｜−1】P2 “proposed it as an **antidote**” antidote 专指解毒剂，对细菌感染不地道。改法：“proposed it as a treatment”（0 词；antidote 去粗，加粗变为 30——若要保 31，可改用 remedy，但 P5 已有 remedy，宁可去粗）。

## 可改
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P4 “About three days later he **awakened**” awakened 偏文学；母语者说 woke／came round。改法：“About three days later he woke”（0 词；awakened 去粗或保留均可——若要保加粗，可接受原词）。
6. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P4 “Patterson's wife, epidemiologist Steffanie Strathdee” 零冠词是美式新闻体，英式说 the epidemiologist（+1 词，需另删 1 词，可不改）。
7. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P4 “to have phages **infused** into a **vein** for a **systemic** multidrug-resistant infection” 名词串偏长，可接受。
8. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P2 末 “Frederick Twort had reported a similar agent in 1915.” 放在段尾像补注；可移到 d'Hérelle 句前作让步，或保留。
