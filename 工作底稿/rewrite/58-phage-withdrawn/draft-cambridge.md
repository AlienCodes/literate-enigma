# EN: The Virus That Saved a Life
# ZH: 病毒盟友：噬菌体

## 英文

In late 2015 Tom Patterson, a 69-year-old psychiatry professor at UC San Diego, fell ill on holiday in Egypt. The bacterium Acinetobacter baumannii turned **drug-proof** against every **antibiotic**. Flown home by 12 December, he sank when a **surgical** drain slipped. Bacteria **spilled** through his **abdomen** and **besieged** his bloodstream, leaving him **unresponsive** in a **coma**.

The **weapon** that reached him was a **century-old** idea. In 1917, at the Institut Pasteur, Félix d'Hérelle studied a dysentery patient's samples and found a virus that **preys** on **harmful** **germs**. He named it **bacteriophage**, **literally** a bacteria-**eater**, and proposed it as an **antidote** to infection. Twort had reported a similar agent in 1915.

Postwar, antibiotics like penicillin became medicine's **cornerstone**, and the West dropped phage therapy as a **quaint** **precursor**. It survived in Georgia. George Eliava founded a Tbilisi institute in 1923, where d'Hérelle worked from 1933 to 1935. Eliava was executed in 1937. Phage stayed a **standby**, sold **over the counter** like a **cocktail**.

His **spouse** Steffanie Strathdee sought phage researchers. Texas A&M and US Navy teams, which had collected phages from **sewage**, found matches. The **lab-grown**, **rediscovered** phages were **infused** by **vein**. In March 2016 he received them, a US first, and woke three days later. He was **discharged** on 12 August and returned to work.

Antibiotic resistance is a growing danger that has driven a **resurgence** of interest in phages, almost a **renaissance**. A therapy the West had **forsaken** looked a **miracle**. Patterson's **awakening** amid an **escalating** hunt **stirred** respect for d'Hérelle's idea. The phages that **roused** him had been drawn from sewage, and they shamed an age that had dismissed them.

## 中文

2015年末，69岁的加州大学圣迭戈分校精神病学教授 Tom Patterson 在埃及度假时病倒。他感染的鲍曼不动杆菌对每一种**抗生素**(antibiotic)都变得**抗药的**(drug-proof)。12月12日前他被送回家中，一根**外科**(surgical)引流管滑脱后病情急转。细菌**溢入**(spilled)他的**腹部**(abdomen)，**围攻**(besieged)其血液，令他**无反应地**(unresponsive)陷入**昏迷**(coma)。

救他的**武器**(weapon)是一个**百年之久的**(century-old)构想。1917年，在巴斯德研究所，Félix d'Hérelle 研究一名痢疾病人的样本，发现了一种**捕食**(preys)**有害的**(harmful)**病菌**(germs)的病毒。他将其命名为**噬菌体**(bacteriophage)，**字面上**(literally)即一个细菌**食者**(eater)，并提出把它用作对抗感染的**解药**(antidote)。Twort 曾在1915年报告过一种类似的物质。

战后，青霉素等抗生素成为医学的**基石**(cornerstone)，西方遂将噬菌体疗法当作**古旧的**(quaint)**前身**(precursor)而弃置。它在格鲁吉亚延续下来。George Eliava 于1923年在第比利斯创建研究所，d'Hérelle 于1933至1935年在那里工作。Eliava 于1937年被处决。在当地，噬菌体一直是常见的**常备品**(standby)，像**鸡尾混合剂**(cocktail)一样**可柜台购买**(over the counter)。

他的**配偶**(spouse) Steffanie Strathdee 四处寻找研究噬菌体的人。德州农工大学与美国海军的团队从**污水**(sewage)中采集噬菌体，找到了匹配者。这些**实验室培养的**(lab-grown)、被**重新发现的**(rediscovered)噬菌体经由**静脉**(vein)**注入**(infused)。2016年3月，作为美国首例，他接受了治疗，约三天后苏醒。他于8月12日**出院**(discharged)并重返工作。

抗生素耐药正成为日益严峻的威胁，并推动了人们对噬菌体**重新兴起**(resurgence)的兴趣，几近一场**复兴**(renaissance)。一门曾被西方**弃绝的**(forsaken)疗法，忽然显得像个**奇迹**(miracle)。Patterson 的**苏醒**(awakening)，在寻找新药不断**升级**(escalating)之际，**激起**(stirred)了人们对 d'Hérelle 构想的敬意。将他**唤醒的**(roused)噬菌体采自污水，令曾摒弃它们的时代蒙羞。

## 史实与来源

- late 2015, Tom Patterson (69, UC San Diego psychiatry professor) fell ill on holiday in Egypt with wife Steffanie Strathdee; flown to Frankfurt 3 Dec, San Diego 12 Dec; Acinetobacter baumannii resistant to all available antibiotics; drain slipped, bacteria spread to abdomen and bloodstream, septic shock, coma — S1
- 1917, Félix d'Hérelle at the Institut Pasteur used a dysentery patient's samples to find a virus that infects bacteria; named it bacteriophage ("bacteria-eater"); proposed treating infections; Frederick Twort independently reported a similar agent in 1915 — S2
- after WWII, with penicillin and other antibiotics, phage therapy largely abandoned in the West; persisted in the Soviet Union, especially Georgia; Eliava Institute, Tbilisi, founded 1923 by George Eliava (who met d'Hérelle in Paris); d'Hérelle worked in Tbilisi 1933–35; Eliava executed 1937; preparations sold over the counter — S3
- Strathdee sought phage researchers; Texas A&M and US Navy teams (Navy phages collected from sources including sewage) found matching phages; March 2016 first US patient given intravenous phages for a systemic multidrug-resistant infection; woke about three days later; discharged 12 Aug 2016; returned to work — S1 S4
- rising antibiotic resistance has renewed interest in phage therapy (general) — S5
