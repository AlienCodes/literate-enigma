## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】P2 重复。原文：“in monitoring the **sleepless** boy **closely**. The two **investigators** kept an **attentive** eye on him, noting each **fluctuation** in his state as the **deprivation** **progressively** **deepened**.” 问题：monitoring closely 与 kept an attentive eye 同义叠用。改法：“in monitoring the **sleepless** boy **closely**. Both **investigators** were **attentive** to each **fluctuation** in his state as the **deprivation** **progressively** **deepened**.”（−5 词；加粗不变）。另记：investigators 在研究语境可接受，不扣；progressively deepened 可接受。
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】结尾。原文：“The teenager who had **bounced** back after a single long rest grew into a man who could **barely** **doze**.” 改法：“The teenager who had **bounced** back after a single long rest became, half a century later, a man who could **barely** **doze** off.”（+4 词）doze off＝入睡，对得上失眠；补时间避免误读为成年即失眠（2017 年采访说约十年前开始，即 1963 年后约半个世纪）。
3. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：“for a **regional** fair” 改法：“for a **regional** science fair”（+1 词）
