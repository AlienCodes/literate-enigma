# Article 31: Steve Jobs, source excerpts (all [search] snippets unless tagged [ref])

S1 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs [search]: "born on February 24, 1955 in San Francisco ... adopted ... by Clara and Paul Jobs"; "After the company's board of directors fired him in 1985, he founded NeXT the same year and purchased Pixar in 1986"; "returned to Apple in 1997 as CEO".
S2 https://www.networkworld.com/article/933422/wozniak-calls-apple-garage-origin-a-myth.html [search]: Wozniak: "The garage is a bit of a myth. It's overblown ... we did no designs there." Most Apple I work was done at Woz's HP cubicle.
S3 https://www.cultofmac.com/?p=305088 (and similar) [search]: Apple founded as a partnership on 1 April 1976 by Wozniak, Jobs and Ronald Wayne (41). Wozniak called the exact date "murky".
S4 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_II_(original) [search]: Apple II launched at the West Coast Computer Faire, 17 April 1977 (shipping began June 1977 [ref]).
S5 https://mac-history.net/?p=3418 [search]: Apple went public 12 Dec 1980, raising about $110m; Mac launched 24 Jan 1984 at $2,495, announced by the "1984" commercial.
S6 https://mac-history.net/2008/10/30/showdown-at-apple-john-sculley-vs-steve-jobs ; https://apple-history.com/h3 ; https://forums.appleinsider.com/discussion/141179 [search]: 24 May 1985 board meeting; the board sided with Sculley; Jobs was removed as head of the Macintosh division and lost all operating duties but stayed chairman; he resigned about five months later. Sculley has said he "never fired" Jobs.
S7 mac-history.net timeline (https://mac-history.net/?p=1348) [search]: Jobs resigned as chairman in September 1985 (one source says 16 Sept). The 17 Sept 1985 resignation letter date is [ref]. Use "September 1985".
S8 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixar ; https://computerhistory.org/blog/pixars-true-story/ [search]: Graphics Group spun off as Pixar, 3 Feb 1986. Jobs paid $5m to Lucasfilm plus $5m capital into the company ($10m total).
S9 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story [search]: "first feature-length computer-animated film", released 22 Nov 1995.
S10 https://www.upi.com/Archives/1995/11/29/Pixar-goes-public-soars/9307817621200/ ; cultofmac [search]: Pixar IPO 29 Nov 1995 at $22, high $49.50; Jobs (~80% owner) became a billionaire.
S11 https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/5547/Apple-buys-NeXT-and-Steve-Jobs-returns/ [search]: 20 Dec 1996, Apple to buy NeXT for $429m plus 1.5m Apple shares; Jobs returned as an adviser.
S12 https://www.mactech.com/1997/09/16/md1-jobs-interim-ceo/ [search]: Board named Jobs interim CEO at its September 1997 meeting (16 Sept 1997).
S13 https://appleinsider.com/articles/18/08/06/august-6-1997----the-day-apple-and-microsoft-made-peace ; mentalfloss [search]: 6 Aug 1997, Microsoft bought $150m of non-voting Apple stock and committed to Office for Mac for 5 years; Apple dropped its lawsuit and made IE the Mac's default browser.
S14 https://www.soundandvision.com/content/flashback-1997-apple-launches-'think-different'-campaign [search]: "Think Different" slogan introduced 8 Aug 1997 (Macworld Expo); TV campaign debuted 28 Sept 1997.
S15 https://yourstory.com/2024/09/steve-jobs-apple-comeback-strategy-tech-giant-success [search, secondary]: Jobs said at D8 (2010) that Apple had been about 90 days from going bankrupt. This is Jobs's own later claim, not an audited figure.
S16 https://www.westporttechmuseum.com/blogs/techtastic/today-in-tech-history-august-15th-1998-apple-releases-the-original-imac ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMac [search]: iMac first shipped 15 Aug 1998 (it was announced 6 May 1998 [ref]).
S17 https://www.npr.org/2021/10/23/1048706632/20-years-ago-the-ipod-was-born ; ilounge [search]: iPod announced 23 Oct 2001 at $399. iTunes Music Store introduced 28 Apr 2003.
S18 https://history.com/this-day-in-history/january-9/steve-jobs-debuts-the-iphone [search]: iPhone unveiled at Macworld San Francisco, 9 Jan 2007, and went on sale in the US on 29 June 2007.
S19 https://www.newsweek.com/chiefs-gradual-decline-68137 ; https://venturebeat.com/business/steve-jobs-refused-potentially-life-saving-surgery-biographer-confirms [search]: October 2003, diagnosed with a pancreatic islet-cell (neuroendocrine) tumour. He delayed surgery about nine months, until July 2004.
S20 https://news.stanford.edu/?p=44946 [search]: Stanford commencement address, 12 June 2005.
S21 https://fortune.com/2011/08/24/fallen-apple-steve-jobs-resigns ; https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/steve-jobs-to-resign-as-apple-ceo [search]: Jobs resigned as CEO on 24 Aug 2011. Tim Cook became CEO and Jobs became chairman. Jobs had been on medical leave since 17 Jan 2011.
S22 https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/october-5/apple-founder-steve-jobs-dies [search]: Died 5 Oct 2011, aged 56, of complications of pancreatic cancer.
S23 https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2011-08-09/apple-rises-from-near-bankruptcy-to-become-most-valuable-company ; https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2011/aug/10/the-ipad-trumps-oil-apple-is-most-valuable-us-co/ [search]: Apple briefly passed Exxon Mobil intraday on 9 Aug 2011 and first closed above it on 10 Aug 2011. Exxon later regained the lead at times.

## Myths to avoid (claim 11)
- Do not write "started in a garage" as literal fact (S2).
- Do not say he "invented" the PC or the smartphone. Wozniak engineered the Apple I/II. Products were team efforts (e.g. Jony Ive for design) [ref].
- Do not use a flat "fired"; the board removed his duties and he resigned (S6).
- Present "90 days from bankruptcy" as Jobs's own claim (S15).
- Avoid quotes such as "sugar water" and "stay hungry" unless attributed to a verified source. Many quotes credited to Jobs are misattributed.
- He did not leave Apple with nothing; he sold nearly all his shares in 1985 [ref].
- Pixar was not a film studio when he bought it; it began as a hardware company [ref].
