# EN: The Secret of Rome's Undying Concrete
# ZH: 罗马混凝土的千年之谜

## 英文
Nearly 1,900 years after its **completion**, in about AD 125–128, the Pantheon's **dome** still **crowns** the **vast** **chamber** below. About 43 metres in **diameter**, it remains the largest unreinforced concrete dome in the world, with no steel inside. It is an **astonishing** **feat** of **architecture**, and a **stubborn** **puzzle**.

Modern concrete is usually strengthened with **embedded** steel bars, which are **prone** to **corrosion**. As the steel **rusts**, it **swells** and **fractures** the concrete around it, and many structures are designed for a **lifespan** of around 50 years. Roman concrete is not stronger than its modern **counterpart**; its **merit** lies in **durability**.

Yet the ancient material seemed to carry a **shortcoming**. It is **dotted** with small white **chunks** of lime, known as lime clasts. Experts long **ascribed** these **lumps** to **sloppy** mixing or **inferior** raw **ingredients**, the kind of **defect** a more **conscientious** builder would have avoided.

A study published in Science Advances in January 2023 offered a different **interpretation**. The clasts, it suggested, were a **by-product** of "hot mixing" with quicklime, a **reactive** lime that heats in water. When water **seeps** into a crack, the clasts **dissolve** and the **calcium** recrystallises as calcium carbonate, **plugging** the gap. In tests, water **ceased** to **trickle** through cracked hot-mixed samples within weeks, but kept flowing through samples made without quicklime.

The **implications** **extend** beyond **archaeology**. Cement **manufacture** accounts for about 8 per cent of global carbon dioxide **emissions**, according to a Chatham House report published in June 2018. Longer-lasting structures could make **demolition** and **renovation** rarer, and **trim** emissions with them. The white lumps once **scorned** as **clumsy** work have **prompted** a **rethink**.

## 中文

万神殿约于公元125至128年**竣工**(completion)，近1,900年后的今天，它的**穹顶**(dome)依然**高踞**(crowns)于下方**宏大的**(vast)**殿堂**(chamber)之上。穹顶**直径**(diameter)约43米，内部不含钢筋，至今仍是世界上最大的无钢筋混凝土穹顶。它是**建筑**(architecture)史上**令人惊叹的**(astonishing)**壮举**(feat)，也是一道**久解不开的**(stubborn)**谜题**(puzzle)。

现代混凝土通常靠**嵌入**(embedded)其中的钢筋加固，而钢筋**容易**(prone)发生**锈蚀**(corrosion)。钢筋**生锈**(rusts)时会**膨胀**(swells)，把周围的混凝土**撑裂**(fractures)；许多建筑的设计**使用寿命**(lifespan)只有50年左右。罗马混凝土并不比现代的**同类材料**(counterpart)更坚固，它的**长处**(merit)在于**耐久性**(durability)。

然而，这种古代材料似乎带着一个**缺点**(shortcoming)。它的内部**点缀**(dotted)着白色的石灰小**碎块**(chunks)，称为"石灰团块"。长期以来，专家把这些**疙瘩**(lumps)**归因于**(ascribed)搅拌**马虎**(sloppy)或原料**成分**(ingredients)**低劣**(inferior)，认为这种**瑕疵**(defect)本该由更**认真负责的**(conscientious)工匠避免。

2023年1月发表于《科学进展》（Science Advances）的一项研究提出了不同的**解释**(interpretation)。该研究认为，这些团块是用生石灰"热混合"留下的**副产品**(by-product)；生石灰是一种遇水发热、**活性强的**(reactive)石灰。水**渗入**(seeps)裂缝时，团块会**溶解**(dissolve)，其中的**钙**(calcium)重新结晶为碳酸钙，**堵住**(plugging)缝隙。试验中，几周之内，水就**不再**(ceased)从开裂的热混合样本中**细细流过**(trickle)，而在不含生石灰的样本中，水却一直流个不停。

这项发现的**意义**(implications)**超出**(extend)了**考古学**(archaeology)的范围。据查塔姆研究所（Chatham House）2018年6月发布的一份报告，水泥**生产**(manufacture)约占全球二氧化碳**排放**(emissions)的8%。建筑更经久耐用，**拆除**(demolition)与**翻修**(renovation)就可能减少，排放也会随之**削减**(trim)。这些白色团块曾被**轻视**(scorned)为**拙劣的**(clumsy)手艺，如今却**促使**(prompted)人们**重新思考**(rethink)。
