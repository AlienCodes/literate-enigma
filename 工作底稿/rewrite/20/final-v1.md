# EN: The Brain That Learned London
# ZH: 被街道重塑的大脑
## 英文

Name two addresses and a London cabbie will **outline** the route **instantly**. That **prowess** is **hard-won**. To earn a **licence**, **aspiring** drivers must pass "the Knowledge", an **arduous** **examination** on about 25,000 streets within six miles of Charing Cross. **Apprentices** typically spend three to four years **committing** this **labyrinth** to memory, and only about half **qualify**.

A study published in PNAS in April 2000 **scanned** 16 male cabbies and 50 other men. The hippocampus is a seahorse-shaped structure **vital** to memory and **navigation**. In the cabbies, its rear, or **posterior**, **segment** was larger than in the other men, and a front, or **anterior**, **region** smaller. Longer **tenure** **accompanied** a larger rear region.

Yet the link left the cause **uncertain**. Perhaps an **innate** **flair** for **spatial** tasks **attracted** people to the **occupation**, or perhaps **stressful** driving was **responsible**. A study published in December 2006 **dispelled** the second **notion**: cabbies had more rear grey matter than bus drivers of **equivalent** **mileage** **confined** to **predetermined** routes.

A **longitudinal** study published in December 2011 **tackled** the first, scanning 79 **trainees** and 31 **peers** at **enrolment** and three to four years later. Only the 39 who qualified showed an **expansion** of grey matter, the tissue **dense** with **nerve** cells, in the rear hippocampus. The **sequence** strongly suggests that learning **preceded** the growth.

The gain appeared to **entail** a **trade-off**: qualified drivers **underperformed** those peers on one visual memory test. The **cohorts** were small and **exclusively** male, yet the studies **imply** the healthy adult brain can **remodel** itself through **intensive** learning. Each street a driver mastered seemed to **occupy** room once **claimed** by something else.

## 中文

随便说出两个地址，伦敦的出租车司机就能**立刻**(instantly)**勾勒**(outline)出路线。这份**本领**(prowess)**来之不易**(hard-won)。要拿到**执照**(licence)，**有志入行的**(aspiring)司机必须通过「知识大考」（The Knowledge，伦敦出租车执照考试的俗称）——一场**艰巨的**(arduous)**考试**(examination)，考查查令十字周边6英里内约25,000条街道。**学徒**(Apprentices)通常要花三到四年才能把这座**迷宫**(labyrinth)**记**(committing)在脑子里，而最终**合格**(qualify)的只有大约一半。

2000年4月，《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）发表的一项研究**扫描**(scanned)了16名男性出租车司机和50名其他男性的大脑。海马体是一个形似海马的结构，对记忆和**导航**(navigation)**至关重要**(vital)。司机们海马体靠后的部分，即**后部**(posterior)**区段**(segment)，比其他男性的大；而靠前的一块，即**前部**(anterior)**区域**(region)，则较小。**从业年限**(tenure)越长，**伴随**(accompanied)的后部区域也越大。

然而，这种关联让原因仍**不确定**(uncertain)。也许是**天生的**(innate)**空间**(spatial)**天赋**(flair)把人们**吸引**(attracted)到这个**职业**(occupation)中来，也许是**紧张的**(stressful)驾驶本身**起了作用**(responsible)。2006年12月发表的一项研究**打消**(dispelled)了第二种**想法**(notion)：与驾驶**里程**(mileage)**相当**(equivalent)、但**局限于**(confined)**固定预设的**(predetermined)线路的公交司机相比，出租车司机后部的灰质更多。

2011年12月发表的一项**纵向**(longitudinal)研究**着手解决**(tackled)第一种疑问：它在**入学**(enrolment)时以及三到四年后，两次扫描了79名**学员**(trainees)和31名**对照者**(peers)。只有最终合格的39人，后部海马体的灰质出现了**增长**(expansion)；灰质是**密布**(dense)**神经**(nerve)细胞的组织。这一**先后顺序**(sequence)有力地表明，学习**先于**(preceded)增长发生。

这种收获似乎**牵涉**(entail)一种**此消彼长的取舍**(trade-off)：在一项视觉记忆测试中，合格司机的**表现不如**(underperformed)那些对照者。这些研究的**样本群体**(cohorts)规模小，而且**清一色**(exclusively)是男性，但它们**意味着**(imply)，健康的成人大脑能够通过**高强度**(intensive)学习**重塑**(remodel)自身。司机掌握的每一条街道，似乎都**占据**(occupy)了一块原本**归**(claimed)其他东西所用的空间。
