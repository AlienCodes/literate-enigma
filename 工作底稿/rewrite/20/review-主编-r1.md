## 核实结果
- PNAS 2000年4月：16名男司机 vs 50名对照、后部大前部小、年限相关 —— 属实（PMC18253）。
- Knowledge：约25,000条街、查令十字6英里、3–4年、约一半通过 —— 属实（ScienceDaily 2011-12-08）。
- Hippocampus 2006年12月：公交司机与出租车司机**按驾驶经验和压力匹配**、走固定线路；出租车司机后部灰质更多 —— 属实（https://www.fil.ion.ucl.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Maguire2006.pdf ；https://www.themantic-education.com/ibpsych/2019/02/01/key-study-london-taxi-drivers-vs-bus-drivers-maguire-2006/）。原文无“里程相当”之说。
- Current Biology 2011年12月：79学员+31对照，39人合格，仅合格者后部灰质增加；合格者一项视觉记忆测试差于对照；**入组时各组无显著差异** —— 属实（https://www.lakeforest.edu/news/plasticity-of-adult-human-brains ；WoollettMaguireCBDec2011.pdf）。

## 逐条问题
1. 【该改·项1 −2】“bus drivers of **equivalent mileage**” —— 来源是按 driving experience（年限）和 stress 匹配，不是里程。且文中“stressful driving”这一解释正是靠“压力匹配”排除的，不写压力，排除就推不出。
   改：`bus drivers of equivalent mileage confined to predetermined routes` → `bus drivers of equivalent experience and stress confined to predetermined routes`（+2词）
   中文：`与驾驶**里程**(mileage)**相当**(equivalent)、但` → `与驾驶经验和压力**相当**(equivalent)、但`
   速查表：删 mileage 行（重点词 55→54）。
2. 【该改·项2 −2】因果缺一拍：第3段提出“天生 flair”假说，第4段只说合格者事后增长，没交代**起点相同**，“learning preceded the growth”对 innate 假说的排除不完整。
   改：`The sequence strongly suggests that learning preceded the growth.` → `All had started alike, so the sequence suggests learning preceded the growth.`（+3词）
   中文：`这一**先后顺序**(sequence)有力地表明，学习**先于**(preceded)增长发生。` → `各组起点并无差别，因此这一**先后顺序**(sequence)表明，学习**先于**(preceded)增长发生。`
3. 【该改·项3 −1】“or perhaps stressful driving was **responsible**”——responsible 无宾语，指代悬空（对什么负责？）。
   改：`or perhaps stressful driving was responsible` → `or perhaps stressful driving was responsible for the difference`（+3词）。若词数不够，可接受不改，但建议改。
   中文：`也许是**紧张的**(stressful)驾驶本身**起了作用**(responsible)` → `也许是**紧张的**(stressful)驾驶本身**造成了**(responsible)这种差异`；速查表释义不变。
4. 【压词数，无扣分】`A study published in December 2006` → `A December 2006 study`（−2）；`A longitudinal study published in December 2011` → `A December 2011 longitudinal study`（−2）。中文不变。

**词数**：269 +2 +3 −4 = **270**（≤271）。若同时采纳第3条（+3）则 273 超限，需再删：`scanned 16 male cabbies and 50 other men` → `scanned 16 male cabbies and 50 other men` 无可删；建议改为 `In the cabbies, its rear, or posterior, segment was larger than in the other men` → `In the cabbies, its rear, or posterior, segment was larger` 不可（丢比较对象）。故第3条改用不加词版：`or perhaps stressful driving was responsible` → `or perhaps the stress of driving was responsible`（+1，仍悬空，不推荐）。**推荐方案：采纳1、2、4，第3条换成 “or perhaps stressful driving caused it”**——会丢 responsible（54→53词，仍≥50），词数 269+2+3−4+0=270（"was responsible"→"caused it" 词数相同）。中文：`驾驶本身**起了作用**(responsible)` → `驾驶本身造成了这种差异`，速查表删 responsible 行。
