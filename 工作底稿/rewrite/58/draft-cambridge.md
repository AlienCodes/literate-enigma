# EN: The Bird That Beat the Letter Home
# ZH: 比信先到家的鸟

## 英文

In Boston on 3 June 1952, Rosario Mazzeo freed a Manx shearwater flown by **airliner** from its **burrow** on Skokholm, an **isle** off Wales. He posted a letter there at once. Thirteen days later the **feathered** **traveller** was back on its **doorstep**, after a **prodigious** 3,200 miles of **unbroken** Atlantic. The letter **limped** in **afterwards**.

Such an **odyssey** demands a compass, and the first **hints** were **celestial**. In the 1950s Gustav Kramer **cooped** starlings in cages and **faked** the sun's position with mirrors. The **duped** birds **swivelled** to match, allowing for its **westward** **arc**. Stephen Emlen later **cloaked** stars in a planetarium's **vault**, and his **nocturnal** buntings steered by the constellations around the **pole-star**, the **axis** of the **nightly** **whirl**.

In 1972 Wiltschko showed that European **robins** read the **magnetic** field. Unlike a **sailor**'s **needle**, their compass gauges the **inclination** of field lines, not polarity. In 1978 Klaus Schulten proposed a light-driven chemical reaction as the sensor. By 2000 cryptochrome, a **protein** in the **retina**, was the **front-runner**. In 2021 the robin's proved more **finely** **tuned** than that of chickens or **pigeons**. Proof in a living bird is **awaited**.

In 2014 Oldenburg researchers found robins in wooden campus **huts** **robbed** of that sense. Grounded aluminium **cladding** **smothered** the **din**, and the birds **reclaimed** their **bearings**. Removing the grounding, or **piping** noise in, **bewildered** them again. The double-blind result was **unambiguous**. The **interference**, a **murmur** from everyday **appliances**, sat far below WHO safety limits.

How a bird knows where it is remains **unanswered**. The compass is partly **cracked**, but the **atlas** that carried the shearwater to Skokholm has yet to be read.

## 中文

1952年6月3日，罗萨里奥·马泽奥在波士顿放飞了一只大西洋鹱。它来自威尔士外海的斯科克霍姆**小岛**(isle)，是从**巢穴**(burrow)里被带走、搭乘**客机**(airliner)飞过来的。马泽奥当即往岛上寄了一封信。十三天后，这位**长着羽毛的**(feathered)**旅行者**(traveller)已回到自家**门口**(doorstep)，身后是**惊人**(prodigious)的3,200英里**连绵不断**(unbroken)的大西洋。那封信**一瘸一拐**(limped)地**随后**(afterwards)才到。

如此一场**远征**(odyssey)需要罗盘，而最初的**线索**(hints)来自**天体**(celestial)。20世纪50年代，古斯塔夫·克雷默把椋鸟**关**(cooped)进笼子，用镜子**伪造**(faked)太阳的位置。**受骗**(duped)的鸟儿随之**转身**(swivelled)，还会把太阳**向西**(westward)划过的**弧线**(arc)算进去。后来，斯蒂芬·埃姆伦在天文馆的**穹顶**(vault)下把星星**遮住**(cloaked)，他那些**夜行**(nocturnal)的靛蓝彩鹀便依靠**北极星**(pole-star)周围的星座辨向，北极星正是群星**每夜**(nightly)**回旋**(whirl)所绕的**轴心**(axis)。

1972年，威尔奇科证明欧亚鸲**知更鸟**(robins)能读取**磁**(magnetic)场。它们的罗盘不同于**水手**(sailor)的**磁针**(needle)，测的是磁力线的**倾角**(inclination)，而不是极性。1978年，克劳斯·舒尔滕提出，感受磁场的是一种由光驱动的化学反应。到2000年，**视网膜**(retina)里的一种**蛋白质**(protein)——隐花色素——成了**头号候选**(front-runner)。2021年，知更鸟的这种蛋白被证明比鸡和**鸽子**(pigeons)的调得更**精细**(finely)、更**灵敏**(tuned)。在活鸟体内的证据仍**有待**(awaited)取得。

2014年，奥尔登堡的研究人员发现，校园里木**屋**(huts)中的知更鸟被**剥夺**(robbed)了这种感知。接地的铝制**外壳**(cladding)把**嘈杂**(din)**压**(smothered)了下去，鸟儿便**找回**(reclaimed)了**方向**(bearings)。拆掉接地，或往屋里**灌**(piping)入噪声，又让它们**晕头转向**(bewildered)。双盲实验的结果**毫不含糊**(unambiguous)。这种**干扰**(interference)是日常**电器**(appliances)发出的**低语**(murmur)，强度远低于世卫组织的安全限值。

鸟如何知道自己身在何处，至今**悬而未决**(unanswered)。罗盘已被部分**破解**(cracked)，而把这只鹱带回斯科克霍姆的那本**地图册**(atlas)，还无人读懂。
