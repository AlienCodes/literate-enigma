# EN: The Bird That Beat the Letter Home
# ZH: 比信先到家的鸟

## 英文

On 3 June 1952 a Manx shearwater was **unpacked** in Boston and freed. It had been **scooped** from its **burrow** on Skokholm, off Wales, and **bundled** onto an **airliner**. Rosario Mazzeo **dashed** off a letter at once. Twelve and a half days later the **feathered** **traveller** **reappeared** on its **doorstep**, an **astounding** **homecoming** across 3,200 miles of **open-ocean**. The letter **limped** in after.

In the 1950s Gustav Kramer kept starlings **cooped** in round cages, shifting the apparent sun with mirrors. The starlings **swivelled** to match, allowing for the sun's **arc** across the **daylight** sky. In the 1960s Stephen Emlen blacked out stars in a planetarium. His indigo buntings steered by stars that **whirl** round the North Star, the **axis** of the **celestial** **vault**.

Other **hints** are **magnetic**. In 1972 Wiltschko showed that European robins read the **inclination** of Earth's field lines, not their **polarity**. In 1978 Klaus Schulten proposed a **light-triggered** chemical process to **decode** the field. By 2000 the **foremost** suspect was cryptochrome, a **protein** in the **retina**. In 2021 robin cryptochrome 4 proved magnetically sensitive in a **test-tube**, more so than chickens' or **pigeons**'. The case is **suggestive**, not yet **nailed** down in a living bird.

The **sting** came in 2014. At Oldenburg, robins in campus **huts** were **wrong-footed**. In huts **cloaked** in grounded aluminium, they **reclaimed** their **bearings**. **Disconnecting** the grounding, or **piping** in **interference**, **foiled** them again. Double-blind tests blamed the **buzz** of everyday electronic **appliances**, a **whisper** far below WHO safety limits.

The inner **atlas** that tells a robin where it is stays **opaque**, its **workings** **unexplained**. How AX6587 found Skokholm from Boston has **stumped** science.

## 中文

1952年6月3日，一只大西洋鹱在波士顿被**拆箱取出**(unpacked)，随即放飞。几天前，它从威尔士外海斯科克霍姆岛的**洞穴巢**(burrow)里被**一把捞起**(scooped)，又被**匆匆塞上**(bundled)一架**客机**(airliner)。罗萨里奥·马泽奥当即**草草写下**(dashed)一封信寄出。十二天半之后，这位**长着羽毛**(feathered)的**旅行者**(traveller)**重新出现**(reappeared)在自家**门口**(doorstep)——横越约3200英里**开阔大洋**(open-ocean)，完成了一次**令人震惊**(astounding)的**归家之旅**(homecoming)。那封信则**蹒跚**(limped)来迟。

20世纪50年代，古斯塔夫·克雷默把椋鸟**关**(cooped)在圆形笼子里，用镜子改变太阳的视位置。椋鸟随之**转向**(swivelled)，还会把太阳划过**白昼**(daylight)天空的**弧线**(arc)计算在内。60年代，斯蒂芬·埃姆伦在天文馆里遮掉部分星星。他的靛蓝彩鹀靠那些绕北极星**旋转**(whirl)的星星辨向；北极星正是**天球**(celestial)**穹顶**(vault)的**转轴**(axis)。

另一些**线索**(hints)与**磁**(magnetic)有关。1972年，威尔奇科证明欧亚鸲读取的是地磁场磁力线的**倾角**(inclination)，而不是**极性**(polarity)。1978年，克劳斯·舒尔滕提出，一种**由光触发**(light-triggered)的化学过程能让鸟**解读**(decode)磁场。到2000年，**头号**(foremost)嫌疑对象是隐花色素——**视网膜**(retina)里的一种**蛋白质**(protein)。2021年，知更鸟的隐花色素4在**试管**(test-tube)中显示出对磁场的敏感，比鸡和**鸽子**(pigeons)的更强。证据颇具**启发性**(suggestive)，但尚未在活鸟体内**坐实**(nailed)。

2014年，**意外一击**(sting)到来。在奥尔登堡，校园**小木屋**(huts)里的知更鸟**乱了阵脚**(wrong-footed)。在**包裹**(cloaked)着接地铝皮的屋子里，它们**找回**(reclaimed)了**方向**(bearings)。**断开**(disconnecting)接地，或往屋里**输入**(piping)**干扰**(interference)，又让它们**失灵**(foiled)。双盲实验把矛头指向日常电子**电器**(appliances)发出的**嗡嗡声**(buzz)——那只是远低于世卫组织安全限值的一声**低语**(whisper)。

告诉知更鸟"身在何处"的那本内在**地图册**(atlas)依然**不透明**(opaque)，其**运作机制**(workings)**无从解释**(unexplained)。AX6587如何从波士顿找到斯科克霍姆，至今**难倒**(stumped)了科学。
