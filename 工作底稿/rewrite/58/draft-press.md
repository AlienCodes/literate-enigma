# EN: The Bird That Beat the Letter Home
# ZH: 比信先到家的鸟

## 英文

At 8:15 on 3 June 1952, a Manx shearwater was **unpacked** and freed at Boston's Logan Airport. Rosario Mazzeo had carried it from its **burrow** on Skokholm, off Wales, by **rail** and **airliner**, then posted the island a letter. Twelve and a half days later, after a 3,200-mile **odyssey** across **mid-ocean** **emptiness**, the **wanderer** **resurfaced** on its **doorstep**. The **courier** crept at a **snail**'s pace, and Ronald Lockley assumed the bird had been released in London.

Gustav Kramer began to **decode** such **prodigious** **bearings** in the 1950s. When mirrors created the **illusion** of a shifted sun, caged starlings **swivelled** to match, reading it like a **dial** and allowing for its **hourly** **arc**.

In the 1960s Stephen Emlen **curtained** off patches of a planetarium's **celestial** **vault**. Indigo buntings, he found, steer by the constellations around the **pole-star**, the **axis** of the sky's **nightly** **whirl**.

In 1972 Wiltschko showed that European **robins** read Earth's **magnetic** field by its **inclination**, not by the **polarity** a **sailor**'s **needle** follows. In 1978 Klaus Schulten proposed a **light-driven** reaction, and in 2000 cryptochrome, a **protein** in the **retina**, became chief suspect. Robin cryptochrome 4 proved magnetically responsive in 2021 laboratory tests, **outdoing** that of **poultry** and **pigeons**, though unproven in living birds.

In 2014 Oldenburg added an **eerie**, **man-made** twist. Robins **cooped** in wooden **huts** were **bewildered**. Huts **clad** in earthed aluminium **sheeting** let them **reclaim** their bearings, and removing the earthing or adding electronic **racket** **derailed** them again. The **interference**, from **commonplace** **appliances**, sat far below WHO safety limits.

How a shearwater knows where it is, the **atlas** behind the needle, is not yet fully **unravelled**.

## 中文

1952年6月3日上午8点15分，一只大西洋鹱在波士顿洛根机场被**取出**(unpacked)放飞。罗萨里奥·马泽奥把它从威尔士外海斯科克霍姆岛的**洞巢**(burrow)里带出来，先乘**火车**(rail)，再搭**客机**(airliner)，随后给岛上寄了一封信。十二天半之后，这位**漫游者**(wanderer)飞越三千二百英里**大洋中央**(mid-ocean)的一片**空茫**(emptiness)，完成一场**远征**(odyssey)，重新**现身**(resurfaced)于自家**门口**(doorstep)。相比之下，**信使**(courier)慢如**蜗牛**(snail)，罗纳德·洛克利还以为这只鸟是在伦敦放的。

古斯塔夫·克雷默在20世纪50年代开始**破解**(decode)这种**惊人**(prodigious)的**方向感**(bearings)。镜子制造出太阳移位的**假象**(illusion)，笼中的椋鸟随之**转身**(swivelled)，把太阳当作**表盘**(dial)来读，还会顾及它**每小时**(hourly)划过的**弧线**(arc)。

20世纪60年代，斯蒂芬·埃姆伦把天文馆里**天体**(celestial)运行的**穹顶**(vault)一片片**遮住**(curtained)。他发现，靛蓝彩鹀依靠**北极星**(pole-star)周围的星座辨向，那里正是夜空**每晚**(nightly)绕之**旋转**(whirl)的**轴心**(axis)。

1972年，维尔奇科证明欧亚鸲能读取地球的**磁场**(magnetic)，看的是磁力线的**倾角**(inclination)，而不是**水手**(sailor)罗盘**指针**(needle)所循的**极性**(polarity)。1978年，克劳斯·舒尔滕提出一种**光驱动**(light-driven)的化学反应；2000年，**视网膜**(retina)中的一种名为隐花色素的**蛋白质**(protein)成了头号嫌疑。2021年的实验室测试中，**知更鸟**(robins)的隐花色素4对磁场有反应，**胜过**(outdoing)**家禽**(poultry)和**鸽子**(pigeons)的同类蛋白，不过在活鸟体内尚未得到证实。

2014年，奥尔登堡大学的研究带来一个**诡异**(eerie)而且**人为**(man-made)的转折。**关押**(cooped)在木头**小屋**(huts)里的知更鸟**晕头转向**(bewildered)。小屋**包覆**(clad)接地的铝制**护板**(sheeting)后，它们**找回**(reclaim)了方向；拆掉接地或在屋里加入电子**噪声**(racket)，它们又被**带偏**(derailed)。这种**干扰**(interference)来自**寻常**(commonplace)**电器**(appliances)，强度远低于世卫组织的安全限值。

大西洋鹱怎样知道自己身在何处，也就是指针背后的那张**地图**(atlas)，至今尚未被完全**解开**(unravelled)。
