## 新稿逐条问题

1. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“His concise essay reached Darwin on 18 June 1858. Its argument paralleled his own almost precisely; Darwin told Lyell…” 问题：前句主语是 His（华莱士的）essay，Darwin 只作宾语；紧接 “his own” 母语读者第一反应会指回华莱士，指代不清。改法：“Its argument paralleled Darwin's own almost precisely; he told his mentor Lyell he had never seen…”（Darwin's 换 his、he 换 Darwin 为 0 词；“his mentor” +2、但删去 “Darwin” 一词后净 +1 ——计：his→Darwin's 0，Darwin→he 0，加 his mentor +2；如需 ≤+1，可只加 “mentor” 之外改为 “he told Lyell”，0 词，不解决第 2 条）
2. 【可改｜4｜−0.5】Lyell 全文只以姓出现、无身份交代（r1 稿 “his friend Charles Lyell” 已删），考生不知是谁。改法见第 1 条 “his mentor Lyell”（地质学家莱尔是达尔文导师式友人；或 “his friend Lyell” 同为 +1 词：Darwin→he 0，加 his friend +2，−1 需另删，故推荐 “mentor” 也为 +2）。为守 ≤+1：将第 1 条与本条合并为 “…paralleled Darwin's own almost precisely; he told his friend Lyell…”，同时末句改 “He never named it after himself.”（−2，见第 4 条）抵消，净 0。
3. 【可改｜4｜−0.5】“its first impression” 的“印次”义考生陌生，正文无提示。保词可接受；若不改，速查表已覆盖，仅保留扣分。
4. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：“…a lengthy exposition of the theory and entitled it Darwinism. Wallace never named the theory after himself.” 问题：the theory 两句连用，Wallace 再次重复主语，收尾拖沓。改法：“He never named it after himself.”（−2 词）
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：“In February 1858, two decades after Darwin's first insight, Alfred Russel Wallace, a naturalist afflicted by fever in the Malay Archipelago, independently conceived the same idea.” 问题：主语前后各压一个插入成分，谓语到第 25 词才出现，考生难断句。改法（0 词）：“In February 1858, two decades after Darwin's first insight, Alfred Russel Wallace independently conceived the same idea, a naturalist afflicted by fever in the Malay Archipelago.”——此倒置同位语也不理想；更好：“…Alfred Russel Wallace, a naturalist afflicted by fever in the Malay Archipelago, conceived the same idea independently.” 仍长；可接受，仅保留 0.5 扣分，不强制。
