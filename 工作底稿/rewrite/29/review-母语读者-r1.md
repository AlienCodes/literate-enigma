## 逐条问题

1. 【该改｜3｜−1.5】原文：“Ultimately, on 24 November 1859 came Darwin's treatise, On the Origin of Species.” 问题：前句是“反响平淡、年度无革命性发现”，ultimately 在此无承接对象，读者不知“最终”什么；倒装 came 的主语悬空。改法：“Ultimately, the stir came on 24 November 1859, with Darwin's treatise, On the Origin of Species.”（+3 词）
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“His priority seemed threatened.” 问题：priority 单用，母语者与考研生都会先读成“优先事项”；学术语境固定说 claim to priority。改法：“His claim to priority seemed threatened.”（+2 词）
3. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“Darwin was grieving an infant son lost to scarlet fever on 28 June.” 问题：on 28 June 可挂 grieving 也可挂 lost，歧义。改法：“Darwin was grieving: scarlet fever had killed his infant son on 28 June.”（0 词，保留 grieving/infant/scarlet）
4. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“remarked the year had been barren of…” 问题：remark 后省略 that 在书面语中不自然。改法：“remarked that the year had been barren of…”（+1 词）
5. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“In October 1858, before the book existed, Wallace signified his acceptance…” 问题：上一段已到 1859 年 11 月，此处倒回 1858 却用一般过去时、靠 “before the book existed” 补救，时序跳且这一插入语拙。改法：“Wallace had already signified his acceptance in October 1858, assuring Hooker in a cordial letter of his satisfaction with the arrangement.”（−1 词）
6. 【该改｜6｜−1】原文：“Neither contributor attended the proceedings.” 问题：contributor 指给刊物供稿者，宣读论文的人母语者说 author；用法不典型。改法：“Neither author attended the proceedings.”（0 词；重点词减为 53 个，仍 ≥50；速查表与中文“供稿人”同步删改为“作者”）
7. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】原文：“had subscribed for the entirety of its first impression” 问题：entirety 最典型用法是 in its entirety；“the entirety of” 在此偏生硬。改法（保词）：“had subscribed for its first impression, 1,250 copies, in its entirety.”（0 词）
8. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】原文：“a lengthy exposition of the doctrine” 问题：doctrine 偏宗教/政治教义，科学理论母语者说 theory（华莱士书副标题即 Theory）。若保留该词可接受，但建议换 “of the theory” 并换掉该重点词。改法：“a lengthy exposition of the theory”（0 词）
9. 【可改｜2｜−0.5】原文：“The compromise may have kept rivalry…” 问题：联合宣读是第三方安排，不是双方让步的“妥协”；用 arrangement 更准，但 arrangement 已在 P5 用。可改为 “The joint reading may have kept rivalry…”（+1 词），或保留。
10. 【可改｜4｜−0.5】“first impression” 的“印次”义考生陌生，正文无提示；可改 “first printing”，或接受并依赖速查表。
11. 【可改｜9｜−0.5】中文“林奈学会是伦敦的一个科学学会”附在句尾，像注释。改：“……在伦敦的科学学会——林奈学会同时宣读”。

另记（不扣分）：“read simultaneously” 实为同一会议先后宣读，史学著作常这样说，可接受；如追求精确可加 “at the same meeting”。
