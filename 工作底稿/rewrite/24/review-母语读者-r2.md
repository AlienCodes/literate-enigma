## 逐句通读意见（重点句）

- "warmer air pressed down like a lid, holding the chill captive beneath"：地道，比喻单一干净，无问题。
- "the capital's smoke was trapped too, thickening into a choking smog"：自然。
- "reassessed the evidence and cast doubt on that explanation, estimating…"：地道，三个动作衔接顺畅。
- "Reform proved protracted, slowed by official inertia."：protracted 与 slowed 意思略重，但母语者会这么写，不扣。
- "empowered municipal councils"：英式更常说 local councils/local authorities，但 municipal councils 无误，不扣。
- 结尾：见第2条。
