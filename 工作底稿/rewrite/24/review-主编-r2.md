## 逐条问题
1. 【可改｜项1｜−1】原文：“and **pedigree** **cattle** at the Smithfield **agricultural** show died or had to be **slaughtered**.”
   问题：来源（S13、核准表 T6）只写 prize cattle；Smithfield Show 参展的多为肥育商品牛，未必是“纯种”。属来源外具体化，易误解。
   改法（词数不变，271）：“and **prize** **cattle** at the Smithfield **agricultural** show died or had to be **slaughtered**.” —— 若 prize 不宜作重点词，则不加粗，重点词由 52 减为 51（仍 ≥50），并相应改中文“纯种(pedigree)”→“获奖的”，速查表删 pedigree 行。
2. 【该改｜项3｜−1】原文：“One **villain** **flickered** in their own homes, in a source of **warmth** and **comfort** so **habitual** that few thought to **accuse** it.”
   问题：两个 in 并列，第二个 “in a source of warmth” 让 villain 好像“在一个温暖来源里”闪烁，指代糊。母语者会把“温暖来源”作 villain 的同位语。
   改法（词数 270）：“One **villain** **flickered** in their own grates, a source of **warmth** and **comfort** so **habitual** that few thought to **accuse** it.” 中文同步：“有一个**元凶**(villain)就在他们自家炉栅里**摇曳**(flickered)，它带来……”
3. 【可改｜项5｜−1】comfort、response 仍为高频词（同 r1 第6条），由语言学家终判；可不改。
