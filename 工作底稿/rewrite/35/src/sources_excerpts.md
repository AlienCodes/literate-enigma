## Myths
- "Built it alone" — false: the Manhattan Project had >125,000 staff at peak; Los Alamos alone had thousands (S21); Groves was overall head.
- "Communist spy" — no evidence; AEC did not allege divulging secrets (S20); DOE 2022 affirmed loyalty (S12).
- Gita line spoken at Trinity — documented recollection is from 1965 (S5).
- Truman's exact words — second-hand, varying (S7).
- "One day before expiry" — DOE: two days before consulting contract expired (S11).
