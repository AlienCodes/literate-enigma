# EN: The Stink That Built London's Sewers
# ZH: 大恶臭逼出了下水道

## 英文

In the **oppressive** summer of 1858, curtains in the **riverside** Houses of Parliament were soaked in chloride of lime, a **bleach** **powder**, to **suppress** the **sickly** air **creeping** off the Thames. **Honourable** members **contemplated** leaving the **precincts** **altogether**.

The river was the **outlet** for the **untreated** **sewage** of some three million Londoners, **dumped** through **antiquated** sewers. **Stewed** by the sun, the **festering** **excrement** gave off **vapours** so **overpowering** and **repulsive** that the episode is known as the Great Stink.

On 15 July the Chancellor, Benjamin Disraeli, called the river a **hellish** pool **reeking** with **unbearable** horrors. The **legislature** **consequently** acted with **urgency**, and the **enactment** of a bill to fund new sewers took about 18 days.

The **mastermind** of the new **sanitation** **infrastructure** was the engineer Joseph Bazalgette. His 82 miles of intercepting sewers caught outflows before they reached the river and **diverted** them east, **downstream** of the city. Some 1,100 miles of street sewers fed this **backbone**, and the **masonry** took about 318 million **bricks**. He worked out the **calibre** needed, then increased it **twofold**. The main lines lie **buried** **underneath** the **newly** built Victoria, Albert and Chelsea Embankments. **Substantially** complete by 1875, much of the system is in use today.

Miasma, the **dominant** but **misguided** **belief**, held that disease spread through foul, **harmful** air. The **objective**, **accordingly**, was the **removal** of the smell. The sewers worked **nevertheless**. They kept sewage out of the water city **dwellers** drank.

In 1866 cholera killed about 5,600 people in the East End, the only **sector** of the **metropolis** whose **linkage** to the system was **pending**. The capital suffered no major cholera epidemic afterwards.

## 中文

1858年那个**闷热难耐**(oppressive)的夏天，**临河**(riverside)而建的议会大厦里，窗帘都浸过氯化石灰，这是一种**漂白**(bleach)用的**粉末**(powder)，为的是**压住**(suppress)从泰晤士河**悄悄漫进来**(creeping)的那股**令人作呕**(sickly)的气味。**尊敬的**(Honourable)议员们甚至**考虑**(contemplated)过**干脆**(altogether)撤离议会**辖区**(precincts)。

这条河是约三百万伦敦人**未经处理**(untreated)的**污水**(sewage)的**出口**(outlet)，污水经由**老旧**(antiquated)的下水道**倾倒**(dumped)进来。在烈日**炖煮**(Stewed)之下，**溃烂发酵**(festering)的**粪便**(excrement)散发出的**臭气**(vapours)如此**难以抵挡**(overpowering)、如此**令人厌恶**(repulsive)，这段往事因此被称为"大恶臭"。

7月15日，财政大臣本杰明·迪斯雷利称这条河是一潭**地狱般的**(hellish)池水，**散发着**(reeking)种种**难以忍受的**(unbearable)恐怖。**立法机构**(legislature)也**因此**(consequently)带着**紧迫感**(urgency)行动起来，一项为新下水道拨款的法案只用了约18天就完成**立法**(enactment)。

这批新建**环境卫生**(sanitation)方面的**基础设施**(infrastructure)，其**总设计师**(mastermind)是工程师约瑟夫·巴泽尔杰特。他修建的82英里截流下水道在污水入河之前将其截住，**改道**(diverted)东去，送往城市**下游**(downstream)。约1,100英里的街道下水道汇入这条**主干**(backbone)，**砌筑工程**(masonry)共用了约3.18亿块**砖头**(bricks)。他先算出所需的**管径**(calibre)，再把它**扩大一倍**(twofold)。主干管**埋设**(buried)在**新近**(newly)修起的维多利亚、阿尔伯特和切尔西河堤**底下**(underneath)。到1875年，整个系统已**基本**(Substantially)完工，其中大部分至今仍在使用。

瘴气说是当时**占主导地位**(dominant)却**误入歧途**(misguided)的**观念**(belief)，认为疾病经由污浊**有害**(harmful)的空气传播。**相应地**(accordingly)，修建的**目标**(objective)是**除去**(removal)臭味。下水道**终究**(nevertheless)奏效了。它们让污水不再进入城市**居民**(dwellers)饮用的水。

1866年，霍乱在东区夺去约5,600人的生命。在这座**大都市**(metropolis)里，东区是唯一一个与系统的**接驳**(linkage)工程还**悬而未决**(pending)的**区域**(sector)。此后，首都再没有发生过大规模霍乱疫情。

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】到1858年约三百万伦敦人的污水经老旧下水道和排污口直接排入泰晤士河；1858年夏天炎热，腐烂污水发出难以忍受的恶臭，史称"大恶臭"（英文 untreated、dumped、antiquated、Stewed by the sun、excrement 为对 sewage 直排、old sewers、hot summer、rotting sewage 的描写性转述）。S1
- 【T2】新议会大厦窗帘浸氯化石灰（英文 a bleach powder）压住臭味；议员考虑过搬离（contemplated leaving the precincts）。1858年7月15日财政大臣迪斯雷利把河形容为冥河般的臭池，英文以 hellish pool reeking with unbearable horrors 转述，不直接引用；拨款法案约18天成为法律（urgency、enactment）。S2
- 【T3】巴泽尔杰特修建约82英里截流下水道，入河前截住、送往城东下游；约1,100英里街道下水道；约3.18亿块砖；算出所需管径后加倍（twofold）；主干管在新修的维多利亚、阿尔伯特和切尔西河堤之下；1875年基本完工，大部分至今在用。S3
- 【T4】当时主流为瘴气说（dominant belief），认为疾病经污浊空气传播；下水道主要为除臭而建（objective…removal of the smell）；奏效是因为污水不再进入伦敦人饮用水。misguided 依据约稿单"瘴气说是错的"的反转。S4
- 【T5】1866年霍乱在东区夺去约5,600人，东区是唯一尚未接入截流系统的地区（英文 whose linkage to the system was pending）；此后伦敦再无大规模霍乱。克罗斯内斯泵站1865年启用一事因篇幅未写。S5
