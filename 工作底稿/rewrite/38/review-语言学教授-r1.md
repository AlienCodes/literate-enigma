## 逐条问题（均给净词数；各段须保持 ≥10 重点词，全文 ≤271）

1. **可改｜第6项｜−0.5**
   原文：An eleven-year-old English girl **nominated** the name, after the Roman **deity** of the **underworld**.
   问题：nominate 的典型宾语是人（nominate sb for/as），给事物提名字说 suggest/propose/put forward；suggest 为 ✗基础，proposed 已占。
   改法：An eleven-year-old English girl **put forward** the name, after the Roman **deity** of the **underworld**.（put forward 为 △短语，判**够格**：put forward a proposal/theory 是考研写作与阅读高频搭配；used_words 未占）同段配套删 1 词：a **dim** dot that had shifted between them → a **dim** dot that shifted between them。**净词数 0**；P1 重点词仍 10。中文"**提议**(put forward)"不变；速查表 nominated 行换为：put forward | phr. v. 提出（建议、理论等）（put forward a plan/theory） | 1。

2. **可改｜第6项｜−0.5**
   原文：the **astronomer** Clyde Tombaugh **perceived** a **dim** dot
   问题：perceive 偏指心理感知或"认为"（be perceived as），比对底片"发现、辨认出"一个小点，母语者用 spot/detect/pick out；detected、discerned 已占，spotted 为基础。
   改法：the **astronomer** Clyde Tombaugh **picked out** a **dim** dot（picked out 为 △短语，判**够格**："辨认出、挑出"义有学习价值；used_words 未占；+1 词）。同段须再删 1 词保持 271：An eleven-year-old English girl → An eleven-year-old girl（English 属可选细节，史实表 T2 未列为★必写）。**净词数 0**。中文"**辨认出**(picked out)"；速查表 perceived 行换为：picked out | phr. v. (pick out) 辨认出；挑选（pick out a face in the crowd；pick out the best ones） | 1。
   ——若不想删 English，可保留 perceived，只扣此 0.5。

3. **可改｜第6项｜−0.5**
   原文：passed resolutions **affirming** Pluto as a planet.
   问题：affirm 典型接 that 从句或抽象名词（affirm one's support），"affirm X as Y"少见。
   改法：passed resolutions **affirming** that Pluto was a planet.（+1 词）同段删 1 词：The American Dialect Society made the **playful** **slang** verb "plutoed", to **devalue**, its word of 2006 → …made the **playful** **slang** verb "plutoed", to **devalue**, its 2006 word（−1，"of"）——如嫌 its 2006 word 不自然，则本条不改。**净词数 0**。中文不变。

4. **可改｜第9项｜−0.5**
   原文：就在**争议**(controversy)之中，一幕带有**讽刺**(irony)意味的情节早已在**途中**(transit)。
   问题："一幕情节在途中"搭配不通，直译 an irony was in transit。
   改法：就在一片**争议**(controversy)声中，颇具**讽刺**(irony)意味的事早已在**途中**(transit)。（英文 an irony was already in transit 是有意的修辞，可保留，不扣英文）

   改法：被**重新归类**(reclassified)为**矮行星**(dwarf)
