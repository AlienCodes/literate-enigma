## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“The word had **hitherto** been used **loosely**,” 问题：前文从未把 planet 当作“词”提起，The word 无先行词，读者要猜。改法：“The term "planet" had **hitherto** been used **loosely**,”（+1 词）；同段抵消：“a **far-off** body, later named Eris, that at first seemed larger than Pluto” → “a **far-off** body, later named Eris, at first thought larger than Pluto”（−1 词）。P2 净 0。
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“made the **playful** **slang** verb "plutoed", to **devalue**, its word of 2006” 问题：插入语 to devalue 夹在宾语与宾补之间，读起来像不定式目的状语，句子头重。改法：“made "plutoed", **playful** **slang** for "to **devalue**", its word of 2006”（−1 词；P4 净 −1）
3. 【该改｜6｜−1】原文：“An eleven-year-old English girl **nominated** the name” 问题：nominate 的宾语是人选（nominate sb for an award），“nominate a name”不地道，应为 suggested/proposed。proposed 已被第26篇占用（实测），suggested 为基础词；无零成本替换，接受扣分。若写作组另有 ✓ 未占用词（如 put forward 不加粗、另补加粗）可再议。
4. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“**perceived** a **dim** dot” perceive 偏书面，母语者说 noticed/spotted（均为基础词），接受。
5. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“the International Astronomical Union, **convened** in Prague” 机构作 convened 的逻辑主语略别扭，母语者说 meeting in Prague；接受。
6. 【可改｜9｜−0.5】中文“一幕带有**讽刺**(irony)意味的情节早已在**途中**(transit)”：此处 irony 是“命运的巧合”，“讽刺”偏贬义。改法：“一个颇具**讽刺**(irony)意味的巧合早已在**途中**(transit)”（若语言学家认可，可改“耐人寻味的巧合”并调整速查表）。
