## 总体判断
- 节奏：P1 奢华与隐患（吸烟室、氢气）→P2 雷暴、半分钟烧光→P3 生还→P4 报道与成因→P5 信心崩塌，结构好；P2 “incinerated the ship in about half a minute, leaving a crumpled frame” 有画面。
- 结尾 “The ship's first journey to North America had begun on 6 May 1936, exactly a year before the fire.” **事实成立**（首航北美 1936 年 5 月 6 日自法兰克福出发），但放在 “a glamorous epoch of aviation closed” 之后像补充花絮，削弱了收束；且 The ship 紧跟 Graf Zeppelin，指代会先落到 Graf Zeppelin 上。

## 必改
1. 【该改｜2｜−1】结尾补遗句。改法：把该句移到 P1 末作伏笔，改成 “Its first North American voyage had begun on 6 May 1936, exactly a year before it burned.”（−2 词：the ship's→its、journey to North America→North American voyage、the fire→it burned）；P5 以 “…and a **glamorous** **epoch** of **aviation** closed.” 收尾。P1 末句仍有 Its 指 Hindenburg（段内主语一致）。加粗不变。
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“Sixty-two of the 97 aboard survived, a **proportion** of almost **two-thirds**, while a **minority** died: 35 aboard and one man on the ground.” 问题：“a minority died”生硬；而且把地面死者也算进 97 人中的“少数”，逻辑不通。改法：“Of the 97 aboard, 62 survived, a **proportion** of almost **two-thirds**; 35 died, a **minority**, as did one man on the ground.”（0 词）
3. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“Fire on camera left a **fateful** **image** that **abruptly** wrecked **confidence**.” 问题：Fire on camera 像标题腔，主语生硬。改法：“The filmed fire left a **fateful** **image** that **abruptly** wrecked **confidence**.”（0 词）
4. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：“As **perception** soured,” 什么的观感？与上句 confidence 同义叠加。改法：“As public **perception** soured,”（+1 词）

## 可改
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“was **drenched** by a **deluge** when a water tank **burst** above him, and the **soaking** saved him” 三词同义叠用；P3 加粗恰 10，接受。若想减轻：“A water tank **burst** above a 14-year-old cabin boy, and the **deluge** that **drenched** him proved a life-saving **soaking**.”（0 词左右，仍保三词，但因果更顺）——可选。
6. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“**scrambled** clear, **narrowly** escaping” 意思重复。改法：“**scrambled** clear, escaping only **narrowly**”（+1 词）——若词数紧，接受。
7. 【可改｜2｜−0.5】P1 “…for the gas was **combustible**. An American **embargo** on **helium** **export** had left the ship relying on hydrogen.” the gas 先于 hydrogen 出现，指代后置。改法：两句对调（0 词）。
8. 【可改｜4｜−0.5】Lakehurst 未交代（美国新泽西州的飞艇基地），依赖译文。
9. 【可改｜4｜−0.5】“**doped** **fabric**”（涂了涂料的蒙皮）考生难懂，依赖速查表。
