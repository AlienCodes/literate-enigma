## 总体判断
- 结构：发明→被雪藏→胶卷衰落→破产→重组，P2 “A patent followed in 1978. Production never did.” 短句收得漂亮。
- 结尾 “The year of that medal also saw Kodak announce the end of Kodachrome.” 不含 company/invented/killer；**事实成立**（2009 年 6 月宣布停产 Kodachrome，同年 11 月 Sasson 获国家技术与创新奖章），以“发明者受勋之年，正是胶卷谢幕之年”收束，反讽成立、落得住。“The year … saw” 结构略生硬（−0.5，见第 6 条）。

## 必改
1. 【该改｜2｜−1】P2 “Technical **staffers** liked it, but it needed no film.” but 的转折要读者自己补出“柯达靠卖胶卷吃饭”，而这层意思要到第四句才出现，逻辑断口。改法（句序对调，不加事实）：“Kodak's **revenues** showed its **dependence** on film, a **profitable** **cash-cow** and the **pillar** of its **franchise**. Technical **staffers** liked the camera, but it needed no film. By Sasson's **recollection**, his **superiors** were **unimpressed**, calling it cute and urging silence. A patent followed in 1978. Production never did.”（it→the camera，+1 词；P2 段内重排）

## 可改（接受扣分或零成本改）
2. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】P3 “citing **lessened** demand” 母语者说 falling／reduced demand；加粗占名额，接受。
3. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P1 “a **cumbersome** **heavyweight** of about eight pounds” 8 磅称 heavyweight 夸张，且与 cumbersome 叠用；接受。
4. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P3 “a 1980s **apex** of about 145,000 staff” 人数峰值惯说 peak；接受。
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P4 “years of digital **slowness**” 含糊（转型迟缓）；接受。
6. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】P5 “**decorated** Sasson with national **laurels**, **commending** the digital camera” laurels 泛指、commending the camera 略怪；结尾 “The year of that medal also saw…” 偏生硬。可改：“That medal's year also saw Kodak announce the end of Kodachrome.”（−1 词，仍不以时间状语开头）——可选。
