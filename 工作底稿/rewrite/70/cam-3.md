In the late summer of 1977, NASA **despatched** two Voyager probes sixteen days apart. **Affixed** to each was a **gilded** copper **disc** thirty **centimetres** across, and the two were **duplicates**.

A committee chaired by Carl Sagan of Cornell had **curated** this **eclectic** **compendium** **on behalf of** NASA. Spoken greetings in 55 languages sit beside the **rumble** of **thunder**, wind, birds and whales. Human sounds follow, **namely** **footsteps**, **laughter** and the **rhythm** of a **heartbeat**. About ninety minutes of music reflect the **diversity** of cultures and eras, **notably** the **composer** Bach and Chuck Berry's Johnny B. Goode. An **array** of 115 images is **encoded** in analogue form, and two **statesmen**, President Jimmy Carter and UN Secretary-General Kurt Waldheim, supplied printed messages. The **sleeve** shows any **finder** how to play the disc, with a **diagram** locating the Sun by pulsars, used as **navigational** **beacons**.

Sagan had argued for years that the probe should **glance** **backward**, and his **persistence** prevailed. On 14 February 1990, Voyager 1 **accordingly** turned its camera across the **void** from some six billion kilometres away. Its sixty frames formed a **mosaic**, a family portrait of the solar system. Seen from **afar**, Earth was a **negligible** **glimmer** about twelve hundredths of one pixel wide.

The frames reached home **successively** between March and 1 May over several Deep Space Network passes, each radio signal crossing the **gulf** with a **latency** of about five and a half hours. Sagan **meditated** on the image in his 1994 book Pale Blue Dot, reading in it humanity's tiny, **ephemeral** place in the **vastness** of the cosmos.

Voyager 1 entered **interstellar** space in August 2012, the first human-made object to do so, and Voyager 2 followed in 2018. Both work on, though **depletion** of their **finite** **electricity** is expected within a few years.

Two copper discs carry those **audible** greetings **outward** into a **realm** that no human-made object had entered before 2012.
