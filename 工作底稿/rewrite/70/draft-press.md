# EN: The Record Humanity Threw into the Stars
# ZH: 写给外星人的一张唱片

## 英文
Two Voyager **spaceships** set **forth** **separately** in late summer 1977. **Fastened** to each was a **gilded** copper **disc** 30 **centimetres** in **breadth**, a **compendium** of Earth.

Curated for NASA by a committee under Carl Sagan of Cornell, the **array** is **eclectic**. **Vocal** greetings in 55 languages, **rumbling** **thunder**, a **whistling** **gust** and the **moans** of whales share its **grooves** with **footsteps**, **laughter** and the **rhythm** of a **heartbeat**. About 90 minutes of **multicultural** music runs from **baroque** Bach to Chuck Berry's **rock-and-roll**.

There are 115 **encoded** images, and printed **sentiments** from Jimmy Carter and the UN's Kurt Waldheim. The cover is **imprinted** with playing instructions and a **diagram** fixing the Sun's position, with pulsars as **galactic** **beacons**.

On 14 February 1990, about six billion kilometres out, Voyager 1 **pivoted** its **onboard** camera for a **backward** **glance**, after Sagan's long **advocacy**. It made a family portrait of the solar system, a 60-frame **mosaic**. From that **remoteness**, Earth was a **pale**, **bluish** **glimmer** less than a pixel wide.

Each radio signal crossed the **gulf** in about five and a half hours, and the frames reached Deep Space Network **antennas** between March and 1 May.

In his 1994 book Pale Blue Dot, Sagan read the image as a **perspective** on our small, **ephemeral** place amid cosmic **vastness**.

Voyager 1 **glided** into **interstellar** space in August 2012, the first human-made object to do so, and Voyager 2 in 2018. Both work on as NASA's **longest-running** mission, though power from their **generators** is **finite**, with **depletion** expected within a few years.

Each disc carries a map to the Sun aboard a craft **cruising** **outwards** from it.

## 中文

1977年夏末，两艘旅行者号**宇宙飞船**(spaceships)**分头**(separately)踏上**征途**(forth)。每艘飞船上都**固定**(Fastened)着一张**镀金**(gilded)铜**唱片**(disc)，**宽度**(breadth)为30**厘米**(centimetres)，堪称地球的一部**大全**(compendium)。

这批内容由康奈尔大学卡尔·萨根主持的委员会为美国国家航空航天局挑选，整个**阵容**(array)可谓**兼收并蓄**(eclectic)。55种语言的**口头**(Vocal)问候、**隆隆**(rumbling)的**雷鸣**(thunder)、一阵**呼啸**(whistling)的**疾风**(gust)、鲸的**低吟**(moans)，与**脚步声**(footsteps)、**笑声**(laughter)以及**心跳**(heartbeat)的**节律**(rhythm)，同处它的**纹槽**(grooves)之中。约90分钟的**多元文化**(multicultural)音乐，从**巴洛克**(baroque)时期的巴赫一直延伸到查克·贝里的**摇滚乐**(rock-and-roll)。

盘上还有115幅**编码**(encoded)图像，以及吉米·卡特和联合国的库尔特·瓦尔德海姆印成文字的**致意**(sentiments)。封面上**印制**(imprinted)着播放说明，还有一幅确定太阳位置的**示意图**(diagram)，以脉冲星作为**银河系**(galactic)中的**信标**(beacons)。

1990年2月14日，在约60亿公里之外，旅行者1号把**机载**(onboard)相机**转过**(pivoted)来，**向后**(backward)投去**一瞥**(glance)，这是萨根长期**力争**(advocacy)的结果。它拍下了太阳系的全家福，一幅由60帧组成的**拼接图**(mosaic)。从那样**遥远**(remoteness)的地方望去，地球只是一点**暗淡**(pale)而**泛蓝**(bluish)的**微光**(glimmer)，不到一个像素宽。

每个无线电信号穿越这段**鸿沟**(gulf)要花约五个半小时，这些画面在3月至5月1日之间抵达深空网的**天线**(antennas)。

萨根在1994年的著作《暗淡蓝点》中，把这张照片解读为一种**视角**(perspective)，借以审视我们在**浩瀚**(vastness)宇宙中渺小而**短暂**(ephemeral)的位置。

旅行者1号于2012年8月**滑入**(glided)了**星际**(interstellar)空间，成为第一个做到这一点的人造物体，旅行者2号则在2018年进入。两者都在继续工作，是美国国家航空航天局**运行最久**(longest-running)的任务，不过它们**发电机**(generators)的电力是**有限**(finite)的，预计几年内就会**耗尽**(depletion)。

每张唱片都带着一幅通往太阳的地图，乘着一艘一路**巡航**(cruising)的飞船，**向外**(outwards)远离太阳。
