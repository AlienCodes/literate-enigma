## 必改
1. 【项3 −1｜开头】"sits in solitude with one treat and a blunt bargain" —— with 同时管 treat 和 bargain，形成拙劣轭式（一个人不会 "sit with a bargain"）。改：
   "sits in solitude before one treat, facing a blunt bargain"（±0 词）。
   中文：「……**独自**(solitude)坐在一份零食前，面对一桩**直截了当的**(blunt)**交易**(bargain)」。
   开头首句 "Picture a nursery school on Stanford's campus…" 自然，保留。
2. 【项1 −1｜易误解】"children who had watched an adult betray a promise" —— 原研究里孩子是**亲身**被实验者失信，并非旁观；另外 betray a promise 搭配罕见（常说 break a promise / betray sb's trust）。改：
   "children whose trust an adult had just betrayed waited markedly less than peers who had seen a promise kept"（+1 词）。
   中文：「刚被成人**辜负**(betray)信任的孩子，比看到成人守诺的**同龄人**(peers)等待的时间**明显**(markedly)更短。」速查表 betray 例改 (betray sb's trust/a friend)。
3. 【项3 −1】"More than 900 diverse youngsters" —— diverse 修饰群体，不修饰个人（900 个"多样的孩子"是常见误用）。改 "More than 900 youngsters from diverse backgrounds"（+2）。中文不变。
4. 【项3 −0.5｜同类比较】"The raw link was roughly half as strong as the original." —— link 比 the original（指研究？），不对等。改 "…as the original finding."（+1）。
5. 【项3 −0.5】"shrank it by about two thirds, leaving it mostly statistically insignificant" —— 一个相关"大多不显著"说不通，两个 it 也略绕。改 "leaving most estimates statistically insignificant"（±0）。中文：「……多数估计值在**统计上**(statistically)**不显著**(insignificant)。」
