# 第49篇 来源摘要（检索摘要，不得直接引用原文）
- S1 Wikipedia "Lascaux" / French Ministry of Culture (archeologie.culture.gouv.fr): Marcel Ravidat, 18, walking his dog Robot near Montignac, found a hole left by an uprooted tree (dog investigated the hole); on 12 September 1940 he returned with three other boys (Jacques Marsal, Georges Agnel, Simon Coencas) and they entered the cave. Ravidat later embellished the story in retellings, saying the dog had fallen into the cave.
- S2 Art: about 680 painted figures and about 1,500 engravings (Ministry of Culture); age usually estimated at 17,000–22,000 years; animals incl. horses, bulls/aurochs, stags.
- S3 Opened to the public 14 July 1948; carbon dioxide, heat, humidity and other contaminants produced by about 1,200 visitors a day visibly damaged the paintings; closed to the public in 1963; paintings restored, daily monitoring introduced.
- S4 UNESCO World Heritage List 1979 (Vézère Valley sites).
- S5 Lascaux II, a highly accurate partial replica (Hall of the Bulls, Axial Gallery) near the original, opened 1983. Lascaux IV opened in December 2016 (10 Dec official opening) at the International Centre for Cave Art, Montignac.
- S6 2001: outbreak of white mould (Fusarium solani) on cave walls; after biocide treatment, black stains appeared and spread, by 2007 a major threat.
