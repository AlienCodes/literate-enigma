# EN: The Fungus That Turns Ants into Zombies
# ZH: 让蚂蚁变僵尸的真菌

## 英文

In a Thai rainforest, a carpenter ant quits its **colony**'s **procession** through the **canopy** and heads **downward**. It starts to **wander** **randomly** over **leafy** **undergrowth**, **swaying** as if **intoxicated**. Around **midday** it **clenches** its **jaws** **tightly** on a main **vein** **underneath** a leaf, about 25 **centimetres** above the ground.

By **dusk** the ant is dead. Its jaw muscles have been **thoroughly** **shredded**, and death does not **unlock** the grip on the **lifeless** body. Two or three days later a stalk pushes from its head and **sprinkles** **infectious** spores onto the ground below, where **unsuspecting** ants searching for food can pick them up.

The **fungal** **invader**, Ophiocordyceps unilateralis, is **picky** about **location**. Most bites land on the **north-west** side of plants, where the air is **humid** and the temperature suits the fungus. When researchers moved infected ants to **loftier** spots, the fungus grew **abnormally**. On the ground, the bodies **disappeared** **altogether**.

A 2017 PNAS study peered inside with **three-dimensional** **microscope** imaging. Fungal cells had formed a linked **cellular** **lattice**, **entangled** with the **muscle** fibres of the legs and jaw. The picture **vividly** **evokes** a **puppet** on strings. Yet the fungus did not **infiltrate** the brain.

The **habit** has deep **geological** roots. A 48-million-year-old **fossil** leaf from Messel, in Germany, bears the same bite **scars**.

The fungus helped **inspire** The Last of Us, a 2013 video game. Its **makers** at Naughty Dog drew on a Planet Earth episode, with David Attenborough as **narrator**. Neil Druckmann **envisioned** a **scenario** in which it jumped to humans.

Marks **imprinted** on a leaf at Messel show the bite is far older than the camera that filmed it.

## 中文

在泰国的雨林里，一只木蚁脱离了**蚁群**(colony)穿行**树冠**(canopy)的**队列**(procession)，**向下**(downward)走去。它开始**漫无目的地**(randomly)在**枝叶繁茂**(leafy)的**林下灌丛**(undergrowth)上**游荡**(wander)，身子**摇摇晃晃**(swaying)，仿佛**醉了**(intoxicated)。**正午**(midday)前后，它**死死地**(tightly)用**颚**(jaws)去**咬住**(clenches)一片叶子**底下**(underneath)的主**叶脉**(vein)，离地约25**厘米**(centimetres)。

到了**黄昏**(dusk)，这只蚂蚁已经死去。它的下颚肌肉已被**彻底**(thoroughly)地**撕毁**(shredded)，死亡也**松不开**(unlock)咬在**毫无生气**(lifeless)的身体上的那一口。两三天后，一根菌柄从它头部钻出，把**具传染性**(infectious)的孢子**撒落**(sprinkles)到下方地面，**毫无防备**(unsuspecting)的觅食蚂蚁可能把它们带走。

这个属于**真菌**(fungal)的**入侵者**(invader)名叫偏侧蛇虫草菌，对**位置**(location)十分**挑剔**(picky)。大多数咬痕落在植物的**西北**(north-west)侧，那里空气**潮湿**(humid)，温度也适合这种真菌。研究人员把受感染的蚂蚁挪到**更高**(loftier)的地方，真菌就**长得畸形**(abnormally)；挪到地面，尸体则**完全**(altogether)地**不见了**(disappeared)。

2017年发表于《美国国家科学院院刊》的一项研究，用**三维**(three-dimensional)的**显微镜**(microscope)成像窥探内部。真菌细胞形成了相互连接的**细胞**(cellular)构成的**网格**(lattice)，与腿部和下颚的**肌肉**(muscle)纤维**缠绕**(entangled)在一起。这幅图景**生动地**(vividly)会**让人想起**(evokes)提线**木偶**(puppet)。然而，这种真菌并没有**侵入**(infiltrate)大脑。

这种**习性**(habit)有着深远的**地质**(geological)渊源。德国梅塞尔出土的一片4800万年前的叶片**化石**(fossil)上，留着同样的咬**痕**(scars)。

这种真菌帮助**启发**(inspire)了2013年的电子游戏《最后生还者》。顽皮狗工作室的**创作者**(makers)借鉴了《地球脉动》的一集，由大卫·阿滕伯勒担任**解说**(narrator)。尼尔·德鲁克曼**设想**(envisioned)了它跳到人类身上的**情景**(scenario)。

梅塞尔叶片上**留下**(imprinted)的印记表明，这一口咬，比拍下它的摄像机古老得多。

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】偏侧蛇虫草菌感染泰国雨林的木蚁（Camponotus leonardi）；受感染蚂蚁离开树冠上的路径（英文 procession 指蚁群行进的队列），在低矮植被上踉跄乱走（"drunken walk"，英文写 swaying as if intoxicated，randomly 对应 erratically）。S1 National Geographic / BMC Ecology（Hughes et al., 2011）
- 【T2】它在正午前后咬住叶片背面的主叶脉，离地约25厘米，多在植物西北侧，湿度温度适合真菌；把蚂蚁移高，真菌畸形生长；移到地面，尸体消失。英文 picky about location 为对选址精确的概括。S1 S2
- 【T3】蚂蚁约在日落时死亡；下颚肌肉被破坏，死后仍咬住不放。英文 thoroughly shredded 为对 destroyed 的生动表述。S2 ESA blog / LiveScience / NBC（2011）
- 【T4】死后两三天，子实体（菌柄）从头部长出，把孢子撒向下方地面，觅食的蚂蚁可能带上。infectious、unsuspecting 为说明性措辞。S3 EarthSky / Penn State（2011）
- 【T5】2017年 PNAS 三维显微成像：真菌细胞在腿部和下颚肌肉纤维周围形成相互连接的网络，却从未进入大脑。提线木偶为作者比喻，来源未用。S4 Penn State / PNAS（Fredericksen, Hughes et al., 2017）
