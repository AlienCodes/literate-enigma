# EN: Apollo 13: The Successful Failure
# ZH: 阿波罗13号：成功的失败
## 英文

On April 11th 1970 NASA launched Apollo 13, bound for the third crewed **lunar** landing. On April 13th, some 200,000 miles out, an **oxygen** tank **exploded** in the service **module**, home to most of the ship's oxygen and power. The **blast** **crippled** the **spacecraft**; the landing was **aborted**.

The **astronauts** took **refuge** in the lunar module, Aquarius, built for two men and about two days. Using its engine, the crew **steered** the **craft** onto a **trajectory** around the Moon, whose gravity swung it home. They **rationed** water, **conserved** power and **shivered** in a **bitterly** cold **cabin**.

A quieter **hazard** **emerged**: with a third man aboard, **carbon dioxide** was **accumulating**. The command module, the crew's **capsule**, used square **filter** **cartridges**; Aquarius's **sockets** were round. From plastic bags, **cardboard** and tape already on board, ground engineers **devised** an **adapter**, then talked the crew through **assembling** it. On April 17th the capsule **splashed** down in the Pacific.

Yet the **culprit** was made on Earth. A NASA review board **traced** the **explosion** to the tank's **thermostat** switches, designed for the ship's 28-**volt** supply. Back in 1965, the spacecraft's builder rewrote the **specification** to let the **heaters** run on 65-volt launch-site power; the switches were never **upgraded**, a **mismatch** neither that **contractor** nor NASA **detected**. Weeks before launch, to **drain** the stubborn tank, a heater ran about eight hours. The switches probably **malfunctioned**; unchecked heat **scorched** the **insulation** on its **wiring**, which **ignited** in flight.

NASA would call Apollo 13 a "successful failure". The **paradox** holds: a flight undone by a five-year-old engineering **oversight** was **salvaged** within four days by **ingenuity** on the ground.

## 中文

1970年4月11日，NASA发射了阿波罗13号，此行本应完成人类第三次载人**登月**(lunar)。4月13日，在距地球约20万英里处，服务**舱段**(module)里的一个**氧气**(oxygen)罐**爆炸**(exploded)了，而飞船的氧气和电力大多储存在这个舱段里。这次**爆炸**(blast)**重创**(crippled)了**飞船**(spacecraft)，登月只得**中止**(aborted)。

**宇航员**(astronauts)们转到登月舱“水瓶座”号里**避难**(refuge)，这艘登月舱原本只够两个人用两天左右。机组借助它的发动机，**操纵**(steered)**航天器**(craft)驶入一条绕月**轨迹**(trajectory)，再借月球引力把它甩回地球。他们**限量**(rationed)用水，**节约**(conserved)用电，在**刺骨**(bitterly)寒冷的**舱室**(cabin)里**瑟瑟发抖**(shivered)。

一个不那么显眼的**隐患**(hazard)随之**浮现**(emerged)：舱里多了一个人，**二氧化碳**(carbon dioxide)正不断**积聚**(accumulating)。机组乘坐的**太空舱**(capsule)，也就是指令舱，配的是方形**过滤**(filter)**筒**(cartridges)，而“水瓶座”号的**插口**(sockets)却是圆的。地面工程师用船上现有的塑料袋、**硬纸板**(cardboard)和胶带，**设法拼出**(devised)一个**转接器**(adapter)，再一步步指导机组把它**组装**(assembling)起来。4月17日，太空舱在太平洋**溅落**(splashed)。

然而，**罪魁祸首**(culprit)是在地面上造成的。NASA的一个调查委员会把这次**爆炸**(explosion)**追溯**(traced)到罐里的**恒温**(thermostat)开关上，这些开关是按飞船的28**伏**(volt)电源设计的。早在1965年，飞船承建方就改写了**规格**(specification)，允许**加热器**(heaters)使用发射场65伏的电源，开关却始终没有**升级**(upgraded)；这处**不匹配**(mismatch)，无论是那家**承包商**(contractor)还是NASA都没有**发现**(detected)。发射前几周，那个罐里的氧气怎么也排不干净，为了把它**排空**(drain)，一台加热器连续工作了约八个小时。开关很可能因此**失灵**(malfunctioned)，失控的高温**烧焦**(scorched)了罐内**线路**(wiring)的**绝缘层**(insulation)，飞行途中，绝缘层**起火**(ignited)了。

后来，NASA把阿波罗13号称作“成功的失败”。这个**悖论**(paradox)站得住：一次飞行，毁于五年前的一处工程**疏忽**(oversight)，却在四天之内靠地面上的**巧思**(ingenuity)得以**挽救**(salvaged)。
