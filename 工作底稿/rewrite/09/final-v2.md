# EN: Apollo 13: The Successful Failure
# ZH: 阿波罗13号：成功的失败
## 英文

On April 11th 1970, NASA, America's space agency, launched Apollo 13, meant to make the third crewed **lunar** landing. On April 13th, some 200,000 miles out, an **oxygen** tank **exploded** in the service **module**, the ship's store of oxygen and power. The **blast** **crippled** the **spacecraft**; the landing was **aborted**.

The three **astronauts** took **refuge** in the lunar module, Aquarius, built to support two men for about two days. Its engine **steered** the **craft** onto a **trajectory** around the Moon, whose gravity swung it home. The crew **rationed** water, **conserved** power and **shivered** in a **bitterly** cold **cabin**.

Then a quieter **hazard** **emerged**: **carbon dioxide** was **accumulating**. The command module, the crew's **capsule**, had square **filter** **cartridges**; Aquarius's **sockets** were round. Engineers on the ground **devised** an **adapter** from plastic bags, **cardboard** and tape on board, and talked the crew through **assembling** it. On April 17th the capsule **splashed** down in the Pacific.

Yet the **culprit** lay on the ground. In June 1970 a NASA review board **traced** the **explosion** to the tank's 28-**volt** **thermostat** switches. In 1965 a new **specification** let the tank's **heaters** run on 65-volt ground power, but nobody **upgraded** the switches; neither the **contractor** nor NASA **detected** the **mismatch**. Weeks before launch, a heater ran for about eight hours to **drain** the tank. The switches probably **malfunctioned**, **scorching** the **insulation** on the tank's **wiring**; in flight, that insulation **ignited**.

NASA came to call Apollo 13 a "successful failure". The **paradox** fits a flight lost to a **paperwork** **oversight** five years before launch and **salvaged** within four days by **ingenuity** on the ground.

## 中文

1970年4月11日，美国的航天机构——国家航空航天局（NASA）发射了阿波罗13号，按计划，这将是人类第三次载人**登月**(lunar)。4月13日，在距地球约20万英里处，储存飞船氧气和电力的服务**舱段**(module)里，一个**氧气**(oxygen)罐**炸裂**(exploded)了。爆炸的**冲击**(blast)**重创**(crippled)了**飞船**(spacecraft)；登月计划只得**中止**(aborted)。

三名**宇航员**(astronauts)转到登月舱“水瓶座”号里**避难**(refuge)，而这艘登月舱原本只供两人使用两天左右。登月舱的发动机**操纵**(steered)着**航天器**(craft)驶入一条绕月**轨迹**(trajectory)，再借月球引力把它甩回地球。机组人员**限量**(rationed)用水，**节约**(conserved)用电，在**刺骨**(bitterly)寒冷的**舱室**(cabin)里**瑟瑟发抖**(shivered)。

接着，一个不那么显眼的**隐患**(hazard)**浮现**(emerged)出来：**二氧化碳**(carbon dioxide)正在不断**积聚**(accumulating)。指令舱是机组乘坐的**太空舱**(capsule)，配的是方形的**过滤**(filter)**筒**(cartridges)；“水瓶座”号上的**插口**(sockets)却是圆的。地面工程师用船上现有的塑料袋、**硬纸板**(cardboard)和胶带**设计**(devised)出一个**转接器**(adapter)，再一步步指导机组把它**组装**(assembling)起来。4月17日，太空舱在太平洋**溅落**(splashed)。

然而，**罪魁祸首**(culprit)其实在地面上。1970年6月，NASA的一个调查委员会把这次**爆炸**(explosion)的原因**追溯**(traced)到氧气罐里按28**伏**(volt)设计的**恒温**(thermostat)开关上。1965年，一项新**规格**(specification)允许罐内的**加热器**(heaters)使用65伏的地面电源，却没有人相应**升级**(upgraded)这些开关；无论是**承包商**(contractor)还是NASA，都没有**发现**(detected)这处**不匹配**(mismatch)。发射前几周，为了**排空**(drain)氧气罐，一台加热器连续工作了约八个小时。开关很可能因此**失灵**(malfunctioned)，**烧焦**(scorching)了罐内**线路**(wiring)的**绝缘层**(insulation)；到了飞行途中，这层绝缘材料**起火**(ignited)了。

后来，NASA把阿波罗13号称作“成功的失败”。一次飞行，毁于发射五年前的一处**文书**(paperwork)工作上的**疏忽**(oversight)，又在四天之内靠地面上的**巧思**(ingenuity)得以**挽救**(salvaged)，用这个**悖论**(paradox)来形容再贴切不过。
