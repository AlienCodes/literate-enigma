## 总体判断
- 节奏：神马表演→委员会背书→Pfungst 实验→揭示提示→效应与余波，经典“反转揭秘”结构，P3 的 50/56 对 2/35 是全文高潮。
- 结尾现为 “In the laboratory, Pfungst took the animal's place and proved equally **sensitive** to the cues of **attentive** volunteers.” **事实成立**（Pfungst 在实验室扮演马，发现受试者同样不自觉地给出提示），但它排在 1909 年后的珠宝商一句之后，时间倒流；而珠宝商一句本身其实是更有余味的结尾（揭穿之后仍有人深信马能推理）。

## 必改
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】P5 首句头重、介词悬尾：“The Clever Hans effect, in which **expectancy** shapes **behavioural** results, is a **phenomenon** of **bias** that **double-blind** designs in **psychology** and animal **cognition** guard against.” 改法：“**Double-blind** designs in **psychology** and animal **cognition** now guard against the Clever Hans effect, a **phenomenon** of **bias** in which **expectancy** shapes **behavioural** results.”（−1 词）
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】P3：“his **accuracy** fell to a **ratio** of two in 35.” 问题：fell to a ratio of 生硬。改法：“With the questioner **invisible**, his **accuracy** fell to two in 35, a far poorer **ratio**.”（that person→the questioner，+1 词）
3. 【该改｜2｜−0.5】P5 后两句对调，以珠宝商收尾：“…**behavioural** results. In the laboratory, Pfungst took the animal's place and proved equally **sensitive** to the cues of **attentive** volunteers. Hans later passed to a **jeweller** still convinced horses could reason.”（+1 词 still）——时间顺序恢复，且结尾以“揭穿后仍有人相信”收束，反讽有力，不新增事实（珠宝商相信马能推理已在原文）。
