## 3 语言质量（逐句，−5.5）
1. 该改 −1｜"In ten days from 31 August 1854" 缺冠词，母语者读着卡。→ "In the ten days from 31 August 1854"。
2. 该改 −1｜"a physician who long maintained that cholera was caught by swallowing, not inhaling" —— 时态应为过去完成（他从1849年就这么主张）；swallowing/inhaling 缺宾语，读者会问"吞什么"。→ "a physician who had long maintained that cholera was caught by swallowing something, not by inhaling it"。
3. 该改 −0.5｜"its water tested almost clean" 口语化、略含混（"tested clean"多用于药检）。→ "its water showed little impurity when he examined it on 3 September"。
4. 该改 −1｜"interpreted the cluster of deaths ... as poison drifting through the air" —— 聚集本身不"是"毒气，是毒气的证据/结果。→ "read the cluster ... as the work of poison drifting through the air"。
5. 该改 −1｜"She perished; so did a visiting niece who drank it and returned to Islington." 语序让人以为先死后回家。→ "She perished, as did a visiting niece who drank it, went home to Islington and died there."
6. 该改 −1｜"contending nobody had demonstrated its drinkers suffered disproportionately" 连省两个 that，名词串过重。→ "contending that no one had shown that those who drank from it suffered disproportionately"。
7. 可改（并入第2项扣）｜"A map could chart where people died." 省略了"却查不出喝了什么"的对照，考研生未必补得出。→ "A map could chart where people died, but not what they drank."

## 4 适配读者（−1.5）
- workhouse、parish、curate 三个19世纪英国专名无任何交代（−1）；可在句中顺带：a workhouse "for the destitute"、"a young local curate"。
- "Soho / Hampstead / Islington" 相对远近未交代，寡妇案例的力度依赖于"远"（−0.5）：→ "in distant Hampstead"。

## 6 用法典型（−2）
- "had not ventured near Broad Street" —— venture 暗含冒险/胆怯，这里寡妇只是搬走了，用法偏离最该学的义项。→ "had not been near Broad Street for months"（或把 ventured 用在真有风险的语境）。−1
- "Snow's renowned map" —— 1855年这张图并不 renowned（12月才首次展示），且 renowned 多修饰人/机构（renowned for...）。→ "Snow's now-famous map"，或把 renowned 换到别处。−1
- 可改：consulted ... then the living（consult 轭式修辞，俏皮但偏离"查阅"义），不扣。

## 1 史实（−3）
- "daily deaths had already plunged from a peak of 143 to 12"：斯诺表一是"致命发病数"（fatal attacks），不是当日死亡数。→ "daily fatal attacks"，或 "new fatal cases"。−2
- "to vindicate Snow"：怀特黑德说服了自己和地方委员会，但同年政府调查仍不采信；"vindicate"说得过满。→ "to confirm Snow's case"。−1（与2项结尾问题相关，不重复扣）

## 2 论证与结构（−2）
- 结尾：前句刚说政府调查驳回，下一句说怀特黑德"证明斯诺正确"，但未交代他的结论是否被采纳，读者会以为官方翻案，与史实矛盾。建议补半句："...his door-to-door survey, not the map, is what finally made the case."（−2）

## 8 忠实（−2）
- "tested almost clean" 译"水看上去几乎是干净的"——"看上去"弱化了"化验"结果。→ "化验结果显示水几乎是干净的"。−0.5
- "contending" 译"认为"语气偏弱。→ "坚称"。−0.5
- "vindicate Snow" 译"斯诺才得以证明清白"——斯诺未被指控，"清白"不对。→ "斯诺的观点才得到证实"。−1

## 9 通顺（−1）
- "在一片死亡之中" 欧化。→ "周围的人成片死去，而……"。−1

## 10 对应（−0.5）
- demonstrated 译"证明"、vindicate 译"证明清白"，两词中文重叠。改后者为"证实"。
