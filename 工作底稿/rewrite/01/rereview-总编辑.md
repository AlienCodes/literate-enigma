## 修订引入的新问题
- **该改｜−1（项1）"Hampstead had no cholera."** Snow p.44–45（src 第1737–1741行）原文："There was no cholera at the time, either at West End or in the neighbourhood where the niece died." 有两处不准：① 原文说的是 West End（汉普斯特德的一个村落），不是整个 Hampstead；② 删掉了 "at the time"，英文变成了笼统的断言。中文「当时汉普斯特德并没有霍乱」有「当时」，比英文准。最省词的改法：**"Hampstead then had no cholera."**（+1 词，共271词）；如果词数允许，**"Her district then had no cholera."** 更好。中文可以保持不变。
- 顺带核对：寡妇 "had not set foot in Soho for months"（原文 "not been in the neighbourhood of Broad Street for many months"）可以接受；"had the pump's water carted to her daily" 与原文 "A cart went… every day" 一致；niece "visiting… drank it"、"died" 均无误；删去伊斯灵顿没有造成错误。Meanwhile 的时间关系成立。
- "Finding little visible impurity" 没有写 "in the water"，但紧接在 pump 之后，指代清楚，不扣分。
