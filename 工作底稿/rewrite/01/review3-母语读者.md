## 逐条问题

### 3 语言质量（逐句）
1. 【该改 −1】"nobody had demonstrated its drinkers suffered disproportionately" —— demonstrated 后省 that，读者先读成 "demonstrated its drinkers"（展示喝水者），造成歧义；"its drinkers" 本身也别扭。改：**"contending that nobody had demonstrated that those who drank from it suffered disproportionately."**
2. 【该改 −1】"a distant Hampstead widow" —— distant 修饰人时首先读作“冷淡的”或“远亲”；应修饰地点。改：**"a widow in distant Hampstead"** 或 "a widow miles away in Hampstead"。
3. 【该改 −1】"Crucial cases isolated water from air." —— 抽象且字面上像把两种物质分离，编辑一定会问。改：**"The crucial cases separated the effects of water from those of air."** 或 "Crucial cases isolated water as the cause."（见第 6 项）
4. 【可改 −0.5】"the work of poison drifting through the air" —— 不加冠词的 poison 读起来缺一拍。改：**"the work of a poison drifting through the air"**。
5. 【可改 −0.5】"any outbreak would probably have one nearby" —— “疫情拥有一口泵”搭配松。改：**"that almost any outbreak would be close to one."**
6. 【可改 −0.5】"Over 70 nearby brewery labourers" —— 三重前置修饰堆叠，报刊体。改：**"More than 70 labourers at a nearby brewery, who had a beer allowance and a well of their own, …"**
7. 【可改 −0.5】"She perished, as did a visiting niece who drank it and returned to Islington." —— 时间顺序含糊（死于何处？）。改：**"She perished, as did a niece who drank it on a visit and died after returning home to Islington."**

### 4 适配读者
1. 【该改 −0.5】"daily fatal attacks" —— Snow 的术语，现代读者会把 attacks 读成“袭击”。改："the number of new fatal cases each day had already plunged …"
2. 【可改 −0.5】Soho、Hampstead、Islington、curate、workhouse 未作任何交代；至少加 "London's Soho"、"the village of Hampstead" 之类一两个词。

### 2 论证与结构
1. 【该改 −1】第 3 段 "the cluster of deaths Snow mapped" —— 地图此前从未出现，却成为结尾论点（"A map could chart…"）的支点。第 2 段应加半句：**"…most had drunk from it. He plotted each death on a map."**

### 1 史实
1. 【可改 −0.5】"At his urging, parish officials removed the handle" 与 "daily fatal attacks … to 12" 同句，易让读者以为 12 是卸柄前一天数字；来源注明 12 为 9 月 8 日当天。建议 "by that day"。

### 6 用法典型
1. 【该改 −1】isolated —— 文中“分离水与空气”并非最该学的义项（考研常见：隔离、孤立；科研中 isolate a cause/variable）。按第 3 条改为 "isolated water as the cause"，既地道又是典型义。
- condemn 用“宣布有害/不宜使用”义，属地道搭配（condemn a building），速查表已注明，不扣。

### 5 学习价值（word_tier.py 结果）
50 个词：✓考研 24、✓高中中上 13、✓雅思 4、✓短语 3、✓常用 2、✓派生 1、✓四六级 1、△高中较常用 2（confirmed 4.67、register 4.59）。本人判定：register（死亡登记册）与 confirmed（证实论断）用的都是中等以上义项，可保留。无 ✗，无同形重复。12 分。

### 7 速查表
1. 【可改 −0.5】refute 释义含“否认”——这是有争议的用法，考生会误用；删去或注“（此义有争议，慎用）”。

### 9 中文通顺
1. 【可改 −0.5】“关键病例把水和空气这两个因素分离开来”偏欧化；英文改后同步改为“关键病例把水单独确认为病因”或“几个关键病例把水和空气区分开来”。
