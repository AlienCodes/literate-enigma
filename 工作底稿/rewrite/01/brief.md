## 0 一页摘要

- **题材**：保留。1854 年伦敦苏豪区宽街（Broad Street）霍乱与约翰·斯诺（John Snow）。
- **论点（一句话）**：一张地图只能告诉你人死在**哪里**，可坏空气和坏水挤在同一片街巷里，所以那一簇死亡两种理论都讲得通（当时就有权威评论者把它读成空气传播）。能把水和空气分开的，是人们**喝了什么**，而最能说明问题的，是住处和饮水分了家的那几类人。
- **标题**：**Where They Died, What They Drank** ／《死在哪里，喝了什么》
- **结构**：5 段，约 52／56／52／64／36 词，合计 260 词左右（硬限 249–271）。
- **三条红线**：①不写「十天 616 人」；②不写地图帮他找到了水泵；③不写卸下泵柄止住了疫情。完整清单见第 5 节。

## 1 题材与角度

### 1.1 为什么不换题材
这个故事的流行版本错得很系统（数字、地图的时间、泵柄的作用），但原始材料齐全：斯诺 1855 年的原著、他的逐日表、同时代评论、官方报告都能查到。把神话剥掉以后，剩下的东西比神话本身更有意思，也更少见于课本。换题材只会丢掉这个优势。

### 1.2 流行版本与真实经过

| 流行版本 | 真实经过（依据见第 4 节） |
|---|---|
| 斯诺画出死亡地图，看出围着水泵的规律，顺藤摸瓜找到水泵 | 他一听说疫情**就怀疑那口泵**（他从 1849 年起就认为霍乱是「吞下去」的，经水传播）。那张地图收录的死亡**截至 9 月 30 日**，1854 年 12 月 4 日才首次展示，是事后画的 |
| 地图上的规律证明了是水 | 死亡簇两种理论都能解释。1855 年，评论家埃德蒙·帕克斯（Edmund Parkes）看了同样的分布，认为它**更像空气传播**，还说这一带水泵太多，疫情不管在哪儿爆发，附近都会有一口泵 |
| 卸下泵柄，疫情戛然而止 | 泵柄 9 月 8 日才卸下。致命病例在 9 月 1 日达到峰值（143 例），到 8 日已降到 12 例。斯诺自己承认，无法判断当时井水是否还有毒 |
| 水一看就脏 | 这口井的水「几乎人人都当它十分纯净」（passed with almost everybody as being perfectly pure），可见的杂质**比附近几口与霍乱无关的泵还少**。显微镜下的颗粒也看不出「有组织的结构」 |

所以，这个故事真正说明的不是「看见」的胜利，而是**问**的胜利。水看上去是干净的，地图在批评者眼里像是空气传播。把两种解释分开的，是挨家挨户问出来的一件事：他们喝的是哪里的水。最锋利的证据落在住处和饮水分了家的人身上：泵旁边的济贫院和啤酒厂，人们呼吸同样的空气，却不喝这口泵的水，几乎没人死；几英里外汉普斯特德（Hampstead）的一位寡妇，远离苏豪的空气，专门让人送这口泵的水来喝，结果死了。

### 1.3 论点（团队统一口径）
- **中文**：地图记录的是人死在哪里，但在苏豪，坏空气和坏水占着同一片街巷，死亡簇分不出是谁的错。分出来的是「喝了什么」，在住处与饮水分道扬镳的人身上看得最清楚。
- **English (for the team, not for the page)**: *A map could show where Soho's victims died, but bad air and bad water shared the same streets, so the cluster fitted either theory; what told them apart was what people drank — seen most sharply where place and water parted company.*

### 1.4 与全库的区隔（必须守住）
全库已有几篇用过相近的框架：**17**「少数折返者推翻多数」、**16**「预期中的空白本身就是预言」、**09**「搜寻把『没有』变成证据」、**18**「逐一排除本地嫌疑」、**49**「计数赢下辩论」，**36** 写的也是哈索尔（Hassall）与显微镜。
本篇的轴线是 **「地点 vs 暴露（饮水）」**，以及「看 vs 问」。所以正文和结尾都**不要**写成「例外比多数重要」「异常推翻了旧理论」这类说法，否则会和 17、16 撞车。例外在本篇里的作用，是**把地点和饮水拆开的手段**，不是主题本身。

## 3 论证步骤与分段

### 3.1 七个论证步骤
1. **布景与触发**：何时、何地、多严重；当时的主流理论；斯诺带着现成的理论到场，立刻怀疑那口泵。
2. **看不出来**：泵里的水看上去是干净的，凭肉眼和检验定不了罪。于是他不再看水，转而去问喝水的人。
3. **规律**：拿到死亡登记名单，到现场逐户问。死亡几乎都集中在泵附近，离别的泵更近的那几例，也大多追得回这口泵。
4. **行动与诚实交代**：9 月 7 日陈情，8 日卸下泵柄，但这时疫情早已回落。
5. **规律为什么不够**：地图是事后画的。死亡簇两种理论都讲得通，帕克斯就读成了空气传播。
6. **判别性证据**：住处与饮水分了家的人。近处：泵旁的济贫院和啤酒厂，空气相同，水不同，几乎无人死亡。远处：汉普斯特德的寡妇和她的侄女，空气不同，水相同，都死了。斯诺称这两例「perhaps the most conclusive of all」。
7. **意义**：地图回答「在哪里」，这场争论要回答的是「喝了什么」。

### 3.2 推荐分段：5 段
| 段 | 步骤 | 词数目标 |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 1＋2 | 50–55 |
| P2 | 3＋4 | 54–60 |
| P3 | 5 | 48–55 |
| P4 | 6 | 60–68 |
| P5 | 7 | 30–40 |

**为什么是 5 段**：
- P4 必须把「近处幸免」和「远处中招」放在同一段，才能形成「空气相同、水不同／空气不同、水相同」的对照。拆成两段，对照就断了。
- P5 要短，收尾句才有分量；全文的评论只放在这一段（仓库惯例：评论只进最后一段，两句格言不相邻）。
- 分成 6 段，平均每段只有约 43 词，论证会显得零碎；分成 4 段，P2 或 P4 会超过 80 词，视频字幕放不下。
- **备选**：如果 P4 超过 70 词，可以拆成「P4 近处（济贫院、啤酒厂）＋ P5 远处（寡妇）＋ P6 意义」，共 6 段。拆开后，对照要靠 P5 开头一个明确的转折来接（意思是「反过来也成立」），不许靠读者自己去补。

### 3.3 逐段计划
★ 必用　☆ 强烈建议　○ 可选（词数允许才用）。编号对应第 4 节。

#### P1 布景与触发（≈52 词）
- **任务**：交代时间、地点、规模，以及当时的正统理论。斯诺是谁、他原本相信什么、他立刻怀疑什么。最后以「水看上去是干净的」作为转折。
- ★ A1：1854 年 8 月 31 日夜里爆发；★ A2：十天内、离一个街角 250 码以内，死亡五百多人
- ★ B1：主流医学把病归于「瘴气」（miasma），即腐物散发的坏空气。miasma 必须当场用 bad air 或 foul air 解释，不能让考研读者自己猜
- ★ C1：斯诺是住在附近的内科医生；★ B2：他从 1849 年起就主张霍乱是吞下去的，不是吸进去的，经被病人排泄物污染的水传播
- ★ D1：他一听说就怀疑宽街那口人来人往的水泵
- ★ D2 / ☆ D4：9 月 3 日他检查井水，有机杂质少到他不敢下结论；这口井的水几乎人人当它纯净，杂质还比附近几口无害的泵少
- ○ A3：全程共 616 人；○ D3：显微镜检查结果
- **交接**：用一个短句转到下一段，意思是「定不了水的罪，他就去查喝水的人」。

#### P2 规律与泵柄（≈56 词）
- **任务**：用登记册和实地询问得出规律，接着写他怎么行动，并如实交代行动的时机。
- ★ D6：从登记总署（General Register Office）抄来死亡名单，到现场逐户询问
- ★ D7：死亡几乎全在泵附近；离别的泵更近的十例里，五家说一向专门去宽街打水，三例是在泵旁上学的孩子
- ☆ D8：泵附近的 73 例中，已知有 61 例常喝或偶尔喝这口泵的水
- ★ E1：9 月 7 日晚，他向本教区济贫委员会（Board of Guardians）陈述；次日泵柄被卸下
- ★ E3：此时每天新增的致命病例已从 9 月 1 日的 143 例降到 12 例；斯诺承认无法判断井水当时是否仍有毒
- ○ E2：据当时的记述，委员会并不相信他，但照办了
- **交接**：泵柄是后人记住的那个动作，另一件后人记住的东西是地图。下一段写地图。

#### P3 规律为什么不够（≈52 词）
- **任务**：把地图放回正确的时间，再说明死亡簇本身分不出两种理论。这一段就是原计划里「当时的解释为何不够」的前半：先承认当时的正统理论**也能**解释这一簇，下一段再写它在哪里解释不了。
- ★ F1：那张每例死亡画一道黑杠的地图，收录的死亡截至 9 月 30 日，12 月才首次在伦敦流行病学会（Epidemiological Society of London）展示
- ★ F3：1855 年评议斯诺新书时，帕克斯认为这种向四周扩散的分布更像空气传播，还指出这一带水泵极多，疫情不管在哪儿爆发，附近总会有一口泵
- ○ F4：当地人还怪罪半年前挖开整修的排水沟，或传说埋在附近的 1665 年鼠疫死者坑
- **交接**：一句话点明，要把水和空气分开，得找住处和饮水不一致的人。

#### P4 判别性证据（≈64 词）
- **任务**：写近处和远处两组对照，以斯诺自己的判断收尾。
- ★ G1：波兰街济贫院四分之三以上被死过人的房子包围，535 名收容者只死了 5 个（按周围街道的死亡率，应当死一百多人）；院内有自己的泵井，另有自来水公司供水，从不去宽街打水
- ★ G2：泵旁的啤酒厂七十多名工人无一人重病。厂里配给麦芽酒，老板相信他们根本不喝水；厂内还有深井
- ★ G4：汉普斯特德一位 59 岁的寡妇，好几个月没到过宽街一带，因为喜欢这口泵的水，每天让车运一大瓶来。她 8 月 31 日和 9 月 1 日喝了，9 月 2 日病故
- ★ G5：来访的侄女也喝了这水，回到伊斯灵顿「a high and healthy part of Islington」的家中，同样染病身亡；两地当时都没有霍乱
- ☆ G6：斯诺认为这两例「perhaps the most conclusive of all」
- ○ B4：「high and healthy」恰是当时理论认为安全的地方，可点一个短语

#### P5 意义（≈36 词）
- **任务**：先写一句事实性或分析性的话，再落收尾句（要求见第 6 节）。不引入需要另行核实的新事实。
- ○ H2（强烈推荐，前提是词数允许）：1855 年驳回斯诺的官方调查，理由恰恰是「没有证据表明喝这口井水的居民比别人死得多」。连反对者也把分歧放在了「喝水」上，这正好替收尾句作证。只许转述，写法见 H2。
- ○ H4：宽街原址（今 Broadwick Street）的复制水泵

## 4 核准史实表

「可写」给出措辞上限，「不可写」列出常见的越界写法。★／☆／○ 同上；**禁用** = 核不到或已证伪。
来源代号见第 9 节。S1 = 斯诺 1855 年原著（第二版），页码为书页。

### A 疫情与数字
- **A1 ★ 爆发时间**
  - 可写：the night of 31 August 1854；at the end of August 1854。
  - 不可写：in September 1854（笼统说成九月）；「此前毫无病例」。
  - 依据：S1 p.38，"There were a few cases of cholera in the neighbourhood of Broad Street, Golden Square, in the latter part of August; and the so-called outbreak, which commenced in the night between the 31st August and the 1st September, was, as in all similar instances, only a violent increase of the malady." S1 p.39 又说他把爆发日定为周四（31 日）。
- **A2 ★ 十天、五百余例**
  - 可写：more than 500 people died within ten days；within 250 yards of one street corner（或 of the pump）。
  - 不可写：616 in ten days；「整个苏豪」。
  - 依据：S1 p.38，"Within two hundred and fifty yards of the spot where Cambridge Street joins Broad Street, there were upwards of five hundred fatal attacks of cholera in ten days." 我用斯诺表一核算：8 月 31 日至 9 月 9 日致命发病 522 例；9 月 1 日至 10 日死亡 530 例。数据见 S2，原始表见 S1 p.49。
- **A3 ○ 全程 616**
  - 可写：616 in all, by Snow's count；over six weeks。
  - 不可写：「十天」「九月内」。
  - 依据：S1 p.49 表一，8 月 19 日至 9 月 30 日，致命发病合计 616，其中 45 例日期不明；死亡栏合计也是 616。斯诺在 p.48 另说 "rather more than six hundred deaths"。
- **A4 ○ 峰值**
  - 可写：致命发病 9 月 1 日 143 例，为单日最高；死亡 9 月 2 日 127 例。
  - 禁用：维基百科的「三天内死 127 人」。表一 1–3 日的死亡数是 70＋127＋76。
  - 依据：S1 p.51，"The greatest number of attacks in any one day occurred on the 1st of September"。
- **A5 ○ 居民逃离**
  - 可写：within six days more than three-quarters of the people in the worst-hit streets had fled。
  - 不可写：「苏豪四分之三的人」。
  - 依据：S1 p.38，"in less than six days from the commencement of the outbreak, the most afflicted streets were deserted by more than three-quarters of their inhabitants."
- **A6 禁用**：南丁格尔、米德尔塞克斯医院等细节。与论点无关，来源也是二手。

### B 两种理论
- **B1 ★ 瘴气说**
  - 可写：most doctors blamed "miasma"（bad air rising from filth and decay）。
  - 不可写：「所有医生」；把 miasma 当作不加解释的术语丢给读者。
  - 依据：S6 维基（Competing theories of cholera），"Miasma theorists concluded that cholera was caused by particles in the air, or 'miasmata', which arose from decomposing matter"。S1 目录 p.9 有一章题为 "Cholera not communicated by means of effluvia"，是斯诺专门针对这一说法写的。
- **B2 ★ 斯诺 1849 年的理论**
  - 可写：since 1849 Snow had argued that cholera was swallowed, not breathed, and spread through water fouled by the sick。
  - 不可写：「他发现了细菌」；「germ theory 是斯诺的用语」；「他没有任何理论」。
  - 依据：S1 序言，"The first edition of this work, which was published in August 1849, was only a slender pamphlet." S1 p.15，"the morbid material producing cholera must be introduced into the alimentary canal—must, in fact, be swallowed accidentally"。另见 S11 拍卖目录："first voiced his theory that cholera is an infectious disease of the alimentary canal and is transmitted through the ingestion of fecal matter from infected patients, mainly through contaminated water"。
- **B3 ○ 斯诺预料显微镜可能看不见病原**
  - 可写：he did not expect a microscope to show the poison。
  - 依据：S1 p.15，"It is no objection to this view that the structure of the cholera poison cannot be recognised by the microscope".
- **B4 ○ 「高而健康」恰是正统理论认为安全的地方**
  - 可写：just the sort of high ground the theory of the day counted safe。只许点一个短语。
  - 依据：S10（HistData 文档，Friendly 编）："In 1852, William Farr, published a report of the Registrar-General on mortality due to cholera in England in the years 1848-1849 ... Farr initially believed that cholera arose from bad air ("miasma") associated with low elevation above the River Thames." 文档附带的示例代码把它称为 "Farr's mortality ~ 1/ elevation law"，即死亡率与海拔成反比。
- **B5 ○ 霍乱弧菌**
  - 可写：the bacterium was not identified in Britain until decades later。
  - 不可写：「斯诺看到了细菌」。
  - 依据：S6，"The cholera-causing bacterium Vibrio cholerae was isolated in 1854, but the finding became well known and accepted only decades later."（帕奇尼 1854 年在佛罗伦萨，科赫 1883–84 年。）本篇不建议写。

### C 斯诺其人
- **C1 ★ 住在附近的内科医生**
  - 可写：a physician who lived nearby / a short walk away。
  - 不可写：具体距离；Dr Snow（视频规则不许用缩写头衔）。
  - 依据：S1 扉页 "Member of the Royal College of Physicians"；序言落款 "Sackville Street, Piccadilly, 11 December, 1854"。S8：他 1852–58 年住在 18 Sackville Street。
- **C2 ○ 麻醉**
  - 可写：he had given Queen Victoria chloroform in 1853。
  - 依据：S8，"on 7 April 1853, Queen Victoria asked John Snow to administer chloroform during the delivery of her eighth child, Leopold"。本篇不建议写，占词数，又与论点无关。
- **C3 禁用** teetotaller／「滴酒不沾」
  - 依据：S8，他 1830 年加入禁酒运动，但约 1845 年起为助消化喝少量葡萄酒。
- **C4 ○ 去世**
  - 可写：died in June 1858, aged 45。
  - 依据：S8，1858 年 6 月 10 日中风，16 日去世。

### D 调查
- **D1 ★ 立刻怀疑那口泵**
  - 可写：he suspected the pump at once。
  - 不可写：「地图让他怀疑泵」；「他逐一排查后才想到泵」。
  - 依据：S1 p.38–39，"As soon as I became acquainted with the situation and extent of this irruption of cholera, I suspected some contamination of the water of the much-frequented street-pump in Broad Street".
- **D2 ★ 9 月 3 日的检查**
  - 可写：on 3 September he found so little impurity in it that he hesitated。
  - 不可写：「水清澈见底」；「什么都没有」（他在随后两天看到了白色絮状颗粒）。
  - 依据：S1 p.39，"on examining the water, on the evening of the 3rd September, I found so little impurity in it of an organic nature, that I hesitated to come to a conclusion."；"the water varied, during the next two days, in the amount of organic impurity, visible to the naked eye, on close inspection, in the form of small white, flocculent particles".
- **D3 ○ 哈索尔的显微镜检查**
  - 可写：a microscope showed nothing that could be identified as the cause；particles with "no organised structure"。
  - 不可写：「斯诺没有显微镜」；「显微镜下什么活物都没有」（哈索尔看到了大量微小的「oval animalcules」，只是认为无关紧要）。
  - 依据：S1 p.52，"Dr. Hassall, who was good enough to examine some of this water with the microscope, informed me that these particles had no organised structure ... He found a great number of very minute oval animalcules in the water, which are of no importance, except as an additional proof that the water contained organic matter".
- **D4 ☆ 井水有「纯净」的名声，杂质还比无害的泵少**
  - 可写：its water passed for pure, and held less visible impurity than some harmless pumps nearby。
  - 不可写：「伦敦最干净的水」；「最受欢迎的泵」。
  - 依据：S1 p.53–54，"the water passed with almost everybody as being perfectly pure, and it did in fact contain a less quantity of impurity than the water of some other pumps in the same parish, which had no share in the propagation of cholera." 另见 p.42，"The pump was frequented much more than is usual, even for a London pump in a populous neighbourhood."
- **D5 禁用**：「他 9 月 5 日去登记总署」。这个日期只见于 Cosgrove 转引的教区报告版本，原著没写。
- **D6 ★ 死亡登记名单**
  - 可写：he copied the official register of deaths and went to the streets to ask where the dead had drawn their water。
  - 不可写：「敲遍每一扇门」；「他查了全部 616 例」。
  - 依据：S1 p.39，"I requested permission, therefore, to take a list, at the General Register Office, of the deaths from cholera, registered during the week ending 2nd September ... I made inquiry, in detail, respecting the eighty-three deaths registered as having taken place during the last three days of the week."；p.39 "On proceeding to the spot..."。p.41 还提到他同时忙于南伦敦的调查，没能逐一追问所有死者。
- **D7 ★ 规律，以及离别的泵更近的十例**
  - 可写：nearly all had died close to the pump；of ten who died nearer another pump, five households fetched Broad Street water by preference and three were children schooled near it。
  - 不可写：「十例全都喝了宽街水」；「死亡在两条街外归零」。
  - 依据：S1 p.39–40，"nearly all the deaths had taken place within a short distance of the pump. There were only ten deaths in houses situated decidedly nearer to another street pump. In five of these cases the families of the deceased persons informed me that they always sent to the pump in Broad Street, as they preferred the water to that of the pump which was nearer. In three other cases, the deceased were children who went to school near the pump in Broad Street. Two of them were known to drink the water; and the parents of the third think it probable that it did so."；p.47 "the deaths either very much diminished, or ceased altogether, at every point where it becomes decidedly nearer to send to another pump".
- **D8 ☆ 61／73**
  - 可写：61 of the 73 who died near it were known to drink its water。
  - 不可写：「所有死者都喝过」（另有 6 例查不到，6 例据说没喝）。
  - 依据：S1 p.40，"there were sixty-one instances in which I was informed that the deceased persons used to drink the pump-water from Broad Street, either constantly or occasionally. In six instances I could get no information ... and in six cases I was informed that the deceased persons did not drink the pump-water before their illness."
- **D9 ○ 斯诺承认的反例**
  - 依据：S1 p.45，"There were many persons who drank the water from Broad Street pump about the time of the outbreak, without being attacked with cholera". 写结尾时要记住这一句：不能说喝了就死。

### E 泵柄
- **E1 ★ 陈情与卸柄**
  - 可写：on the evening of 7 September he put his findings to the parish's Board of Guardians, and the handle was removed the next day。
  - 不可写：「斯诺亲手卸下泵柄」；「政府／市议会下令」。避免写 St James's，视频朗读会把 St 念错。
  - 依据：S1 p.40，"I had an interview with the Board of Guardians of St. James's parish, on the evening of Thursday, 7th September, and represented the above circumstances to them. In consequence of what I said, the handle of the pump was removed on the following day."
- **E2 ○ 委员会并不相信他**
  - 可写：the guardians, reportedly unconvinced, did it anyway。必须带 reportedly 或 by one account。
  - 不可写：把它写成不加限定的事实，或写成「委员会接受了建议，没接受结论」。
  - 依据：S5，理查森（B. W. Richardson，斯诺的友人和传记作者，1858）："The vestry was incredulous, but had the good sense to carry out the advice. The pump-handle was removed, and the plague was stayed." 注意后半句正是神话，不可采信。Chave（1958，检索摘要）："although they were quite incredulous they ordered the pump-handle to be removed on the following morning"。S6 所引兰克斯特（Lankester，1866）："He was not believed – not a member of his own profession, not an individual in the parish believed that Snow was right."
- **E3 ★ 疫情早已回落**
  - 可写：by then new fatal cases had fallen from 143 a day to 12；Snow himself could not tell whether the removal made a difference。
  - 不可写：「疫情应声而止」「救下了一条街」「病例立即停止」。
  - 依据：S1 p.51，"On September the 8th—the day when the handle of the pump was removed—there were twelve attacks"；S1 p.51–52，"the attacks had so far diminished before the use of the water was stopped, that it is impossible to decide whether the well still contained the cholera poison in an active state, or whether, from some cause, the water had become free from it."；S5 所引布拉德福德·希尔（1955）："it is clear that the end of the epidemic was not dramatically determined by its removal"。
- **E4 ○ 居民逃离也压低了死亡**
  - 依据：S1 p.51，"There is no doubt that the mortality was much diminished ... by the flight of the population".
- **E5 禁用**：「疫情过后官方把泵柄装了回去」。
  - 原因：只有维基百科无出处的说法，和一篇讲 1867 年林肯律师学院（Lincoln's Inn）水泵的档案文章，都不是宽街。
- **E6 禁用**：「卸柄防住了第二波（死婴的父亲 9 月 8 日发病）」。
  - 原因：细节未核实。最多可写 may have prevented a second wave，本篇不建议写。

### F 地图与对地图的解读
- **F1 ★ 地图是事后画的**
  - 可写：the famous map, a black bar for each death, was drawn afterwards and first shown in December 1854。
  - 不可写：「地图把他引向水泵」；「他在地图上看出规律」（指 9 月 8 日以前）；「地图证明了是水」。
  - 依据：S1 p.46，"All the deaths from cholera which were registered in the six weeks from 19th August to 30th September within this locality ... are shown in the map by a black line in the situation of the house"。收录的死亡截至 9 月 30 日，所以地图不可能早于 9 月 8 日。S4（约翰·斯诺档案馆地图说明）："Snow exhibited a pre-publication copy of his cholera map at the 4 December 1854 meeting of the Epidemiological Society of London."；Kenneth Field（Esri）："first shown at a meeting of the London Epidemiological Society in December 1854 but didn't in, and of itself get used in any way to identify the pattern of the outbreak at the time."；Brody 等，《柳叶刀》2000 年，"Map-making and myth-making in Broad Street"。斯诺本人在 S1 p.45 称之为 "a diagram of the topography of the outbreak"。
- **F2 ○ 修订版地图**：1855 年给教区霍乱调查委员会的修订版加了一条步行等距线。不建议写。
- **F3 ★ 帕克斯把死亡簇读成空气传播**
  - 可写：reviewing Snow's book in 1855, Edmund Parkes, a professor of clinical medicine, read the same pattern as a poison spreading through the air, and noted that the district had so many pumps that any outbreak would have had one nearby。
  - 不可写：「斯诺最大的对手」「全医学界都这么看」。原句里有 "where ever"（原文如此），建议转述，不要直接引。
  - 依据：S3，Koch，*Int J Epidemiol* 2013：「'It would clearly appear', agreed Parkes, 'that the center of the [cholera] outburst was a spot in Broad-street, close to which is the accused pump; and that cases were scattered all round this nearly in a circle...' But to Parkes the centric distribution appeared more likely to present the image of an airborne rather than waterborne pattern of disease diffusion. 'If it were owing to the water,' he asked, 'why should not the cholera have prevailed equally everywhere the water was drunk?' ... 'there are, indeed, so many pumps in this district that where ever the outbreak had taken place, it would most probably have had one pump or other in its vicinity'.」
  - 帕克斯的书评发表于 *British and Foreign Medico-Chirurgical Review* 1855;15:449–63，当时他是该刊主编、伦敦大学学院临床医学教授。
- **F4 ○ 街坊的空气说**
  - 可写：some blamed drains dug up months before; others, a pit of plague dead from the 1660s said to lie nearby。
  - 不可写：「鼠疫坑就在宽街底下」。斯诺说它据称在小马尔伯勒街（Little Marlborough Street），就在高死亡区外面。
  - 依据：S1 p.54，"Many persons were inclined to attribute the severity of the malady in this locality to ... the drains in the neighbourhood having been disturbed and put in order about half a year previously ... Many of the non-medical public were disposed to attribute the outbreak of cholera to the supposed existence of a pit in which persons dying of the plague had been buried about two centuries ago"；S9 转述教区委员会：「a pest field said to have been located within this area in 1665, 1666」。

### G 判别性证据
- **G1 ★ 济贫院**
  - 可写：the workhouse in Poland Street, more than three-quarters surrounded by houses where people died, lost only five of its 535 inmates；it had its own pump-well and piped water, and never sent to Broad Street；at the surrounding rate, over 100 would have died。
  - 不可写：borehole；「零死亡」；「院内只死了五人」（另有入院时已发病的人死在院内，不计入 5 人）。
  - 依据：S1 p.42，"The Workhouse in Poland Street is more than three-fourths surrounded by houses in which deaths from cholera occurred, yet out of five hundred and thirty-five inmates only five died of cholera, the other deaths which took place being those of persons admitted after they were attacked. The workhouse has a pump-well on the premises, in addition to the supply from the Grand Junction Water Works, and the inmates never sent to Broad Street for water. If the mortality in the workhouse had been equal to that in the streets immediately surrounding it on three sides, upwards of one hundred persons would have died."
- **G2 ★ 啤酒厂**
  - 可写：a brewery beside the pump lost none of its 70-odd men；none fell seriously ill；they had an allowance of malt liquor and, their employer believed, drank no water；the brewery had its own deep well。
  - 不可写：「零感染」（有两人轻度不适）；「煮沸杀死了细菌」（原文没有）；「只喝啤酒」（这是老板的看法）。
  - 依据：S1 p.42，"There is a Brewery in Broad Street, near to the pump, and on perceiving that no brewer's men were registered as having died of cholera, I called on Mr. Huggins, the proprietor. He informed me that there were above seventy workmen employed in the brewery, and that none of them had suffered from cholera,—at least in a severe form,—only two having been indisposed ... The men are allowed a certain quantity of malt liquor, and Mr. Huggins believes they do not drink water at all ... There is a deep well in the brewery, in addition to the New River water."
- **G3 ○ 雷管厂**
  - 可写：about 200 workers, two tubs kept filled from the pump, 18 died at home。
  - 不可写：「寡妇儿子的工厂」（二手说法），门牌号（二手来源 37 和 38 不一致）。
  - 依据：S1 p.43，"At the percussion-cap manufactory, 37 Broad Street, where, I understand, about two hundred workpeople were employed, two tubs were kept on the premises always supplied with water from the pump in the street ... and eighteen of these workpeople died of cholera at their own homes".
- **G4 ★ 汉普斯特德的寡妇**
  - 可写：a 59-year-old widow in Hampstead, miles from Soho, who had not been near Broad Street for months；a cart brought her a large bottle of the pump's water every day because she preferred it；she drank it on 31 August and 1 September and died on 2 September。
  - 姓名 Susannah Eley：**慎用**。斯诺原文不写姓名，名字出自后来的研究（The Ghost Map、Look Up London 等），不写也不损失什么。
  - 依据：S1 p.44，"At West End, on 2nd September, the widow of a percussion-cap maker, aged 59 years"；"I was informed by this lady's son that she had not been in the neighbourhood of Broad Street for many months. A cart went from Broad Street to West End every day, and it was the custom to take out a large bottle of the water from the pump in Broad Street, as she preferred it. The water was taken on Thursday, 31st August, and she drank of it in the evening, and also on Friday. She was seized with cholera on the evening of the latter day, and died on Saturday".
- **G5 ★ 侄女**
  - 可写：a visiting niece also drank it, went home to "a high and healthy part of Islington", and died too；there was no cholera in either place at the time。
  - 不可写：「次日死亡」；「啜了一口」（sipped）；「就死在那一瓶水上」。
  - 依据：S1 p.44–45，"A niece, who was on a visit to this lady, also drank of the water; she returned to her residence, in a high and healthy part of Islington, was attacked with cholera, and died also. There was no cholera at the time, either at West End or in the neighbourhood where the niece died."
  - 中文：niece 是侄女还是外甥女，原文无从判断。译文选一个，全篇统一；速查表两义都给。
- **G6 ☆ 斯诺自己的判断**
  - 可写：Snow thought these deaths "perhaps the most conclusive of all"。必须带 perhaps。
  - 不可写：「斯诺追查到了这位寡妇」「斯诺发现了此案」。这个案子是弗雷泽医生（Dr Fraser, of Oakley Square）先提请他注意的；也不要说弗雷泽是官方调查员（未核实）。
  - 依据：S1 p.44，"Dr. Fraser also first called my attention to the following circumstances, which are perhaps the most conclusive of all in proving the connexion between the Broad Street pump and the outbreak of cholera."

### H 余波（全部可选）
- **H1 ○ 怀特黑德与约克的勘查**
  - 可写：the next spring a local curate, at first sceptical, traced the first case to a baby's home beside the well, whose decayed drain and cesspool had been seeping into it。
  - 不可写：「粪坑距井两英尺八英寸」（据约克报告，两英尺八英寸是**房屋排水沟**与井壁的距离）；婴儿发病的具体日期（二手来源有 8 月 24 日、28 日两说）。
  - 依据：S9 转引约克 1855 年 4 月的报告：「The bottom of the main drain of the house No. 40 Broad Street ... one of its sides was distant from the brick lining of the well only 2 feet 8 inches」，另发现一个砖砌朽坏的 cesspool，「constant percolation for a considerable period had been conveying fluid matter from the drains into the well」；怀特黑德 1855 年春才做逐户调查。
  - 本篇不建议写。词数不够，还会把焦点引到「机制」上。
- **H2 ○ 1855 年官方驳回，理由落在「喝水者」上**（给 P5 用）
  - 可写：a government inquiry in 1855 saw "no reason" to blame the well, saying nobody had shown that its drinkers suffered more than their neighbours。
  - 不可写：长段逐字引语（各转引版本有 find／feel 之类的小差异）；「法尔写的」（维基的归属可疑）。
  - 依据：S7，卫生总会科学调查委员会（General Board of Health, Committee for Scientific Inquiries）1855 年：「After careful inquiry, we see no reason to adopt this belief. We do not feel it established that the water was contaminated in the manner alleged; nor is there before us any sufficient evidence to show whether inhabitants of that district, drinking from that well, suffered in proportion more than other inhabitants of the district who drank from other sources.」S6：「The Board of Health ultimately attributed the 1854 epidemic to miasma.」
- **H3 ○ 法尔 1866 年转变**
  - 依据：S8，「It was not until 1866 that William Farr, one of Snow's chief opponents, realised the validity of his diagnosis」。本篇不建议写。
- **H4 ○ 复制水泵**
  - 依据：S6，1992 年在原址安装复制水泵，2015–16 年因施工移走，2019 年重装在约翰·斯诺酒吧外的人行道上。

## 6 结尾要求

### 6.1 最后一句要做到
1. 把全文收成一个可以带走的框架：**地点（where，地图能回答的）对饮水（what they drank，需要去问的）**。
2. 句子里的每个判断都要能在正文里找到支撑：「在哪里」对应 P3 的地图和帕克斯，「喝了什么」对应 P2 的询问和 P4 的对照。
3. 对宽街而言必须字面成立。如果要往外推广，只准用 can／could／often，不准用 only／always／never。
4. 呼应标题，但不照抄标题。
5. 32 词以内，最好 12–20 词。不引入新事实，不用问句或感叹句。
6. 最后一段只准有**一句**格言式的话。它前面那句必须是事实句或分析句，推荐用 H2，否则用 E3 或 F1 里的一个具体细节。

### 6.2 三个方向（示意，不给成句）
- **方向 A（推荐）**：地图只能说清「在哪里」，这个问题要靠「喝了什么」才能回答。前半句落在地图，后半句落在人。
- **方向 B**：「看」与「问」的对照。水看上去是干净的，地图看上去像空气传播，证据在喝水的人身上。适合 P1 写了 D4、P3 写了 F3 的版本。
- **方向 C（只在 P5 用了 H2 时）**：连驳回斯诺的一方也承认，分歧在于喝这口水的人是不是死得更多；收在「问题问对了，地图才有意义」一类意思上。

### 6.3 最后一句不许
- 暗示卸下泵柄止住了疫情，或泵柄「救了人」；
- 说斯诺当时就「证明」了、说服了同行，或说「例外说服了所有人」；
- 说地图错了、没用、误导人（地图是准确的，只是分不出两种理论）；
- 回到旧稿的道德寓意（certainty is the wrong thing to wait for，不必等确定性），评审已指出它与正文矛盾；
- 写成「少数推翻多数」「异常才是证据」，会与 17、16、09 篇撞车；
- 出现 germs、bacteria，像是斯诺当年就有的证据；
- 用 only、always、never、every 做全称推广；
- 出现问号、感叹号、分号连接的两句格言。

## 7 文风与视频硬约束（执笔与特稿作者）

- **词数**：249–271，一个都不能超出。按 3.2 的分段预算写，交稿时附每段词数。
- **句长**：每句不超过 32 词，平均 16–20 词。一句只讲一件事，长短句交替。
- **英式拼写与用法**：neighbourhood、sceptical、favour、programme；日期写作 8 September 1854；不用 block 作距离单位，写 streets away 或 yards。
- **视频友好**：
  - 不用带句点的缩写（St.、Dr.、Mr.、No. 40、U.S.），也尽量不用 St、Dr、Mr 这类头衔：写 John Snow、the parish's Board of Guardians、number 40。
  - 不用单星号斜体，不用 °、£、±，不用小数。
  - 英尺英寸用文字写（less than three feet），不写 2 ft 8 in。
  - 句中不出现「?」「!」，全文最好都不出现。
  - 句子不以阿拉伯数字开头。
- **引语**：全篇最多 3 处直接引语，每处不超过 10 个词，逐字照原文（含 probably、perhaps）。推荐 "perhaps the most conclusive of all"、"a high and healthy part of Islington"。不要给引语中的词加粗。
- **经济学人腔**：
  - 开头直接进事实：地点、日期、数字，或斯诺本人的最高级判断（必须带 probably）。
  - 不写「侦探」「鞋底功夫」「幽灵地图」「流行病学之父」「the rest is history」这类套话。
  - 评论只放在 P5；P1–P4 让事实往前推，最多用一个短句交接。
  - 少用被动语态。事实细节（车每天从宽街送水、七十多名酿酒工、535 名收容者）比形容词更有力。
- **专名与术语**：
  - miasma 第一次出现就用 bad air 解释。
  - workhouse 用一个短语交代（a workhouse for the poor）。
  - 宽街写 Broad Street，可以不注今名。
  - Board of Guardians 第一次出现前加 the parish's。

## 8 词汇环境（语言学家主责，执笔必读）

50 个重点词占约 260 词的 19%，正文必须**有意识地**写得词汇丰富。执笔起草时，每段至少嵌入 12–15 个下列可用词，全文不少于 65 个候选，语言学家才有得挑。旧稿失分最多的就是这一项（白送词 15–18 个，冷僻词 5–10 个，同词占两格 2 对）。

cholera, miasma, workhouse, inmates, malt, cesspool, sewer, parish, curate, anomaly, stench, sewage, incredulous, pestilence。

**基础词别占名额**：evidence, theory, district, taste, preferred, escaped, fled, spread, pattern, handle, removed 等，都是初中词或高中常用词。

### 8.4 选词纪律
- 取最该学的义项。旧稿的 stroke（一划）、address（地址）、handle（把手）都因此被扣分。

- 专名里的词不加粗（Board of Guardians 的 guardians）。
- 引语里的词不加粗。
- 50 个词要大致铺开，每段 8–12 个。P5 很短，可以只有 5–7 个，差额摊到 P2、P4。
