## 逐条问题

1. 【该改｜项1｜−1】原文 "Neither place had cholera." Snow p.44–45 原话是 "no cholera at the time, either at West End or in the neighbourhood where the niece died"——指汉普斯特德西区和侄女住的那一片（"a high and healthy part of Islington"），不是整个 Hampstead 和 Islington。改：Neither neighbourhood had cholera at the time. 中文相应改为「当时这两处街区都没有霍乱」。
2. 【可改｜项1｜−0.5】"a workhouse with its own well"——济贫院另有 Grand Junction 自来水供应（p.42）。论点关键是「从不去宽街打水」，建议：a workhouse that drew its own water and never sent to the pump。
3. 【该改｜项2｜−0.5】"Its water looked almost clean, so he consulted the death register"——"so" 把「水看着干净」当成查册的原因，逻辑不顺；实情是化验无结论、他转而找别的证据。改：Its water looked almost clean, so he turned from the sample to the death register... 或 "Finding the water almost clean, he turned instead to the death register"。
4. 【可改｜项2｜−0.5】第3段末 "and Snow counted drinkers only among the dead" 归属不清：是帕克斯的批评还是作者的判断？建议：The objection had force: Snow had counted drinkers only among the dead. 这样第4段「关键证据」和结尾怀特黑德的比率才明确是在补这个缺口，反转更干净。
5. 【该改｜项3｜−1】"the pump's drinkers" 不地道（像是泵在喝）。改：those who drank from the pump。（drinkers 作重点词可保留：the pump water's drinkers 仍别扭，建议换 "those who drank" 并另选词，或写 "found that drinkers of the pump water died far more often"。）
6. 【可改｜项3｜−0.5】"Amid the surrounding deaths" 抽象别扭。改：Though almost surrounded by stricken houses, a workhouse...（amid 可移作 "amid streets full of death"）。
7. 【可改｜项3｜−0.5】"cholera was swallowed, not inhaled"——被吞的是病原，不是病。改：that cholera was caught by swallowing, not breathing, ... 或 "that the cholera poison was swallowed, not inhaled"。
8. 【可改｜项5｜−0.5】word_tier 报 1 个 △高中较常用（peak 一层），语言学家须写明理由，否则扣。
9. 【可改｜项6｜−0.5】drinkers 在考研语料里的典型义是「饮酒者」，文中用法非最典型（与第5条合并处理）。
10. 【可改｜项7｜−0.5】poison 释义未给出本文涉及的「（空气中的）有害物质」与19世纪 "poison" 泛指致病物之义；可加注。
11. 【可改｜项8｜−0.5】"Neither place had cholera" 中文随第1条改。
12. 【可改｜项9｜−0.5】「泵里的水看上去几乎是干净的」→「这口泵的水看上去相当干净」；「死亡比例远高于其他人」→「死亡率远高于不喝的人」。

## 必改清单（改完预计 96–97）

2. 第2段首句因果改顺。
