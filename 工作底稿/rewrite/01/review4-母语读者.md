## 逐条问题

### 项3 语言质量（-5）
1. 该改 -1｜“In ten days of 1854” → 母语者说 “Over ten days in 1854” / “In ten days in the late summer of 1854”。“days of 1854” 读着像翻译腔。
2. 该改 -1｜“Its water looked almost clean” → “almost clean” 暗示“有点脏”，与原意（几乎人人当它纯净）相反，且与后文 “so” 的因果别扭。改：“Its water looked clean, so he turned instead to the death register and questioned the living.”
3. 该改 -1｜“a poison drifting through air” → 须加冠词：“drifting through the air”。
4. 该改 -1｜“She perished, as did a niece who drank it while visiting and died back home in Islington.” → perished 与 died 同句重复，且 “as did … and died” 冗赘。改：“She perished; so did a niece who drank it while visiting her and then went home to Islington.”
5. 该改 -1｜“Amid the surrounding deaths, a workhouse …” → “surrounding deaths” 搭配生硬（死亡不会“环绕”）。改：“Amid the deaths all around it, a workhouse with its own well lost a mere five of 535 inmates.”
6. 可改（不扣）｜“convinced since 1849 that cholera was swallowed, not inhaled” —— 严格说被吞下的是病原而非“霍乱”，母语编辑会轻轻标注，但口语可接受。
7. 可改（不扣）｜“Neither place had cholera.” → “Neither district had any cholera at the time.” 更完整。
8. 可改（不扣）｜结尾 “vindicating him” 的 him 前面有 Whitehead 与 Snow 两个男性，虽按就近可指 Snow，仍建议 “vindicating Snow”（但会与前面的 Snow 重复，可改为 “refute Snow but found … , proving him right”）。

### 项6 用法典型（-1）
- 该改 -1｜“the pump's drinkers” —— drinker 最典型义是“喝酒的人”，“the pump's drinkers”是为凑重点词造出的别扭搭配。改：“found that those who drank from the pump died far more often than those who did not”，重点词换 drinkers → 保留在 “water drinkers” 形式：“the pump's regular drinkers”仍别扭，建议直接换词。

其余重点词用法典型：claim lives、attribute A to B、at his urging、plunge、decline to、contend that、a mere、chart 均为最该学的意义与搭配。register 为 △，“death register”地道，判达标。

### 项4 适配读者（-1）
- curate、workhouse 未交代，考研生多不认识。改：“a sceptical curate (a junior clergyman)” 或正文写 “a young clergyman”；workhouse 可写 “a workhouse for the poor”。

### 项2 论证与结构（-1）
- “Snow counted drinkers only among the dead” 过于简省，读者要停下来想才明白“缺对照组”。建议：“and Snow had counted drinkers only among the dead, never among the living.”（与第2段 questioned the living 也要协调——此处“living”指幸存者还是对照？略矛盾，建议统一。）

### 项1 史实（-1）
- “distant Hampstead”：汉普斯特德距苏豪约4英里，“distant”略夸张；可改 “a widow in Hampstead, miles away”。
