## 3 语言质量（主责，逐句）

| # | 级别 | 原文 | 问题 | 改法 | 扣 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | 该改 | a physician **convinced since 1849** that… | 过去分词后挂 since 短语，母语者读着别扭，缺完成体 | a physician **who had been convinced since 1849** that…（或 who had argued since 1849 that…） | −1 |
| 3.2 | 该改 | but **new fatal cases** had already plunged | new fatal cases 是生造搭配；"新增的致命病例"英文说 fatal attacks / deaths | but **daily deaths**（或 fatal attacks）had already plunged from a peak of 143 to 12 | −1 |
| 3.3 | 该改 | Snow had questioned only **the dead's families** | the dead's families 生硬；且 questioned 与第 2 段重复 | Snow had **spoken only to the families of the dead** | −1 |
| 3.4 | 该改 | Amid the deaths in **surrounding streets** | 缺冠词，指特定街区应加 the | Amid the deaths in **the surrounding streets** | −1 |
| 3.5 | 该改 | a widow…, **absent from Soho for months** | 暗示她本住苏豪、只是离开；原意是"几个月没去过" | a widow in Hampstead, miles away, **who had not set foot in Soho for months**, … | −1 |
| 3.6 | 该改 | but found **those who drank pump water succumbed** far more often | 省 that 造成花园小径句；far more often 缺比较对象 | but found **that** those who drank from the pump **died far more often than those who did not** | −1 |
| 3.7 | 该改 | …refute Snow…, vindicating **Snow** | 句内 Snow 重复，母语编辑必删 | …, **vindicating him** | −1 |
| 3.8 | 可改 | **Meanwhile** Henry Whitehead | 句首连接副词后宜加逗号；且 Meanwhile 接在"1855 年调查"后，时间先后含糊（怀特黑德调查始于 1854 年 11 月） | **Meanwhile,** … 或 **Yet** Henry Whitehead… had already… | −0.5 |
| 3.9 | 可改 | **Over** ten days… / **Over** 70 nearby… | 两处 over 表数量/时段，编辑会统一一处为 More than | More than 70 brewery workers | −0.5 |

## 6 用法典型（主责）

| # | 级别 | 词 | 问题 | 改法 | 扣 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | 该改 | **perished** | perish 典型用于战争、灾难、火灾中成批死亡；个人喝水染病而死用 perish 语域偏高、偏戏剧化，不是最该学的用法 | She **died**…；perish 移至第 1 段（500 people perished）| −1 |
| 6.2 | 该改 | **succumbed** far more often | 最该学的是 succumb to（速查表也这么教），文中不带 to，学生学不到核心搭配 | …were far more likely to **succumb to** the disease | −1 |
| 6.3 | 该改 | brewery **labourers** | 酿酒厂工人母语者说 brewery workers/men；labourer 典型指体力苦工（farm labourer, casual labourer），此处为凑词 | 换词，或改 70 men **labouring** in a nearby brewery 之类（仍嫌牵强，建议换掉该词）| −1 |

## 7–10（非主责，简评）
