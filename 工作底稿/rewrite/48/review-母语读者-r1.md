## 总体判断
- 结构：公投否决→议会推翻→理由→准备→切换之夜→事故先降后回升，完整；“H 日”凌晨停车换边是全文高潮。
- 结尾：原句 “But the nervousness subsided, prudent habits faded, and by 1969…” 把“紧张消退、谨慎习惯淡去”写成直陈事实，而来源只给出事故率回升。你拟的 “The nervousness apparently subsided and prudent habits faded, for by 1969 …” 方向正确：apparently 把前两分句标为推断，for 把事故率回升作为证据，逻辑与事实都成立，且仍以 baseline 收尾、落得住。建议再改一处：for 偏书面古雅，改冒号更利落（见第 1 条）。

## 必改
1. 【该改｜2｜−0.5】结尾采纳推断式，用冒号：“The **nervousness** apparently **subsided** and **prudent** **habits** **faded**: by 1969 accident and fatality rates had **reverted** to their **baseline**.”（较原句 −1 词：删 But、and，加 apparently；冒号替 for 再省 1 词）加粗不变。
2. 【该改｜2｜−0.5】P1 “in **defiance** of a **populace** whose **resistance** had made the plan **unpopular**” 循环论证（反对使之不受欢迎），且 resistance 无独立来源。改法：“in **defiance** of a **populace** whose **resistance** the vote had made plain.”（0 词；resistance 由公投 83% 支撑；P1 加粗 11→10，总数 50）
3. 【该改｜3｜−1】P4 “At ten to five, all traffic stopped, **crawled** across and set off at five on **flipped** roads, before **dawn**.” 一连串动词、两个时间状语错位，flipped roads 生硬。改法：“At ten to five all traffic stopped, **crawled** across to the right and restarted at five, before **dawn**, on **flipped** roads.”（+1 词）
4. 【该改｜2｜−0.5】P2 “so **motorists** **overtaking** on narrow two-**lane** **highways** caused **head-on** collisions” caused 断言过强。改法：“…risked **head-on** collisions.”（0 词）
5. 【该改｜3｜−0.5】P5 “a **statistical** gain put down to…” gain 指事故减少不自然。改法：“a **statistical** improvement put down to…”（0 词）
