# EN: The First Shot Against Smallpox
# ZH: 终结天花的第一针

## 英文
Smallpox, a **contagious** **affliction**, killed about three in ten of its **sufferers** and an estimated 300 million in the 20th century. An older **safeguard**, **deliberately** **implanting** matter from its **sores** in the healthy, was **practised** in China by the 16th century and reached England in 1721. The **procedure** itself could cause **grave** **sickness** or death, if less **frequently** than smallpox.

**Rustic** **lore** held that milkmaids with cowpox, a **mild** **ailment** of cattle, were **exempt** from smallpox. In 1774 Benjamin Jesty, a Dorset farmer, had **employed** cowpox on his wife and two sons. On 14 May 1796 Edward Jenner, a **provincial** **practitioner**, **inserted** **fluid** from a milkmaid's cowpox sore into a **scratch** on James Phipps, aged 8.

On 1 July 1796 Jenner exposed the boy to smallpox; no **symptoms** **ensued**. In 1797, however, the Royal Society declined to **publish** his paper, judging the **proof** **deficient**. In June 1798 he **privately** **funded** and issued his Inquiry, with **supplementary** cases. The word **vaccine** **derives** from the **Latin** vacca, cow.

By today's standards, **experimentation** on a child without **informed** **consent** would be **unethical**. Jenner was not the idea's **originator**. His contribution was a test, made **accessible** in print, which let other doctors **replicate** and **disseminate** the **technique**. He died on 26 January 1823, with smallpox far from **eliminated**.

In 1967 the WHO launched an **intensified**, **concerted** campaign to **eradicate** it. The last **naturally** **occurring** case was **diagnosed** in Somalia on 26 October 1977. On 8 May 1980 the World Health Assembly **formally** declared the world free of smallpox, the only human disease ever **vanquished**. That **pronouncement** came almost 184 years after Phipps was **vaccinated**.

## 中文

天花是一种**传染性**(contagious)**病痛**(affliction)，约三成**患者**(sufferers)因此丧命；仅在20世纪，估计就有3亿人死于天花。一种更早的**防护手段**(safeguard)，是把天花**脓疮**(sores)中的物质**有意**(deliberately)**植入**(implanting)健康人体内。到16世纪，中国已在**施行**(practised)这种方法，1721年这种方法又传入英国。这种**做法**(procedure)本身也可能引起**严重**(grave)**疾病**(sickness)乃至死亡，虽然不像天花那样**频繁**(frequently)。

**乡间**(rustic)**传说**(lore)认为，挤奶女工若得过牛痘这种牛的**轻微**(mild)**病症**(ailment)，就能**免于**(exempt)感染天花。1774年，多塞特郡农夫本杰明·杰斯蒂已在妻子和两个儿子身上**使用**(employed)过牛痘。1796年5月14日，**地方**(provincial)**医生**(practitioner)爱德华·詹纳**注入**(inserted)取自一名挤奶女工牛痘疮的**液体**(fluid)，注入的位置是8岁的詹姆斯·菲普斯身上的一道**划痕**(scratch)。

1796年7月1日，詹纳让这个男孩接触天花，此后任何**症状**(symptoms)都没有**随之出现**(ensued)。然而在1797年，皇家学会不予**发表**(publish)他的论文，认为**证据**(proof)**不足**(deficient)。1798年6月，詹纳**自行**(privately)**出资**(funded)印行了《探究》，书中附上了**补充**(supplementary)病例。表示**疫苗**(vaccine)的英文词**源自**(derives)**拉丁语**(latin)的 vacca，意思是“牛”。

按今天的标准，拿孩子做**实验**(experimentation)而不取得**知情**(informed)**同意**(consent)，属于**不道德**(unethical)行为。詹纳并不是这一想法的**首创者**(originator)。他的贡献是一项试验，并借印刷品让人**得以查阅**(accessible)，其他医生由此能够**重复**(replicate)并**推广**(disseminate)这项**技术**(technique)。詹纳于1823年1月26日去世，那时天花还远未被**消除**(eliminated)。

1967年，世界卫生组织发起了一场**强化**(intensified)、**协同**(concerted)的运动，要**根除**(eradicate)天花。最后一例**自然**(naturally)**发生**(occurring)的病例于1977年10月26日在索马里**确诊**(diagnosed)。1980年5月8日，世界卫生大会**正式**(formally)宣布全世界已摆脱天花，天花也成为迄今唯一被**战胜**(vanquished)的人类疾病。这一**宣告**(pronouncement)距菲普斯**接种**(vaccinated)已近184年。
