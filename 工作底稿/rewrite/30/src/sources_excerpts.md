## Myths to avoid
- Jenner did not invent immunisation (variolation predates him) nor first use cowpox (Jesty 1774, others e.g. Peter Plett 1791).
- Romanticised "milkmaid told Jenner" anecdote is unsourced legend.
- Spelling Nelmes/Nelms both seen.
- Don't say Royal Society "formally rejected" or "ridiculed" him.
- "Last case" 1977 = last natural case; 1978 Janet Parker lab case.
