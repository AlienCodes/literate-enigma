# 第50篇 来源摘要（检索摘要，不得直接引用原文）
- S1 Wikipedia "Post-it note" / MNopedia / 3M timeline: in 1968 Spencer Silver, a 3M scientist, was trying to develop a super-strong adhesive and instead created a low-tack, reusable, pressure-sensitive adhesive (acrylate microspheres) that stuck lightly and could be removed and reused.
- S2 For five years Silver promoted the adhesive within 3M, informally and through seminars, calling it a "solution without a problem"; no product emerged.
- S3 In 1974 Art Fry, a 3M colleague who had heard one of Silver's seminars, used scraps of paper as bookmarks in his church choir hymnal; they kept falling out. He thought of using Silver's adhesive to make a bookmark that would stay put without damaging pages.
- S4 1977: test-marketed as "Press 'n Peel" in four US cities; lukewarm reception.
- S5 1978: "Boise Blitz" — 3M gave free samples to offices in Boise, Idaho; more than 90 per cent of those who tried them said they would buy the product.
- S6 Launched across the US as Post-it Notes on 6 April 1980; Canada and Europe in 1981.
- S7 The canary-yellow colour was chosen by chance: a lab next door had only scrap yellow paper.
- S8 3M has long let staff spend about 15% of their time on projects of their own choosing (reported since 1948).
- S9 Spencer Silver died on 8 May 2021, aged 80.
