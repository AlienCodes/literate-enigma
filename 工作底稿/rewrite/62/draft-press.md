# EN: The Day the Doomsday Vault Opened
# ZH: 末日种子库的第一次取种

## 英文

On Spitsbergen, a **corridor** **drilled** into a **frosted** **hillside** leads to a **shadowy** **bunker**. Inside, at minus 18 degrees, **cartons** of seeds sit **neatly** in **ice-cold** **stillness**, waiting **quietly** for a **crisis**.

The Svalbard Global Seed Vault opened near Longyearbyen, in Norway's **archipelago**, in February 2008. **Insulated** by **permanently** frozen ground, it was built to **withstand** nuclear war, disease and other forms of **apocalypse**. It has room for about four and a half million **precious** samples of **beans**, rice, wheat and other crops. It is the world's **ultimate** **backup**, not a **granary**. Like a bank's **safe-deposit box**, it leaves **ownership** with the **depositors**, and only the **rightful** owner can withdraw seeds.

The first **retrieval** was the work not of nature but of war. With civil war **raging** in Syria, ICARDA, a research centre whose **gene** bank stood near Aleppo, had moved its headquarters to Beirut, away from the **turmoil**. In September 2015 it asked for about 116,000 samples, 130 of the 325 boxes deposited before the **hostilities**. Among them were **robust** strains of wheat and **barley**, **tolerant** of drought and heat.

In Lebanon and Morocco, ICARDA **sowed** the seeds, and **vigorous** plants **sprouted** to rebuild its **holdings**. Once **multiplication** had gone far enough, a **homecoming** began in 2017, **consignment** by consignment, **restocking** the Arctic **stronghold**.

That same year, after **unusually** warm temperatures, **uninvited** water from the **thaw** ran into the 100-metre entrance tunnel and **solidified**. The **intrusion** stopped short of the seeds, which stayed at minus 18 degrees. Norway **waterproofed** the tunnel and dug **drainage** **ditches**. A **shelter** built against war and disease now has to be **fortified** against warm weather.

## 中文

在斯匹次卑尔根岛，一条**钻凿**(drilled)进**覆霜**(frosted)的**山坡**(hillside)的**通道**(corridor)，通向一座**幽暗**(shadowy)的**地堡**(bunker)。里面零下18度，装着种子的**纸箱**(cartons)摆放得**整整齐齐**(neatly)，在**冰冷**(ice-cold)的**寂静**(stillness)中**静静**(quietly)等待一场**危机**(crisis)。

斯瓦尔巴全球种子库于2008年2月在挪威斯瓦尔巴**群岛**(archipelago)的朗伊尔城附近启用。它被**永久**(permanently)冻结的土层**隔绝**(Insulated)保护，建来**抵御**(withstand)核战争、疾病和其他种种**末日浩劫**(apocalypse)。它能容纳约450万份**珍贵**(precious)的种子样本，有**豆类**(beans)、水稻、小麦和其他作物。它是世界的**终极**(ultimate)的**备份**(backup)，而不是**粮仓**(granary)。它就像银行的**保险箱**(safe-deposit box)，**所有权**(ownership)留在**存放者**(depositors)手里，只有**合法**(rightful)的主人才能把种子取走。

第一次**取回**(retrieval)种子，起因不是天灾，而是战争。叙利亚内战**肆虐**(raging)，国际干旱地区农业研究中心（ICARDA）原本设在阿勒颇附近的**基因**(gene)库，已把总部迁到贝鲁特，远离那片**动荡**(turmoil)。2015年9月，它请求取回约11.6万份样本，即**战事**(hostilities)爆发前存入的325箱中的130箱。其中有小麦和**大麦**(barley)的**强健**(robust)品种，**耐受**(tolerant)干旱和高温。

在黎巴嫩和摩洛哥，ICARDA**播下**(sowed)这些种子，**茁壮**(vigorous)的植株**发芽**(sprouted)生长，重建它的**收藏**(holdings)。**繁殖**(multiplication)到足够数量后，2017年起，种子开始**回家**(homecoming)，一**批货**(consignment)接一批，重新**充实**(restocking)北极的这座**堡垒**(stronghold)。

同一年，在**异常**(unusually)温暖的天气之后，**融化**(thaw)的水**不请自来**(uninvited)，流进100米长的入口隧道，**凝结**(solidified)成冰。这次**入侵**(intrusion)没有到达种子，种子一直保持在零下18度。挪威给隧道做了**防水**(waterproofed)处理，并挖了**排水**(drainage)用的**沟渠**(ditches)。一座为抵御战争和疾病而建的**避难所**(shelter)，如今也得**加固**(fortified)以对付温暖的天气。
