# EN: The Day the Doomsday Vault Opened
# ZH: 末日种子库的第一次取种

## 英文

On a **rugged** mountainside near Longyearbyen, on the **Arctic** **archipelago** of Svalbard, a tunnel is **drilled** into the rock. At its end, in chambers **insulated** by permafrost, **unopened** **cartons** of **precious** seeds sit **silently** at **minus** 18 degrees Celsius.

The Svalbard Global Seed Vault opened in February 2008 as a **bunker** against the **apocalypse**, built for **posterity**. Its **planners** **envisioned** **atomic** war, disease and other **worst-case** **scenarios**, leaving **ample** room for some four and a half million samples of **beans**, rice, wheat and other crops. Yet the **repository** is less a **fortress** than a **backup**. **Depositors** keep **ownership**, and like a bank's safe-deposit box, it opens **solely** for its **rightful** **proprietors**.

The **first-ever** **requisition**, in September 2015, answered no global **nightmare** but Syria's civil war. ICARDA, whose **gene** bank had stood near **war-torn** Aleppo, had moved its headquarters to Beirut amid the **hostilities**. It asked for about 116,000 samples, 130 of its 325 boxes deposited before the **strife**. Among them were wheat, **barley** and other crops **tolerant** of drought and heat, **invaluable** in **semi-arid** lands.

ICARDA **sowed** the seeds in Lebanon and Morocco, **reaped** the harvests and rebuilt its collection. Once the crops had **bred** enough fresh seed, the institute began a **homecoming** to Svalbard in 2017, **restocking** its northern **haven**.

**Ominously**, reports in May 2017 exposed a bunker **susceptible** to **intrusion**. After **record-breaking** warmth, meltwater had run into the 100-metre entrance tunnel and frozen. It did not reach the seeds, which held at minus 18 degrees. Norway **waterproofed** the tunnel and dug **drainage** **ditches**.

The **heirs** of seeds rescued from a war now rest behind a tunnel **armoured** against **thaw**.

从战争中抢救出来的种子，它们的**后代**(heirs)如今安睡在一条**加固**(armoured)过、抵御**融冻**(thaw)的隧道后面。

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】斯瓦尔巴全球种子库2008年2月26日启用，位于挪威斯瓦尔巴群岛斯匹次卑尔根岛、朗伊尔城附近，挖进山体；种子保存在约−18 °C，有永久冻土保护；可容约450万份种子样本；为防范核战争、疾病等灾难而建，保护豆类、水稻、小麦等作物。S1
- 【T2】它是备份：存入者保有所有权，只有存入者能取回自己的种子，像银行保险箱。S1
- 【T3】2015年9月，叙利亚内战促成第一次取种；ICARDA基因库原在阿勒颇附近，总部因战争迁到贝鲁特；请求取回约11.6万份样本，即战前存入的325箱中的130箱，包括耐旱耐热的小麦、大麦等。S2
- 【T4】ICARDA在黎巴嫩和摩洛哥种下种子、重建收藏，繁育到足够数量后自2017年起把新种子送回斯瓦尔巴。S3
- 【T5】2017年5月报道：创纪录的温暖天气后，融水流进约100米长的入口隧道并结冰，未到达种子，种子保持−18 °C；挪威随后给隧道做防水并挖排水沟。S4
- 【说明】开篇场景（崎岖山坡、钻进岩石、未开封的箱子静静存放）是对"挖进山体、箱装种子冷藏"的描写；drilled 对应来源 dug；atomic war 即 nuclear war；semi-arid 据 ICARDA"干旱地区"性质；reaped the harvests、bred 是对"种植并繁育"的具体化；susceptible to intrusion、armoured 是对融水进入与防水加固的描写；heirs 指取回种子繁育出的后代种子。
