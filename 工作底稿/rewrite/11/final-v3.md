# EN: Microplastics: Everywhere, but How Harmful?
# ZH: 微塑料：无处不在，危害几何？
## 英文

In May 2004 Richard Thompson, a **marine** **biologist** at the University of Plymouth, and colleagues described a new **pollutant** in the journal Science. **Sifting** through **plankton** samples kept since the 1960s, they found **microscopic** plastic **fragments** and **synthetic** **fibres** had grown more **abundant** in the sea. They **labelled** the **particles** microplastics.

Since then the **durable** **debris** has proved **ubiquitous**: on **shorelines**, in deep-sea **sediment**, in **rainfall** and in Arctic ice. **Regulation** came before proof of harm to people: in December 2015 America passed a law banning microbeads from **scrubs** and other **cosmetics** **rinsed** down drains.

The search then turned **inward**. In March 2022 a small Dutch study found plastic in the blood of about three-quarters of 22 healthy **donors**. In March 2024 a study in the New England Journal of Medicine linked plastic in the **fatty** **plaque** of neck **arteries** to a higher risk of heart attack, **stroke** or death. Its authors **cautioned** that **correlation** is not **causation**.

The most **sensational** figures are the **shakiest**. In June 2019 a report for WWF, a **conservation** group, **reckoned** people might swallow a credit card's worth of plastic a week; **critics** call that an **overestimate**. Two months later the World Health Organization was **reassuring** but **tentative**: on limited evidence, microplastics in drinking water posed no **apparent** risk at current levels. In February 2025 a study in Nature Medicine reported plastic in brain **tissue**, popularised as a **spoonful**; others **queried** its **methodology**.

**Detection** has outrun understanding; human studies remain small or **observational**. Harm is **plausible** but **unproven**, and until the **verdict** is in, better **measurement** may guide policy more safely than **panic**.

## 中文

2004年5月，普利茅斯大学的**海洋**(marine)**生物学家**(biologist)理查德·汤普森和同事在《科学》杂志上撰文，介绍了一种新的**污染物**(pollutant)。他们**细细筛查**(Sifting)自20世纪60年代起保存下来的**浮游生物**(plankton)样本，发现海里**极其微小的**(microscopic)塑料**碎片**(fragments)和**合成**(synthetic)**纤维**(fibres)变得越来越**多**(abundant)。他们把这些**颗粒**(particles)**称为**(labelled)“微塑料”。

此后，这些**经久不烂的**(durable)**碎屑**(debris)被证明**无所不在**(ubiquitous)，**海岸线**(shorelines)上、深海**沉积物**(sediment)里、**雨水**(rainfall)中和北极的冰层中都有它们的踪迹。**监管**(regulation)先于人体危害的证据出台：2015年12月，美国立法禁止在**磨砂膏**(scrubs)等用后被**冲**(rinsed)进下水道的**化妆品**(cosmetics)中使用微珠。

随后，人们的搜寻转向了**体内**(inward)。2022年3月，荷兰一项小规模研究检测了22名健康**献血者**(donors)的血液，约四分之三的人血中有塑料。2024年3月，《新英格兰医学杂志》上的一项研究发现，颈部**动脉**(arteries)的**脂肪性**(fatty)**斑块**(plaque)中含有塑料，与心脏病发作、**中风**(stroke)或死亡的风险升高有关。研究者**提醒**(cautioned)，**相关性**(correlation)并不等于**因果关系**(causation)。

最**耸人听闻的**(sensational)数字，也最**站不住脚**(shakiest)。2019年6月，一份为**自然保护**(conservation)组织世界自然基金会（WWF）撰写的报告**估算**(reckoned)，人们每周可能吞下相当于一张信用卡的塑料；**批评者**(critics)则说这是**高估**(overestimate)。两个月后，世界卫生组织的结论**令人安心**(reassuring)，却只是**初步的**(tentative)：依据有限的证据，按目前的含量，饮用水中的微塑料并无**明显**(apparent)风险。2025年2月，医学期刊《自然·医学》上的一项研究报告在脑**组织**(tissue)中发现了塑料，其含量后来被传成脑中有**一勺**(spoonful)塑料；另有学者对它的**研究方法**(methodology)提出了**质疑**(queried)。

**检测**(Detection)走在了认识前面；针对人体的研究规模仍小，或只是**观察性的**(observational)。说它有害**合乎情理**(plausible)，却**尚未证实**(unproven)；在**定论**(verdict)出来之前，依据更好的**测量**(measurement)来制定政策，或许比被**恐慌**(panic)左右更稳妥。
