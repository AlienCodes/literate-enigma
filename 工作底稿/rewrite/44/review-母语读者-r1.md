## 总体判断

## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】P5：“They captured a single **mouthful**, not the **emotional** **attachment** that nearly a century of **familiarity** had built. To many, the old taste carried **nostalgia**, a **sentimental** **heritage**.” 问题：第二句换个说法重复第一句。P5 加粗恰 10，删句会降到 7，无零成本改法；建议总编辑把第二句改成具体事实（如抗议者的说法）而保留三词，或接受扣分。
2. 【可改｜2｜−0.5】结尾 label 字面不成立（见上）。改法：“The tests had hidden the brand from every taster, and the brand was what **loyal** drinkers fought **fiercely** to defend.”（0 词）
3. 【可改｜2｜−0.5】P1：“Each stall doubled as **publicity**, letting **consumers** judge by **palate** alone.” 问题：“只凭味觉判断”是盲测的作用，不是“宣传”的作用，分词挂错。改法：“Each stall, letting **consumers** judge by **palate** alone, doubled as **publicity**.”（0 词）
