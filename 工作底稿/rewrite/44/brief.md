## 0 摘要
百事在全美商场设"百事挑战"盲测摊位，品尝者一般更常选百事 → 可口可乐做了近 20 万次盲测，新配方（更甜）胜过原版和百事 → 1985-04-23 宣布更换已有 99 年历史的配方 → 反弹：热线每天约 1,500 个电话（此前约 400），几乎全是抱怨；抗议团体、抢购囤货；1985 年 6 月马林斯提起集体诉讼（一周后驳回）→ 百事 CEO 给员工放假一天庆祝，认为可口可乐"认输" → 79 天后，1985-07-11 原配方以"可口可乐经典"回归；ABC 打断肥皂剧插播，两大电视网新闻头条。
- **论点**：盲测量的是一口的味道，量不到人们对一个品牌的感情。
- **规格**：5 段，**252–268 词**（上限 271），**重点词 54（下限 50），每段 ≥10，专名不加粗**。英式拼写（flavour, organised）。

## 5 结尾
1. ≤26 词；不新增事实；不含 79 / days / new / New Coke；不下判决式定论；**修辞须事实成立**。
2. **不得与 05–43 同构**：43 *Ötzi has a name, a last meal and a cause of death, while his anonymous killer has none*（X has A, B and C, while Y has none）；42 *The mirror still carries that … flaw, and the light it gathers is corrected only after leaving it*；41 *Every boy came home alive, and two rescuers were mourned*；40 *Yet his one win came from a stone whose odds…*；39 *Inky, somewhere beyond the pipe, remained wholly oblivious to the debate*；38 *The discoverer's ashes swept past the heart that bears his name*；37 *The legend had left him conspicuously unharmed*；36 *Alice's appetite was evidently shared by millions*；35 *The stigma of … had persisted for … beyond his lifetime*；34 *If so, its fame began with*；33 *The boy who had seemed to … would, decades later*；32 *The test's first beneficiary had been a … who had*；31 *had spent … at the helm*；30 *That pronouncement came almost N years after*；29 *never + 过去式*；28 *retain*；27 *lay in / The essence of*；26 *The country that*；25 *once + 过去分词 + have prompted*；24 *all along*；23 *now + 及物动词 + into*。另禁：时间状语开头、What/Yet/Every 开头、最高级、问句、冒号、分号、破折号、may/might、evidently、had left、never、remained、came from、mourned、still carries、while … has none。
3. **建议**：盲测与感情——200,000 次盲测都把杯子上的名字遮住了，而人们捍卫的恰恰是那个名字（事实成立：盲测不标品牌；抗议者要的是原版可乐）。主语可用 **the tests / every sip / the label / the name**。
