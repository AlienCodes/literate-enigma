## 新问题
1. 【该改，项4 -1】On 10 May they landed on **the island's** deserted south coast。本段里 South Georgia 没有出现过，最近提到的岛是上一段的 Elephant Island，读者会以为他们在象岛登陆，意思就错了。改法：恢复为 `South Georgia's deserted south coast`（+1 词，可用其他改动省下的词抵消）。中文「该岛」也应改回「南乔治亚岛」。
2. 【该改，项3 -1】Precision put six of them ashore within reach of help and, a century on, their ship back into view。put 同时管两个宾语，中间又隔着插入语，读者读到 their ship 要回头找动词；而且 `put … back into view` 本身不是地道搭配（应说 bring back into view）。这个轭式搭配不自然。改法：`Precision put six of them ashore within reach of help and, a century on, brought their ship back into view.`（+1 词）。
