## 逐条问题

### 项3 语言质量（-3）
1. 【该改】原文：**Battered** by **ferocious** **gales**, Worsley **snatched** only four **sightings**… 问题：分词短语的逻辑主语落在 Worsley 身上；被大风连番冲击的是船（和全体人），母语编辑会标为半悬垂修饰。改法：With the boat **battered** by **ferocious** **gales**, Worsley snatched only four…（或 As **ferocious** **gales** **battered** the boat, …）。-1
2. 【该改】原文：a **bleak**, **uninhabited** rock where no rescuer would come。问题：where no rescuer would come 生硬，且把意思说偏了（不是救援者"不会来"，是没人会想到去那里找）。改法：a **bleak**, **uninhabited** rock far from any shipping route, where no one would think to look。-1
3. 【该改】原文：a **voyage** of some 800 nautical miles …… about four miles south of Worsley's recorded position（标题 Four Miles）。问题：前文用 nautical miles，后文和标题只写 miles，读者无法判断是海里还是英里；来源（Endurance22）给的是约 4 海里（约 7.4 km）。中文译作"四英里"亦随之含糊。改法：about four nautical miles south…；中文"约四海里"；标题可保留 Four Miles 或改 Four Sights, Four Miles 并在正文统一。-1

### 项4 适配读者（-0.5）
4. 【该改】原文：On the continent's far side, the expedition's Ross Sea party lost three。问题：Ross Sea party 此前未交代，考研生不知是谁、为何在大陆另一侧。改法：…the expedition's Ross Sea party, sent to lay supply depots for the crossing, lost three men。-0.5

### 项6 用法典型（-2）
5. 【该改】原文：four **sightings** of the sun（标题 Four Sightings）。问题：航海测天的地道说法是 take a sight / sights of the sun；sighting 的典型义是"目击、看到"（UFO/whale sightings），"sightings of the sun"母语读者读来像"看见了四次太阳"。重点词学到的是非典型义。改法：Worsley took only four **sights** of the sun（重点词改为 sight(s)，速查表释义"n. 视力；景象；（航海）天体观测"）；若必须保留 sighting，改为 only four times did he even **catch sight of** the sun 类句式。-1
6. 【该改】原文：Legend rightly **salutes** Shackleton's **charisma**。问题：salute 的典型宾语是人或成就（salute the troops / their courage），且主语 Legend 与 salutes 搭配偏弱；"salute someone's charisma"语感不顺。改法：Legend rightly **celebrates** Shackleton's charisma；若保留 salutes：Legend rightly **salutes** Shackleton **for** his **charisma**。-1

### 项2 论证与结构（-0.5）
7. 【可改】第4段末 Ross Sea party 一句与"精确导航"主线无关，插在结局前稍分散；补上第4条的交代后可保留，或移到"All 28 … survived"同句作对比（…survived, unlike three of the Ross Sea party on the far side）。-0.5
