## 逐条问题

1. **该改 / 第3项 −1.5**（第1段）
   原文：The term **denoted** a **grim** **threshold**, reached when dam storage, the water in the city's **reservoirs** as a **percentage** of capacity, fell to 13.5 per cent.
   问题：插入语套插入语，"dam storage, the water … as a percentage of capacity" 读起来像名词堆，为塞 percentage 而扭曲。
   改为：The term **denoted** a **grim** **threshold**: the point at which water in the city's **reservoirs**, measured as a **percentage** of capacity, fell to 13.5 per cent.（词数 ±0）

2. **该改 / 第3项 −1.5**（第3段）
   原文：Officials **disclosed** dam levels and **household** **consumption**, making **thrift** **visible**, and publishing the **tallies** let neighbours **benchmark** their use.
   问题：分词短语后接 "and publishing…let" 主语突变，读者先把 publishing 当成与 making 并列的分词，句子"走岔"。
   改为：Officials **disclosed** dam levels and **household** **consumption**, making **thrift** **visible**; the published **tallies** let neighbours **benchmark** their use.（−1 词）

3. **该改 / 第3项 −1**（第2段）
   原文：The **countdown** turned a **chronic** **shortage** into an **imminent** date.
   问题：imminent 修饰事件/威胁，"imminent date" 搭配别扭。
   改为：The **countdown** turned a **chronic** **shortage** into an **imminent** deadline.（±0）

4. **可改 / 第3项 −0.5**（第3段）
   原文：Daily use was roughly **halved**, from about 1.2 billion litres … to about 500 million
   问题：1.2→0.5 是降了近六成，"roughly halved" 与数字不符，母语读者会停顿。
   改为：Daily use was more than **halved**, …（+1 词）

5. **该改 / 第3项 −1**（第5段）
   原文：**Townships**, poor areas **segregated** under **apartheid**, had long used **scant** water
   问题：segregated 未说按什么隔离，指代含糊；"used scant water" 生硬。
   改为：**Townships**, poor areas **segregated** by race under **apartheid**, had long got by on **scant** water（+3 词）

6. **该改 / 第3项 −1**（第5段）
   原文：The 50-litre limit was **eased** in October 2018.
   问题：夹在"批评"两句与总结句之间，打断论证节奏；建议移到第4段末（replenished 一句之后），内容不变（±0）。

7. **该改 / 第2项 −2**（结尾）
   原文：Households, farmers and winter rains together kept the collection points **idle**, while the poorest had the least to **sacrifice**.
   问题：while 分句逻辑含混——"最穷的人能牺牲的最少"读起来像在说他们付出得少，与上文"负担不均"正相反，结尾力度被削弱，指向不清。
   改为：Households, farmers and winter rains together kept the collection points **idle**, but the **sacrifice** weighed heaviest on those with the least to give.（+4 词）
   中文同步：家庭、农民和冬雨共同让取水点一直**闲置**(idle)，但这份**牺牲**(sacrifice)最沉重地压在了最拿不出东西的人身上。
