P1 Al Jazeera 2018-01-16 / VOA Learning English / The EastAfrican — Day Zero = combined dam storage below 13.5%; predicted 21 April 2018 (announced January 2018). If reached, taps off except poorest neighbourhoods; about 200 collection sites. Level 6 restrictions = 87 L/person/day (Jan 2018); later 50 L.

P2 Daily Maverick 2018-03-12; News24 2018-02-05; Engineering News 2018-02-06 — Mayor warned 18 Jan 2018 Day Zero "virtually unavoidable", likely 21 April. Groenland Water Users Association farmers released ~10 billion litres from their dam (early February 2018). 7 March 2018: Day Zero unlikely in 2018. Consumption fell from >600m to ~520m L/day (Feb 2018).

P4 Wikipedia "Cape Town water crisis"; Circle of Blue; NASA Earth Observatory — dam levels fell from 2015; crisis peaked mid-2017 to mid-2018, dams 14–29%; consumption 1,200m L/day (2015) → ~500m L/day (2018). Three years of drought (2015–2017).
