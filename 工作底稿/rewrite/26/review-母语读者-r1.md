## 逐条问题

1. **该改｜第3项 −1.5**
   原文：To avert imprisonment, he submitted to hormone injections and forfeited his security clearance.
   问题：目的状语 To avert imprisonment 同时管住两个谓语，读成"为免入狱而丢掉安全许可"，逻辑错位：失去许可是定罪的后果，不是他换取免狱的手段。
   改法：He forfeited his security clearance and, to avert imprisonment, submitted to hormone injections.（词数不变）

2. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：Exploiting them, his machines, operational from March 1940, automated the laborious search for each day's rotor settings.
   问题：分词开头加插入语，主语 machines 前后夹两个逗号块，节奏拥堵；them 的指代要回跳到 insights；"机器 exploiting 洞见"也有点别扭。
   改法：His machines exploited those insights and, operational from March 1940, automated the laborious search for each day's rotor settings.（+2 词；速查表 exploiting → exploited）

3. **可改｜第3项 −0.5**
   原文：Alan Turing was a formidable intellect by his early twenties.
   问题："was a … intellect by his early twenties" 母语者读着发紧（by 要配完成体或 already）；更自然的是 had a formidable intellect。
   改法：Born in June 1912, Alan Turing had a formidable intellect by his early twenties.（词数不变；中文改为"二十出头时，他已具备**非凡的**(formidable)**智力**(intellect)"，速查表 intellect 首义"智力"即可）

4. **可改｜第3项 −0.5**
   原文：In a paper published in Mind in October 1950, he posed…
   问题：与第1段 "In a paper read in November 1936" 句式重复；Mind 不加说明，学习者会当普通名词"头脑"读。
   改法：In the journal Mind in October 1950, he posed…（−2 词，同时交代 Mind 是期刊；排版宜斜体）

5. **该改｜第6项 −1**
   原文：An interrogator exchanges typed messages with a concealed human and an anonymous machine.
   问题：两个形容词分配不对称，暗示只有人被藏起、只有机器匿名；实际两方都既隐藏又匿名。anonymous 的典型用法（身份不公开）没体现出来。
   改法：An interrogator exchanges typed messages with two concealed, anonymous players: a human and a machine.（+1 词；players 呼应 game）

6. **该改｜第4项 −0.5**
   原文：he joined the classified effort at Bletchley Park
   问题：the classified effort 不说是什么工作，Bletchley Park 对考研生只是个地名。
   改法：he joined the classified codebreaking effort at Bletchley Park（+1 词）

7. **可改｜第4项 −0.5**
   原文：An inquest ruled it suicide
   问题：inquest 是专门词，学习者不熟，正文无提示。
   改法：An official inquest ruled it suicide（+1 词）

8. **可改｜第5项 −0.5**（供语言学家复核）slowly 属高频基础词，占名额偏弱。不在此改正文。

9. **可改｜第8项 −0.5** Recognition came slowly 译"姗姗来迟"，加了"迟到"的评价色彩，可改为"**认可**(recognition)来得**缓慢**(slowly)"或保留但知悉。

10. **配合控词（非扣分）**：The country that prosecuted him in 1952 put his face on its money → 删 in 1952（−2 词；日期上文已给，句尾更有力）。

其余：Enigma、imitation game、rotor（中文已注"转子"）交代可接受；英式拼写 apologised、licence 一致。
