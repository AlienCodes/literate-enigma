## r1 问题处理情况
1. To avert imprisonment 逻辑错位 → **已改**：语序改后逻辑清楚，节奏好。
2. Exploiting them… → **已改**："His team's machines exploited them and, operational from March 1940, automated…" 自然；them 紧接上句 insights，指代清楚；"His team's" 也更准确。
3. was a formidable intellect → **已改**：had a formidable intellect by his early twenties 是可接受的新闻体。
4. In a paper published in Mind → **未改**（见下条）。
5. concealed human / anonymous machine → **已改**："two concealed players, one human and one machine" 地道，与 game 呼应。
6. classified effort → **已改**：classified codebreaking effort。
7. inquest → **已改**：A coroner's inquest 是英式标准说法，coroner 也有学习价值。
8. slowly 太基础 → **已改**：换成 Recognition lagged。不及物的 lagged 母语者完全接受，短句收束有力。
9. 姗姗来迟 → **已改**：现为"来得迟缓"。
10. 结尾删 in 1952 → **已改**：结尾干净有力。

另核 "rooted in mathematical logic"：比 built from 更地道。
