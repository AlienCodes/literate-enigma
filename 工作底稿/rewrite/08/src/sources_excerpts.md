# 第08篇 来源摘录（2026-10-01 检索；被拦截站点见末尾）

P1 Molina & Rowland, "Stratospheric sink for chlorofluoromethanes: chlorine atom-catalysed destruction of ozone", Nature 249, 810–812, 28 June 1974（检索摘要：physics.it.com、encyclopedia.pub、NRDC/D. Doniger；"published ... in Nature in June 1974"）。
P2 Farman, Gardiner & Shanklin, "Large losses of total ozone in Antarctica reveal seasonal ClOx/NOx interaction", Nature 315, 207–210, 16 May 1985（NORA/NERC eprint 523353；historyofinformation.com；Springer Nature Communities "How one Nature paper in 1985..."；UKRI/NERC "The story behind the discovery of the ozone hole"）。地面 Dobson 分光光度计，哈雷湾（Halley Bay）春季总臭氧 1980 年代初锐减。
P3 NASA Ozone Watch "History of the ozone hole"（检索摘要，原页被拦截）："Software was designed so that data are never just thrown out. Rather, questionable data are 'flagged' as not being of best quality. One such screen was on ozone such that ozone amounts less than 180 DU were flagged as possibly being in error. This was a reasonable check since no reliable measurement of ozone this low had ever been reported before 1983." Oct 1983 数据 1984 年 8 月开始处理（Fleig、Heath、Bhartia 团队）。RealClimate 2017-12 "What did NASA know and when did they know it?"（摘要）：flagged 数据激增被注意并调查。NASA Q&A with Pawan Bhartia（climate.nasa.gov/news/781，拦截，仅标题可见）。
  → 结论：「卫星数据被程序自动删除/丢弃」是流行误传；准确说法是低值被标记为可疑（flagged），NASA 未抢先发表。慎用一切细节（180 DU、处理月份）——正文不写数字。
P4 UN Press SG/SM/12449（2009）、UNEP Ozone Secretariat、IISD SDG：16 September 2009，Timor-Leste 批准后 Vienna Convention 与 Montreal Protocol 成为联合国史上首批获普遍批准（universal ratification, 196 parties）的条约。注意：是「all 196 parties/全体国家」，表述用 every country in the UN / universal ratification；「首个」须带 Vienna Convention 同时达成——写 among the first / the first treaties 均可，不写「唯一」。
P5 Protocol 签署 16 September 1987（UNEP Ozone Secretariat 通识；国际臭氧层保护日即此日）。
P6 EPA archive "Regulatory history of CFCs"、IISD ENB：London 1990 年 6 月修正——发达国家 2000 年前淘汰 CFCs；Copenhagen 1992 提前到 1995 年底/1996。
P7 Nobel Chemistry 1995：Crutzen、Molina、Rowland 共享，"for their work in atmospheric chemistry, particularly concerning the formation and decomposition of ozone"；宣布 11 October 1995（MIT News；nobelprize.org 摘要）。
P8 2022 Scientific Assessment of Ozone Depletion（WMO/UNEP/NOAA/NASA/EC，2023 年 1 月 9 日发布；NOAA CSL 执行摘要）："return to 1980 values around 2066 in the Antarctic, around 2045 in the Arctic, and around 2040 for the near-global average (60°N–60°S)"；近 99% 消耗臭氧物质已淘汰。
