## 逐条问题（含返修新引入）

### 项3 语言质量（-2）
1. 【该改·新引入】原文：Its software flagged them as **dubious**, since none had been reliably measured.
   问题：v2 的 "readings that low… none" 被改成 "them… none" 后，none 只能回指 them（这些低值），句子变成"因为这些低值没有一个被可靠测到过"——像在说读数本身不可靠，正好和下一句 "The sensor had worked" 打架。原意是"此前从没人测到过这么低的臭氧"。
   改法：Its software flagged them as **dubious**, since ozone that low had never been reliably measured. -1
2. 【该改·新引入】原文：**Persistent** staff read it in a painstaking routine of manual observation.
   问题：persistent 放在名词前修饰人，母语读者第一反应是"缠人的、赶不走的员工"（persistent salesman/caller），不是"坚持不懈"。表语位置才自然。
   改法：The staff were **persistent**, reading it in a **painstaking** **routine** of **manual** **observation**. -1
3. 【可改，不扣】...so inert that they drift intact into the stratosphere..., where they could liberate chlorine：一般现在时 are/drift 接 could，时态略散；可写 where sunlight can break them apart to liberate chlorine，或统一 could。
4. 【可改，不扣】who trusted that a baffling number was no glitch：trusted that 稍弱；可写 who refused to dismiss a baffling number as a glitch, and published it。

### 项4 适配读者（-0.5）
5. 【该改·新引入】had plummeted each spring since the mid-1970s：删了 southern 以后，中国读者会默认 3–5 月；南极春季是 9–11 月，读者会对不上。改法：恢复 each southern spring（+1 词），或 each spring (September–November)；中文同改为"每到南半球春季"。-0.5

### 其余各项
- 史实：since the mid-1970s、NASA says 有来源且措辞稳妥；不扣。
- 中文：拆句后通顺；swift/迅速、atmosphere/大气 对应准确。速查表新增 atmosphere、swift，以及调整后的 liberate、humble 释义都准确。
