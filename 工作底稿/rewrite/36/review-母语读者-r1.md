## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“and **motherhood** followed in July 1993 with the **arrival** of a daughter.” 问题：抽象名词作主语＋followed…with the arrival，绕且像公文。改法：“and her daughter's **arrival** in July 1993 brought **motherhood**.”（−3 词）
2. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：“the **inspiration** for Harry Potter, an **orphaned** young **wizard**, came **unexpectedly** to Joanne Rowling, a **would-be** **novelist**.” 问题：主语带同位语，动词迟到，人物到句末才出现，头重。改法：“Joanne Rowling, a **would-be** **novelist**, was **unexpectedly** struck by the **inspiration** for Harry Potter, an **orphaned** young **wizard**.”（+1 词；struck 不加粗）
3. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】原文：P1 末 “Rowling then **relocated**…” 紧接 P2 首 “Rowling married…”，名字连用。改法：P2 “She married in Porto in October 1992”（0 词）
4. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“took on the manuscript, an early **endorsement**,” 同位语指代模糊（谁的认可？），略生硬；加粗占名额，接受。
5. 【可改｜3｜−0.5】“Bloomsbury paid an advance, a **sum** of 1,500 pounds.” 母语者说 an advance of £1,500；sum 占名额，接受。
6. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“give English **tuition**” 英式成立但偏正式，母语者多说 teach English；接受。
7. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“state **welfare**” 英式说 state benefits（约稿单亦写 benefits）；welfare 占名额，接受。
8. 【可改｜6｜−0.5】“500 **hardcovers**” 美式；英式 hardbacks，但 hardback 实测 ✗冷僻，不能替换，接受。
