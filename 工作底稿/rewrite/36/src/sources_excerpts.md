## Myths
- Café to keep warm / no heating: ✗ Rowling denied (S3).
- Elephant House = birthplace: ✗ overstated (S3).
- Wrote on napkins: ○ unsourced; the train idea had no pen (she said she had no working pen [ref]); she did say Hogwarts houses were jotted on an airplane sick bag (tweet) [search].
- "Rejected by 12" exact: ✓ as commonly reported (from agency); avoid claiming she personally counted unless quoting.
- "Billionaire": ○ Forbes listed her, then dropped her in 2012 (S10); don't call her a current billionaire.
