## 逐条问题

1. **该改｜第3项 −1.5**
   原文：Gangs of twenty or more dockworkers carried sacks, barrels and crates piece by piece, strenuous drudgery.
   问题：句末光秃秃的同位语 "strenuous drudgery" 挂在列举之后，没有标点提示，母语读者第一遍会以为是第四样货物（sacks, barrels, crates…, drudgery）。strenuous + drudgery 也语义叠床（drudgery 本身就苦），节奏生硬。
   改法：…carried sacks, barrels and crates aboard piece by piece — strenuous drudgery. 或 …piece by piece, in strenuous, slow-going drudgery.（最好用破折号）

2. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：Few spectators could have seen so unremarkable a cargo as a landmark.
   问题：spectators 暗示看比赛/表演，港口应是 onlookers；"see X as a landmark" + "so … a" 倒装，结构绕，读者要回读。
   改法：Few onlookers could have guessed that so unremarkable a cargo marked a turning point. 若须保留 landmark/spectators：Few spectators on the quay would have taken so unremarkable a cargo for a landmark.

3. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：In 1956 loading loose cargo cost about $5.83 a ton; the Ideal X, by the company's reckoning, about 16 cents a ton.
   问题：省略（gapping）中间插入状语，再加 "the Ideal X" 作主语（是船不是"装船"），读者要自己补 "loading the Ideal X cost"；对考研生尤其难。
   改法：…cost about $5.83 a ton; loading the Ideal X cost, by the company's reckoning, about 16 cents.

4. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：let the committee use its patents without royalties, including those on corner fittings for hoisting and locking.
   问题：including 离 patents 太远，紧贴 royalties，指代歧义（"those" 像指 royalties）；"use … without royalties" 也不如 royalty-free 自然。
   改法：let the committee use its patents, including those on corner fittings for hoisting and locking, without paying royalties.

5. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：The efficiency came at the expense of livelihoods.
   问题：定冠词 The efficiency 无先行词——前文从未用 efficiency 一词，只说了能顺畅转运；读者会问"哪种效率"。
   改法：This efficiency came at the expense of livelihoods. 或 The new efficiency came at the expense of dockers' livelihoods.

6. **可改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：A box could then transfer smoothly between ship, crane, lorry and train.
   问题：crane 是装卸工具，不是运输方式，与 ship/lorry/train 并列不对类；母语读者会卡一下。
   改法：A box could then be lifted by crane and transfer smoothly between ship, lorry and train.

7. **该改｜第2项 −1**
   原文：Yet containers were not new.
   问题：Yet 表转折，但上一句讲成本骤降，与"集装箱不新"并无对立，转折逻辑不成立。
   改法：Containers themselves were not new, though. 或 The box itself was not new.

8. **可改｜第1项 −1（易误解）**
   原文：trailer bodies detached from lorries
   问题：Ideal X 的箱子是拆掉底盘/车轮的挂车车厢，"detached from lorries"易误读成从卡车上卸下货厢（挂车本就不是卡车的一部分）；另美国场景用 lorries 有点别扭但符合英式统一，不扣。
   改法：trailer bodies detached from their wheels and chassis.

9. **该改｜第6项 −1**
   原文：London's historic docks closed successively from 1967 to 1980
   问题：successively 修饰 closed 在母语里很少见，典型是 successively + 比较级（successively higher）或 successive governments；此处自然说法是 one after another。学生学到的搭配不典型。
   改法：closed one after another between 1967 and 1980（若保留重点词，改用 successive：the docks closed in successive waves from 1967 to 1980）。

10. **可改｜第4项 −0.5**
    原文：while Felixstowe expanded.
    问题：Felixstowe 未交代，考研生不知道是英国港口，与伦敦码头的对比读不出来。
    改法：while Felixstowe, a container port on England's east coast, expanded.

11. **可改｜第7项 −0.5**
    原文：successively | adv. 相继地（successively higher/closed）
    问题："successively closed" 不是典型搭配，不宜当例。
    改法：例改 successively higher/larger；附 successive (adj.)。

12. **可改｜第9项 −0.5**
    原文：二十多人一帮的码头工人把麻袋、木桶和板条箱一件件扛上扛下，是繁重的苦差事。
    问题："……，是繁重的苦差事"缺主语，口语化断裂。
    改法：……一件件扛上扛下，这是繁重的苦差事。

13. **可改｜第10项 −0.5**
    原文：真正的障碍在于**达成共识**(consensus)
    问题：consensus 是名词"共识"，加粗落在"达成共识"把动作也算进去，对应不准。
    改法：真正的障碍在于难以达成**共识**(consensus)。

其余句子（slipped out of、Clamped to her deck、by the company's reckoning、gave way to、rewards proved substantial 等）自然地道，不扣。
