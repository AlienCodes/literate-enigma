## 逐条问题（剩余）

1. **该改｜第3项 −1**
   原文：In January 1963 Sea-Land, the Ideal X's renamed operator, let the committee…
   问题："renamed operator" 读起来像"被换掉/改名的运营方"，母语读者要停下来猜：是谁改了名、改成什么。插入语信息密度太高。
   改法：In January 1963 Sea-Land, as the Ideal X's operator was by then called, let the committee use its patents free of royalties, including those on corner fittings for hoisting.

2. **可改｜第3项 −0.5**
   原文：Containers were not new.
   问题：紧接成本对比，过于突兀，像与上文矛盾；需一个轻微的让步信号。
   改法：Containers themselves were not new.

3. **可改｜第6项 −0.5**
   原文：a box could transfer smoothly between ship, lorry and train.
   问题：transfer 作不及物动词表示"物被转运"不太典型（多用于人换乘），学生应学 be transferred。
   改法：Lifted by crane, a box could be transferred smoothly between ship, lorry and train.

其余改动句（At the time, freight travelled loose. / Much of the cargo crossed the harbour… / by a cumulative 700 per cent / Tireless cranes now lift identical boxes into orderly, immense stacks.）均地道，结尾有画面感、由正文撑住，不扣。
