# EN: Lost in the Glare

In the **misty** **cloud-forest** of Monteverde, Costa Rica, a moth **skims** over an ultraviolet lamp and flips **upside-down**, **belly** to the sky. Footage of such **acrobatics**, shot in **stereo** for a 2024 study by Imperial College London, **scotches** an **age-old** **belief**.

Insects are not **attracted** to light but **confused** by it. They follow a **rule of thumb**, keeping their backs to the brightest thing in view, which under an open sky holds them level. A lamp **hijacks** that rule, leaving them circling it or **inverted** above it. Of 477 **clips** filmed in the field, covering ten insect orders, almost none showed a flight straight at the light.

Fireflies **flirt** in light, so bright nights **smother** the **banter**. In a 2020 survey, experts ranked light pollution second only to habitat loss among threats to them, ahead of **pesticides**. At Tufts University, **suitors** under artificial light **blinked** about five patterns a minute instead of ten. Females **winked** back half as often under red or blue light, scarcely at all under white, and not once under **amber**.

The harm **spills** further. At 55 **roadside** sites in the Thames Valley, street lighting cut moth **caterpillar** numbers by 47% in **hedges** and 33% in grassy **verges**, white LEDs proving **harsher** than yellow **sodium** lamps. In seven of 14 Swiss **meadows** fitted with street lamps, **nocturnal** visits to the **blooms** fell by 62%. The plants set fewer fruit, and **daytime** insects did not **pick up the slack**.

Light pollution **blankets** the skies over more than 80% of the world's people and hides the Milky Way from over a third of **humanity**. **Citizen** scientists counting stars from 2011 to 2022 found the night sky growing about 9.6% brighter each year. A child born under 250 **naked-eye** stars would, at that **pace**, see about 100 by their eighteenth birthday.
