# EN: Lost in the Glare

In the **misty** **cloud-forest** of Monteverde, Costa Rica, a **fluttering** moth **skims** over an ultraviolet lamp and flips **upside-down**, **belly** to the sky. Filmed in **stereo** for a 2024 Imperial College London study, such **acrobatics** **scotch** an **age-old** **belief**.

Insects are not **attracted** to light but **confused** by it. By **instinct**, they keep their backs to whatever looks most **luminous**, **ordinarily** the sky, which holds them level. A lamp **hijacks** that instinct, trapping them on a **merry-go-round** or leaving them **inverted** above it. In 477 field **clips**, almost none **darted** straight at the light.

Fireflies **flirt** in light. A male **blinks** an **overture**, a female **winks** back, and bright nights **smother** the **banter**. At Tufts University, **suitors** under artificial light **mustered** about five flash patterns a minute instead of ten. Females answered half as often under red or blue light, scarcely at all under white, and not once under **amber**.

The harm **spills** into the wider **ecosystem**. At 55 **roadside** **sites** in the Thames Valley, **lamp-lit** **hedges** held 47% fewer moth **caterpillars** than unlit ones, and grassy **verges** 33% fewer, with white LEDs **harsher** than yellow **sodium** lamps. In seven of 14 Swiss **meadows** given street lamps, **nocturnal** visits to the **blooms** fell by 62%. The plants set fewer fruit, and **daytime** insects did not **pick up the slack**.

Light pollution **blankets** the skies of more than 80% of **humanity** and hides the **ribbon** of the Milky Way from over a third. **Citizen** scientists counting stars from 2011 to 2022 found the night sky growing about 9.6% brighter each year. A child born under 250 **naked-eye** stars would, at that **pace**, see about 100 by their eighteenth birthday.
