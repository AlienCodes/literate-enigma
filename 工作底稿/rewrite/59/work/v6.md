# EN: Lost in the Glare

In Costa Rica's **misty** Monteverde **cloud-forest**, a **fluttering** moth **skims** over an ultraviolet lamp and flips **upside-down**, **belly** to the sky. Filmed in **stereo** for a 2024 Imperial College study, such **acrobatics** **scotch** an **age-old** **belief**.

Insects are not **attracted** to light but **confused** by it. A **built-in** **rule of thumb** keeps their backs to whatever looks most **luminous**, **ordinarily** the sky, and so holds them level. A lamp **hijacks** that rule, leaving insects **swirling** in its **halo** or **inverted** above it. In 477 field **clips**, almost none **darted** straight at the light.

Fireflies, living **lanterns**, **flirt** in light. A male **blinks** an **overture**, a female **winks** back, and bright nights **smother** the **banter**. At Tufts University, **suitors** under artificial light **mustered** about five flash patterns a minute, not ten. Females answered half as often under red or blue light, scarcely at all under white and never under **amber**.

The harm **spills** into the **ecosystem**. At 55 **roadside** **sites** in the Thames Valley, **lamp-lit** **hedges** held 47% fewer moth **caterpillars** than unlit ones, with white LEDs **harsher** than yellow **sodium** lamps. Street lamps set up in seven of 14 Swiss **meadows** cut **nocturnal** visits to their **blooms** by 62%. The plants set fewer fruit, and **daytime** insects did not **pick up the slack**.

Light pollution **blankets** the skies of more than 80% of **humanity**, hiding the **shimmering** **ribbon** of the Milky Way from over a third. **Citizen** scientists' star counts from 2011 to 2022 show the night sky growing about 9.6% brighter a year. A child born under 250 **naked-eye** stars would, at that **pace**, see about 100 by their eighteenth birthday.
