## 逐条问题

### 项3 语言质量（-2）
1. 【该改】原文：Fleming named the substance penicillin… Yet the **crude** juice was **unstable** 问题：前文只出现 mould 与 "it"，"the substance" 无先行词，读者要倒回去猜是什么物质；随后的 "juice" 又冒出第三个说法，同一东西三种叫法。改法：Fleming called the mould's bacteria-killing "juice" penicillin and published… Yet the **crude** juice was… -1
2. 【该改】原文：In Oxford, Howard Florey, an Australian pathologist, **revived** the work… 问题：全文唯一没给时间的事件节点（按"时间要具体"规则）；"for about a decade" 之后读者不知何时重启。改法：In Oxford in 1939 → 按规则写到月：In Oxford, from late 1938 / In the autumn of 1939, Howard Florey…（以史实栏核定为准）-1
3. 【可改，不扣】only the four injected with penicillin lived：the four 未先引入，可写 only the four that had also been injected with penicillin survived。
4. 【可改，不扣】By D-Day, in June 1944 —— 逗号可删：By D-Day in June 1944。

### 项4 适配读者（-1）
5. 【该改】staphylococci：考研生多半不认识，英文无交代（中文有"葡萄球菌"）。改法：a dish of staphylococci, common bacteria → 或直接 a dish of staphylococcus bacteria。-0.5
6. 【该改】corn-steep liquor：读者会读成"玉米酒"。改法：corn-steep liquor, a cheap by-product of starch-making。-0.5

### 项6 用法典型（-1）
7. 【该改】原文：a laboratory downstairs, where moulds were **cultivated** 问题：cultivate 最该学的是 cultivate land/crops、cultivate a relationship；微生物学母语者说 grown / cultured，cultivated moulds 读着像种庄稼。改法：保留词则改搭配语境不易；建议换句：where a mycologist **cultivated** moulds for study（有人作主语，读成"培育"更自然），或换词 cultured。-1

### 未扣分备注
- 标题 The Mould That Changed the World / 改变人类命运的霉菌：响亮，中英对应，合格。
- 英式拼写与日期（mould, 25 May 1940, from holiday）全文一致；corn-steep liquor 是专名，不算美式混用。
- 时间除第2条外都写到月或日。
- 结尾 Fortune… toil 收束有力，由正文支撑；"The obstacle was now quantity." 短句节奏好。
- stubbornly hard、lie dormant、a curiosity rather than a cure、gravely ill、rallied、boosted yields、was overlooked 均地道典型。
