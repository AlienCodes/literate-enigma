## 必改
1. 【项3/6 −1】转折句 "Off eastern Australia, it broke." —— rule 作不及物 break 不地道（母语者说 a rule *is broken* / *breaks down*）。改：
   "That is the rule. Off eastern Australia, it was broken."（+1 词）
   中文不变。
2. 【项2/3 −1】结尾 "Somewhere off eastern Australia, a few outsiders once reshaped an entire population's music." —— ① 紧跟 "reluctant to define this as culture"，缺衔接，像突兀复述；② "Somewhere" 与前文已具体的东澳矛盾、显得含糊。改：
   "Whatever the label, a few outsiders off eastern Australia once reshaped an entire population's music."（+2 词）
   中文：「无论如何称呼，几位**外来者**(outsiders)曾在澳大利亚东部海域**重塑**(reshaped)了整个种群的音乐。」
