## 逐条问题（含返修新引入）

### 项3 语言质量（-2）
1. 【该改·新引入】原文：Inquiries in Washington and London heard **testimony**… That July, American **legislation**… 问题：调查删去了时间，违反"时间要具体"；"That July" 的年份只能从首段倒推，读者要回读。改法：Inquiries in Washington and London that spring heard **testimony**… In July 1912, American **legislation**…（词数紧可删 "and the Californian's crew" 中的 "the Californian's crew" 改 "witnesses"，或首段删 "1911"）。-1
2. 【该改·新引入】原文：The **outdated** rules behind her had stayed **afloat** since 1894 问题："behind her" 有歧义（"在她身后的"/"她背后的"），母语者会停顿。改法：The **outdated** rules she sailed under had stayed **afloat** since 1894. 中文"她所遵循的那套过时规则"。-1

### 项4 适配读者（-0.5）
