- **S4 2015 普查**：Current Biology 25(19), 5 October 2015, "Long-term census data reveal abundant wildlife populations at Chernobyl" https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/publications/long-term-census-data-reveal-abundant-wildlife-populations-at-che/ ；csmonitor 2015-10-11 https://m.csmonitor.com/Science/2015/1011/Wildlife-thriving-in-abandoned-Chernobyl-zone ：elk, roe deer, red deer, wild boar 相对丰度与四个未污染保护区相近；wolf >7 倍；直升机数据显示事故后 1–10 年 elk/roe deer/boar 上升；"regardless of potential radiation effects on individual animals"。（研究区为白俄罗斯 Polesie 保护区；足迹计数为冬季雪迹样线，摘要层面○。）
