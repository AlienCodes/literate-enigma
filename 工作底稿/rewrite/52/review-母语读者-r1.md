## 必改
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】P2 “The farmers' **plight** turned military.” 困境本身不会“变成军事的”。改法：“The farmers' **plight** took a military turn.”（+1 词）
2. 【该改｜3｜−1】P4 “Five weeks of **warfare** had barely dented the **hardy** flocks or their **endurance**.” 与上句 resilient／toughness 叠成四词同义，且 dent … their endurance 搭配别扭。改法：“Five weeks of **warfare** had barely dented the **hardy** flocks' **endurance**.”（−2 词）
3. 【该改｜6｜−1】P5 “This **premium**, **redeemed** per bird” premium 指保险费／溢价，bounty 不叫 premium；赏金说 claimed/paid，不说 redeemed。两词均占名额（P5 恰 10），无零成本替换，接受扣分；若语言学家可替换为 ✓ 未占用词（如 reward 若可用、paid 为基础词），再议。
4. 【该改｜3｜−0.5】P2 “sending no **battalion** but a **detachment**” 惯用 not a … but a …。改法：“sending not a **battalion** but a **detachment**”（+1 词）
