## 总体判断
- 幽默到位：英式冷幽默贯穿始终——“made each dealer **solemnly** **vow** **secrecy**, **ostensibly** to spare the public **indignation**”“his modest **civil-service** **remuneration** needed **supplementing**”“Not a **whisper** of the **deceit** appeared in print”，都是一本正经地讲骗局，好笑而不油滑。
- 结尾 “Of his two buyers, the one who paid kept **mum** and the one who kept his money went to the police.” 反讽漂亮、**事实成立**（受骗者因羞愧不报案，第二位起疑者报警），kept mum／kept his money 的对称很妙，落得住 ✓。唯一的问题是 buyers：P5 刚说“第二次出售”是讹传，第二人并没有买，称他 buyer 自相矛盾（见第 2 条）。

## 对点名词的判断
- **momentous**、**solemnly** **vow**、**ostensibly**、**high-ranking**、**script**（与 encore 同属戏剧比喻，连贯）、**much-repeated**：都自然，保留。
- swallowed the loss **quietly**：swallow the loss 是地道成语，quietly 略多余但无伤，保留。
- **world-famous** Eiffel Tower：多余（埃菲尔铁塔无须说“世界闻名”），像凑数（第 3 条）。
- **penal** island：英式说 prison island；penal island 可懂但生硬，接受（−0.5）。

## 必改

   “The **high-ranking** deputy was Victor Lustig, a **crook** whose **ministerial** stationery was a **forgery**. He had read a newspaper article in which the authorities **lamented** the **hefty** cost of the tower's **upkeep**.”（Victor Lustig→He −1，was Lustig→was Victor Lustig +1，its→the tower's +1；净 +1 词）
