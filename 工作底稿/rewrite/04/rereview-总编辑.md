## 逐条问题
1. **可改（项2，−0.5）** 原文："Yuan shared the 2004 World Food Prize … and died in Changsha … aged 91. Yet popular myth portrays him as a solitary genius." 问题：Yet 承接的是获奖与逝世，转折的支点不在这里；它真正要反驳的是第 3 段"分给全国协作者"。改法：在 Yet 前加一句短的结论，如 "Many shared the credit."；或者把 Yet 换成 "Popular myth nonetheless portrays…"。在词数上限内，也可以把 "and died" 改成 "; he died"，让获奖与逝世各自成句，削弱 Yet 的错位感。
2. **可改（项3，−0.5）** 原文："a maintainer to maintain its sterility and a restorer to restore fertility"。问题：maintainer/maintain、restorer/restore 同根反复，读来像在解释词典。改法："a maintainer that keeps it sterile and a restorer that brings back fertility"。但这样会失去重点词 maintain/restore；若要保留重点词，可写 "one line to maintain its sterility and another to restore fertility (the 'maintainer' and 'restorer')"。不改也可。
