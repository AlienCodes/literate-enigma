# EN: A Harvest of Many Hands
# ZH: 众手成丰年
## 英文

In July 1964, **inspecting** a **paddy** at Anjiang Agricultural School in Hunan province, the **instructor** Yuan Longping **glimpsed** a **peculiar** plant. It was male-**sterile**, **shedding** no working **pollen**. Rice pollinates itself, so breeding **hybrid** seed in **bulk** depends on such plants. In February 1966 he **detailed** the **anomaly** in the **journal** Chinese Science Bulletin.

The **specimen** **launched** a **hunt** it never finished: for years his **colleagues** could not **breed** a sterile line whose **offspring** **reliably** **inherited** the **trait**. The **breakthrough** came on 23 November 1970, in a **marsh** near Nanhong Farm in Sanya, on the southern island of Hainan. Yuan's assistant, Li Bihu, and Feng Keshan, a farm **technician**, found wild rice with **withered** pollen.

Yuan's **decisive** move came next: rather than **withhold** the **strain**, he **distributed** it to **collaborators** **nationwide**. Hybrids need three **distinct** lines: one sterile, one to **maintain** it, one to **restore** **fertility** in the offspring. In the south, Yan Longan's team in Jiangxi bred sterile lines and Zhang Xiancheng in Guangxi first **identified** a restorer line. In October 1973, at a national conference in Suzhou, near Shanghai, Yuan declared the three-line system complete.

Large-scale **cultivation** began in 1976, on about 140,000 **hectares**. The World Food Prize **estimates** hybrids of this **staple** **yield** up to a fifth more **grain** than **conventional** rice. By the early 2000s they **occupied** almost half of China's rice **acreage**.

Yuan, who shared the 2004 prize with Sierra Leone's Monty Jones, died in Changsha, Hunan, on 22 May 2021, aged 91. Popular **myth** **portrays** him as a **solitary** **genius**. His eye began the hunt; his **generosity** let many hands bring in the **harvest**.

## 中文

1964年7月，湖南省安江农校的**教员**(instructor)袁隆平在**察看**(inspecting)一块**稻田**(paddy)时，**瞥见**(glimpsed)一株**奇特**(peculiar)的稻子。它是雄性**不育**(sterile)株，**散**(shedding)不出有活力的**花粉**(pollen)。水稻是自花授粉的，要**大批**(bulk)培育**杂交**(hybrid)种子，就得靠这样的植株。1966年2月，他在**期刊**(journal)《科学通报》上**详细描述**(detailed)了这一**反常现象**(anomaly)。

这株**标本**(specimen)**开启**(launched)了一场**搜寻**(hunt)，自己却没能走到终点：多年里，他的**同事**(colleagues)始终**培育**(breed)不出一个不育系，能让**后代**(offspring)**稳定地**(reliably)**遗传**(inherited)这一**性状**(trait)。**突破**(breakthrough)出现在1970年11月23日，地点是南方海南岛的三亚南红农场附近的一片**沼泽**(marsh)。袁隆平的助手李必湖和农场**技术员**(technician)冯克珊在那里找到了一种野生稻，它的花粉已经**枯萎**(withered)。

接下来是袁隆平**决定性**(decisive)的一步：他没有把这个**品系**(strain)**扣在**(withhold)自己手里，而是**分给**(distributed)了**全国各地**(nationwide)的**协作者**(collaborators)。杂交稻需要三个**各不相同**(distinct)的系：一个不育，一个用来**维持**(maintain)它的不育，一个让后代**恢复**(restore)**育性**(fertility)。在南方，江西颜龙安的团队育成了不育系，广西的张先程率先**找出**(identified)了恢复系。1973年10月，在上海附近的苏州召开的全国性会议上，袁隆平宣布三系已经配套。

1976年，杂交稻开始大面积**种植**(cultivation)，面积约14万**公顷**(hectares)。据世界粮食奖**估计**(estimates)，这种**主粮**(staple)的杂交品种比**常规**(conventional)水稻最多可多**产**(yield)约五分之一的**稻谷**(grain)。到21世纪初，杂交稻已**占**(occupied)中国水稻**种植面积**(acreage)的近一半。

袁隆平曾与塞拉利昂的蒙蒂·琼斯共获2004年世界粮食奖，2021年5月22日在湖南长沙逝世，享年91岁。流行的**神话**(myth)把他**描绘**(portrays)成一个**独来独往**(solitary)的**天才**(genius)。他的一双眼睛让这场搜寻起了头；他的**慷慨**(generosity)，让许多双手一起把**收成**(harvest)收了回来。
