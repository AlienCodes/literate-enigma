## 逐条问题
1. 【该改｜3｜−1】原文：“The **breakout** went **unreported** for about three months. Made public in April 2016, it travelled the **globe**.” 问题：it 指 breakout，但在一篇讲章鱼出逃的文章里，“it travelled the globe” 先被读成“它（章鱼）周游世界”，产生误会式的笑点。改法：“Made public in April 2016, the story travelled the **globe**.”（+1 词）；同段抵消：“retrieved it from a crayfish **pot** off Napier.” → “retrieved it from a crayfish **pot**.”（−2 词；Napier 首段已交代）。P2 净 −1。
