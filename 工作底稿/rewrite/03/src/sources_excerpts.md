## N 诺贝尔奖
- N1 nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/summary（检索摘要）："for his services to Theoretical Physics, and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect"。
- N2 nobelprize.org 1921 页（检索摘要）："During the selection process in 1921, the Nobel Committee for Physics decided that none of the year's nominations met the criteria as outlined in the will of Alfred Nobel. According to the Nobel Foundation's statutes, the Nobel Prize can in such a case be reserved until the following year, and this statute was then applied."
- N3 投票日：EDN "Einstein wins 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics, November 9, 1922"；Wikipedia "Carl Wilhelm Oseen"："On 9 November 1922, the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences voted to award Albert Einstein the previously reserved 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics"。
- N4 Gullstrand：Europhysics News 2005 "Einstein and the Nobel Committee: Authority vs. Expertise"；Hektoen International 2020 "headed off at the Nobel pass by Alvar Gullstrand"；JAMA Ophthalmology "Gullstrand, Einstein, and the Nobel Prize"。1921 年总报告转述 Gullstrand 专门报告：relativity "demands a great deal of conviction in respect to phenomena, which lie entirely outside experience, it does not seem to meet the requirements..."（引语只作内部依据，正文不直接引）。
- N5 Oseen：Wikipedia "Carl Wilhelm Oseen"（检索摘要）：1922 年入委员会，提名、评审并提议以「光电效应定律」授奖。另见 Il Nuovo Saggiatore "The difficult task to award Einstein a Nobel Prize"；Pal, arXiv:2112.13519。
- N6 Aurivillius 1922-11-10 致信：奖项授予理论物理贡献、尤其光电效应定律，"without taking into account the value that will be accorded to [his] theories of relativity and gravitation"（检索摘要，引自 Annalen der Physik 2022 "The 100th Anniversary of Einstein's Nobel Prize: Facts and Fiction" 等；**慎用：只许转述，不直接引**）。
- N7 行程：乘 Kitano Maru 赴日；1922-11-17 至 12-29 在日本（检索摘要，Annalen der Physik 2025 稿件）。颁奖典礼（1922-12-10）缺席，德国驻瑞典公使代读答谢（Nadolny，名字**慎用**）。
- N8 诺贝尔演讲：nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/lecture；Einstein Papers vol.14 Doc.75；Gutenberg #69572：题为 "Fundamental Ideas and Problems of the Theory of Relativity"，1923-07-11 于哥德堡（Gothenburg）北欧自然科学家大会。
- N9 奖金与米列娃：Einstein Papers Project（einsteinpapers.press.princeton.edu vol14）；Time；The Marginalian：1919 年 2 月离婚判决约定，若他获诺奖，奖金（扣 4 万马克）存入瑞士银行归米列娃，利息归她支配、动用本金需他同意。

## E 日食与公布
- E1 1919-05-29 日全食；Eddington 在普林西比（Príncipe，几内亚湾）；Crommelin 与 Davidson 在巴西索布拉尔（Sobral，Ceará）；Dyson（皇家天文学家）统筹。来源：arXiv:1912.07674；Science Friday；FAPESP Pesquisa。
- E2 1919-11-06 皇家学会与皇家天文学会联席会议（Burlington House），J. J. Thomson 主持。来源同上；Discover。
- E3 《泰晤士报》1919-11-07："Revolution in Science / New Theory of the Universe / Newtonian Ideas Overthrown"（Science Friday；BJHS 文章题即 "Constructing a revolution in science"）。
- E4 《纽约时报》1919-11-10："Lights All Askew in the Heavens"（rarenewspapers.com 原报；ProQuest 100454918；Time）。
- E5 数据：Sobral 小望远镜 1.98″±0.16″，Príncipe 1.61″±0.40″，爱因斯坦预言 1.75″，牛顿式 0.85″；Sobral 大的天体照相望远镜数据因对焦问题被弃（arXiv:2010.13744；arXiv:1409.7812）。
- E6 争议分寸：1980 Earman & Glymour 指偏向；1979 皇家格林尼治天文台用 Zeiss Ascorecord 重测 Sobral 底片，结果与原值基本相同；Kennefick（Physics Today 2009 "Testing relativity from the 1919 eclipse—a question of bias"；Scientific American）认为无可信偏向证据，弃用决定在格林尼治、由 Dyson 主导。
## M 误传
- M1 Time "Did Einstein flunk math?"：1935 年 Ripley's 专栏称其数学不及格；他答 "I never failed in mathematics"。1895 年报考苏黎世联邦理工，数理优异、语言历史等不足，总评不合格。
