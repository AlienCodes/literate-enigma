# EN: The Prize That Looked Away
# ZH: 移开视线的奖
## 英文

On 29 May 1919, as a total **eclipse** **obscured** the sun, two British **expeditions** photographed stars in its apparent **vicinity**. Arthur Eddington **observed** from Príncipe, an island off West Africa; Crommelin and Davidson from Sobral, in north-eastern Brazil. They were testing general relativity's **bold** prediction that the sun's **gravity** would **deflect** starlight.

On 6 November, at a **joint** meeting of the Royal Society and the Royal Astronomical Society, the **findings** were **hailed** as confirming it. The Times **proclaimed** a "Revolution in Science"; a New York Times **headline** read "Lights All Askew in the Heavens". **Overnight**, Einstein, **eminent** among **physicists**, became a global **celebrity**.

Stockholm proved harder to **dazzle**. The physics committee valued **rigorous** **experimental** **confirmation**, and its member Allvar Gullstrand, a Nobel-winning eye **specialist**, **deemed** the **empirical** support for relativity **insufficient**. Since no **nomination** **fulfilled** Nobel's **criteria**, the 1921 prize was **reserved** for a year, as the **statutes** **permit**.

On 9 November 1922 the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences **conferred** it on Einstein, **citing** his services to theoretical physics and especially his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect. That law, which **governs** how light **ejects** **electrons** from metal, helped **underpin** **quantum** theory. A letter the following day **clarified** that the prize took no account of relativity. Einstein, then **aboard** a **steamer** **bound for** Japan, would miss the December **ceremony**.

On 11 July 1923, in Gothenburg, Sweden, he **delivered** his Nobel **lecture** before a **congress** of Nordic scientists, **devoting** it to the **fundamental** ideas and problems of relativity. The theory the committee had looked past reached a Nobel **podium** after all, carried there by the man the prize had **honoured**.

## 中文

1919年5月29日，一次全**日食**(eclipse)**遮蔽**(obscured)了太阳，两支英国**考察队**(expeditions)拍摄了天空中看似位于太阳**附近**(vicinity)的恒星。阿瑟·爱丁顿在西非外海的普林西比岛**观测**(observed)；克罗姆林和戴维森则在巴西东北部的索布拉尔。他们要检验广义相对论的一个**大胆**(bold)预言：太阳的**引力**(gravity)会使经过它附近的星光**偏折**(deflect)。

11月6日，英国皇家学会与皇家天文学会召开**联席**(joint)会议，会上**盛赞**(hailed)观测**结果**(findings)证实了这一预言。《泰晤士报》**宣告**(proclaimed)了一场“科学革命”；《纽约时报》的**标题**(headline)写着“天上的光全歪了”。**一夜之间**(overnight)，在**物理学家**(physicists)中**声望卓著**(eminent)的爱因斯坦成了举世皆知的**名人**(celebrity)。

斯德哥尔摩却没那么容易被**眩惑**(dazzle)。物理学委员会看重的是**严格的**(rigorous)**实验**(experimental)**证实**(confirmation)。委员阿尔瓦·古尔斯特兰德是眼科**专家**(specialist)，本人也是诺贝尔奖得主，他**认定**(deemed)相对论的**实证**(empirical)支持**不足**(insufficient)。由于没有一项**提名**(nomination)**符合**(fulfilled)诺贝尔定下的**标准**(criteria)，1921年的奖便依照**章程**(statutes)**允许**(permit)的办法**保留**(reserved)一年。

1922年11月9日，瑞典皇家科学院把这个奖**授予**(conferred)爱因斯坦，**表彰**(citing)他对理论物理学的贡献，尤其是他发现了光电效应定律。这条定律**决定**(governs)了光如何把**电子**(electrons)从金属中**打出**(ejects)，曾帮助**奠定**(underpin)**量子**(quantum)理论的基础。第二天的一封信**说明**(clarified)，此奖并未考虑相对论。爱因斯坦当时正**乘**(aboard)一艘**开往**(bound for)日本的**轮船**(steamer)，将错过12月的颁奖**典礼**(ceremony)。

1923年7月11日，他在瑞典哥德堡向北欧自然科学家**大会**(congress)**发表**(delivered)诺贝尔**演讲**(lecture)，**专门**(devoting)讲相对论的**基本**(fundamental)思想与问题。委员会绕开不看的那个理论，终究登上了诺贝尔的**讲台**(podium)，把它带上去的，正是这个奖所**嘉奖**(honoured)的人。
