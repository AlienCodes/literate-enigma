## 逐条问题

1. **该改（项1，−1）返修引入** 原文："Einstein, aboard a steamer bound for Japan, missed the December ceremony." 问题：删掉 "then" 后，"aboard" 修饰的是 missed 的时刻，读成 12 月典礼时他还在船上；实际 11-17 已到日本，12 月在日本巡讲。中文"当时乘……"承接上句来信，时间尚可，但英文不对。改法："Einstein, then aboard a steamer bound for Japan, would miss the December ceremony."（恢复 then/would，+2 词）；或 "The news reached Einstein aboard a steamer bound for Japan; he missed the December ceremony."
2. **可改（项4，−0.5）** 原文："a joint meeting of the Royal and Royal Astronomical Societies"。问题：省略式对考研读者费解，易读成笔误。改法："of the Royal Society and the Royal Astronomical Society"（+2 词；可用删 "Almost" 外其他虚词抵消）。
3. **可改（项3，−0.5）** 原文："he delivered his Nobel lecture, devoted to the fundamental ideas and problems of relativity, before a congress of Nordic scientists." 问题：长插入语把 "delivered … before a congress" 拆开，地点状语悬在句尾。改法："he delivered his Nobel lecture before a congress of Nordic scientists, devoting it to the fundamental ideas and problems of relativity."（devoted→devoting 属同词形态，速查表词头随改）。
4. **可改（项9，−0.5）** "晃花眼"偏口语，与全文书面语体略不协。改法："斯德哥尔摩却没那么容易被**炫目**(dazzle)的光环征服"或"……被**眩惑**"。
