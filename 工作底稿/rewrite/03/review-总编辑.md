## 逐条问题

1. **该改（项1，−1）** 原文："citing chiefly 'his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect'"。问题：官方理由主干是 "for his services to Theoretical Physics"，光电效应是 "especially"。只写 chiefly + 后半句，读者会以为理由仅是光电效应，把"表彰理论物理贡献"这层（恰是对相对论留的余地）抹掉了；且 chiefly≠especially。改法："citing 'his services to Theoretical Physics, and especially ... the photoelectric effect'"；若保留词数，改为 "citing his services to theoretical physics and especially 'his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect'"（chiefly 可移作他用或换词）。
2. **该改（项1，−1）** 原文："A letter the following day clarified that relativity had not been evaluated." 问题：Aurivillius 信的意思是授奖"不考虑"相对论（及引力理论）未来可能获得的证实，即"without taking into account"，不是"未经评审"——相对论此前已被委员会评审过（古尔斯特兰德报告正是评审），本稿第3段自己也写了。前后自相抵触。改法："...clarified that the prize took no account of relativity." 中文："说明此奖并未考虑相对论"。
3. **可改（项2，−1）** 原文："Overnight, the eminent physicist became a global celebrity." 问题：第2段未出现 Einstein 之名，"the eminent physicist" 回指第1段所有格 "Einstein's"，且两国报纸刊登相隔三天，"Overnight"与"The next day…on 10 November"并置略显夸张。改法："Almost overnight, Einstein became a global celebrity."（eminent/physicist 可移入第1段：the eminent physicist Albert Einstein's bold prediction）。
4. **可改（项4，−0.5）** 原文："conferred that prize on Einstein"。问题：考研读者未必领会 "that prize" 即保留下来的 1921 年奖，以为是 1922 年奖。改法："conferred the reserved 1921 prize on Einstein"。
5. **可改（项3，−0.5）** 原文："in Gothenburg, a Swedish city, he delivered his Nobel lecture"。问题：同位语 "a Swedish city" 生硬；且未交代场合（北欧自然科学家大会），"Nobel podium" 一说因此略虚。改法："in Gothenburg, before a congress of Scandinavian scientists, he delivered..."。
6. **可改（项8/10，各 −0.5）** "citing → 列举" 与速查表首义"引用"不完全对应，且与问题1联动；"Stockholm proved harder to dazzle → 打动" 丢了"令人目眩"的意象，可作"斯德哥尔摩却没那么容易被**震住**"。
