# EN: The Doctor Who Drank Bacteria to Win an Argument
# ZH: 喝下细菌的医生

## 英文
In 1984 Barry Marshall, a 32-year-old doctor at Fremantle Hospital, drank a **brew** of beef **broth** into which the **microbial** growth of two Petri dishes had been **scraped**.

The **prevalent** **doctrine** blamed ulcers on stress, **spicy** food and excess **acid** **secretion**. No **bacterium**, the **profession** **firmly** held, could **withstand** so **caustic** and **hostile** a **habitat**.

Yet Robin Warren, a pathologist at Royal Perth Hospital, had seen curved, **coiled** bacteria in **gastric** **biopsies** from patients with gastritis. Marshall began to **collaborate** with him. Attempts to culture the **germ** were **invariably** **unsuccessful** until Easter 1982, when plates sat **undisturbed** in the incubator for about five days. They were **ordinarily** **binned** after two. Colonies **blossomed**. The organism was later named Helicobacter pylori.

In 1983 **unimpressed** **assessors** at the Gastroenterological Society of Australia **relegated** the pair's paper to the bottom tenth of that year's **submissions**. Efforts to infect **swine** were **likewise** **in vain**. Marshall made himself the **guinea pig**.

Within days came the **onset** of **vomiting**, a **disagreeable** **odour** on Marshall's breath and **weariness**. About ten days later an endoscopy revealed gastritis, an **inflammation** of the stomach's **mucous membrane** and a **precursor** to ulcers, **albeit** not an ulcer itself. The germ was cultured **directly** from his stomach.

**Spurred** on by an **insistent** **spouse**, Marshall took **antibiotics**, which cleared the infection. Ulcers came to be treated with such **medication**.

In 2005 Marshall and Warren shared the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering Helicobacter pylori and its role in gastritis and peptic ulcer disease. Gastritis, the illness Marshall had **willingly** given himself, is named in the prize citation.

## 中文

1984年，弗里曼特尔医院32岁的医生巴里·马歇尔喝下了一杯**汤剂**(brew)。那是一份牛肉**清汤**(broth)，两个培养皿里长出的**微生物**(microbial)菌落已被**刮进**(scraped)其中。

当时**流行**(prevalent)的**学说**(doctrine)把溃疡归咎于压力、**辛辣**(spicy)食物以及**胃酸**(acid)过量**分泌**(secretion)。**医学界**(profession)一直**坚定**(firmly)地认为，没有哪种**细菌**(bacterium)能**经受**(withstand)得住如此具有**腐蚀性**(caustic)、如此**充满敌意**(hostile)的**栖息环境**(habitat)。

然而，皇家珀斯医院的病理学家罗宾·沃伦，已经在胃炎病人**胃部**(gastric)的**活检**(biopsies)样本中，看到了弯曲**盘绕**(coiled)的细菌。马歇尔开始与他**合作**(collaborate)。培养这种**病菌**(germ)的尝试**一律**(invariably)以**失败**(unsuccessful)告终，直到1982年复活节，培养皿**原封不动**(undisturbed)地在培养箱里放了约五天。这些培养皿**平常**(ordinarily)两天后就会被**扔掉**(binned)。菌落**蓬勃生长**(blossomed)起来。这种微生物后来被命名为幽门螺杆菌。

1983年，澳大利亚胃肠病学会那些**不以为然**(unimpressed)的**评审**(assessors)，把两人的论文**归入**(relegated)当年**投稿**(submissions)中最差的10%。让**猪只**(swine)感染的尝试也**同样**(likewise)是**徒劳**(in vain)。马歇尔便把自己当成了**小白鼠**(guinea pig)。

几天之内，马歇尔便**开始**(onset)出现**呕吐**(vomiting)，呼吸带着**难闻**(disagreeable)的**气味**(odour)，人也感到**疲倦**(weariness)。约十天后，胃镜检查发现了胃炎，也就是胃**黏膜**(mucous membrane)发生**炎症**(inflammation)，这是溃疡的**前兆**(precursor)，**尽管**(albeit)本身还不是溃疡。从他的胃里**直接**(directly)培养出了这种细菌。

在态度**坚决**(insistent)的**妻子**(spouse)一再**催促**(spurred)下，马歇尔服用了**抗生素**(antibiotics)，清除了感染。后来，溃疡开始用这类**药物**(medication)治疗。

2005年，马歇尔与沃伦因发现幽门螺杆菌及其在胃炎和消化性溃疡中的作用，共同获得诺贝尔生理学或医学奖。马歇尔当年**心甘情愿**(willingly)让自己染上的胃炎，就写在获奖评语里。

- 【T3】主流观点认为溃疡由压力、辛辣食物和过多胃酸引起，细菌被认为无法在胃酸中存活（英文以 profession firmly held、caustic and hostile habitat 表述）；1983年他们向澳大利亚胃肠病学会投稿，被评为当年最差的10%。"不以为然的评审"由这一评分推出。S2
