# EN: The Doctor Who Drank Bacteria to Win an Argument
# ZH: 喝下细菌的医生

## 英文

In 1984 Barry Marshall, a **thirty-two-year-old** doctor at Fremantle Hospital, **scraped** two Petri dishes of **microbial** growth into beef **broth** and drank the **brew**. He was **willingly** swallowing the **germ** he had **indicted** for causing ulcers.

The **prevalent** **doctrine** blamed stress, **spicy** **cuisine** and **acid** **overload**, and held that no bacterium could **withstand** the stomach's **hostile**, **caustic** **digestive** **secretions**.

The **heresy** was **kindled** at Royal Perth Hospital, where the **pathology** specialist Robin Warren had seen **curly** bacteria in **gastric** tissue from gastritis patients. Marshall, a young doctor, began to **collaborate** with him. Growing the organism proved **invariably** **unsuccessful** until Easter 1982, when dishes sat **undisturbed** for about five days instead of two and colonies **blossomed**. The microbe was later named Helicobacter pylori.

**Doubters** were **unconvinced**. In 1983 the pair's paper for the Gastroenterological Society of Australia was **relegated** to the bottom ten per cent of that year's **submissions**. Attempts to infect **swine** also **miscarried**, and Marshall became the **guinea** pig.

Within days he was **vomiting**, his breath had a **pungent** **odour** and he felt **fatigued**. About ten days later a **tube** passed into his **gut** made the damage **evident**. It was gastritis, an **inflammation** of the stomach's **mucous** lining and a **prelude** to ulcers, not an ulcer, and the germ was grown from his stomach. His **insistent** **spouse** had him take an **antibiotic**, which **purged** the infection. Ulcers came to be treated with antibiotics.

In 2005 Marshall and Warren shared the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering the **bacterium** and its role in gastritis and ulcer disease.

The experiment's only patient was the author of the **contention** it tested.

## 中文

1984年，弗里曼特尔医院一位**三十二岁**(thirty-two-year-old)的医生巴里·马歇尔，把两个培养皿上的**微生物**(microbial)菌落**刮下**(scraped)，放进牛肉**清汤**(broth)，喝下了这碗**汤剂**(brew)。他是在**心甘情愿**(willingly)地吞下那种被他**指认**(indicted)为溃疡元凶的**病菌**(germ)。

当时**盛行**(prevalent)的**学说**(doctrine)把溃疡归咎于压力、**辛辣**(spicy)的**饮食**(cuisine)和**胃酸**(acid)**过量**(overload)，并认为没有哪种细菌**经受得住**(withstand)胃里那些**险恶**(hostile)而有**腐蚀性**(caustic)的**消化**(digestive)液**分泌物**(secretions)。

这个**异端**(heresy)想法在皇家珀斯医院**萌生**(kindled)。**病理学**(pathology)专家罗宾·沃伦在胃炎患者的**胃部**(gastric)组织中看到了**弯曲**(curly)的细菌。年轻医生马歇尔开始与他**合作**(collaborate)。培养这种微生物的尝试**一律**(invariably)以**失败**(unsuccessful)告终，直到1982年复活节，培养皿**无人打扰**(undisturbed)地放了约五天，而不是通常的两天，菌落终于**蓬勃长出**(blossomed)。这种微生物后来被命名为幽门螺杆菌。

**怀疑者**(doubters)并不**信服**(unconvinced)。1983年，两人投给澳大利亚胃肠病学会的论文被**归入**(relegated)当年**投稿**(submissions)中最后的百分之十。让**猪**(swine)感染的尝试也**落空**(miscarried)了，于是马歇尔当起了**实验品**(guinea)。

几天之内，他开始**呕吐**(vomiting)，口中有**刺鼻**(pungent)的**气味**(odour)，并感到**疲惫**(fatigued)。大约十天后，一根探入他**胃肠**(gut)的**软管**(tube)让损伤**显露**(evident)出来。那是胃炎，即胃部**黏膜**(mucous)层的**炎症**(inflammation)，是溃疡的**前奏**(prelude)，而不是溃疡；病菌也从他胃里培养了出来。他那**执意坚持**(insistent)的**妻子**(spouse)让他服下**抗生素**(antibiotic)，**清除**(purged)了感染。后来，溃疡开始用抗生素治疗。

2005年，马歇尔和沃伦因发现这种**细菌**(bacterium)及其在胃炎和溃疡病中的作用，共同获得诺贝尔生理学或医学奖。

这场实验唯一的病人，正是它所检验的那个**论点**(contention)的提出者。

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】皇家珀斯医院病理学家罗宾·沃伦在胃炎患者的胃活检样本中看到弯曲的螺旋状细菌；年轻医生巴里·马歇尔加入研究。英文 pathology specialist 即病理学家，gastric tissue 指胃活检组织，collaborate 表述"加入"。S1（2005 年诺贝尔颁奖词 / 西澳大学）
- 【T2】培养一再失败；1982 年复活节，培养皿放了约五天（平时两天），长出菌落，后命名为幽门螺杆菌。英文 undisturbed 表述"放着没动"。S1
- 【T3】主流观点认为溃疡由压力、辛辣食物和过多胃酸引起，细菌被认为无法在胃酸中存活；1983 年投给澳大利亚胃肠病学会的论文被评为当年投稿最差的 10%。英文 Doubters were unconvinced 是由此概括。S2（Wikipedia: Barry Marshall / Discover）
- 【T4】1984 年，感染小猪失败后，32 岁的马歇尔（弗里曼特尔医院）把两个培养皿的细菌刮进牛肉汤喝下；几天内呕吐、口臭、疲惫；约十天后胃镜（英文 a tube passed into his gut）显示胃炎，即胃黏膜发炎、溃疡的前兆，并从胃中培养出该菌。他得的是胃炎，不是溃疡。S3（Wikipedia / Lasker / Discover）
