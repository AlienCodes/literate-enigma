| # | 位置 | 原文 → 改后 | 理由 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 | eight captured in Alberta… were hauled there in crates, six more a week later → eight **wolves** captured… in crates; six more **followed** a week later | 原句 eight 后省了中心词，six more 是悬空短语；首次出现时把名词交代清楚 |
| 2 | P1 | they then roamed → they roamed | 前面已有 until late March，then 重复，删掉省出 1 词 |
| 3 | P2 | Ecologists call this a trophic cascade → call this **chain** | 避免无所指的 this；中文同步改为「这种连锁效应」 |
| 4 | P4 | the deeper flaw was water → the deeper flaw **concerned** water | 原句把缺陷等同于水，主语和表语逻辑错位 |
| 5 | P4 | streams eroded after beavers left had cut too deep → streams, eroded since beavers left, had cut too deep | 原句会被误读成 streams eroded 作谓语（花园路径句）；改成插入语后就清楚了 |
| 6 | P5 结尾 | The rivers proved messier than the fable… than any tale of wolves moving rivers → **The wolves' shadow over the rivers proved fainter and messier than the fable.** … more trust than any tale. | 用 shadow 呼应新标题，并去掉旧标题的回扣。fainter 有正文支撑：2025 年反驳称级联「较弱」但仍存在，Brice 2022 的随机抽样也显示效应较弱。没有说狼毫无作用，未越出核准史实。中文改为「狼投在河流上的影子，原来比那则寓言淡得多，也乱得多」 |
| 7 | 中文 | 不加粗的 story → 「这套说法」，不加粗的 tale → 「传说」 | 「故事」只留给加粗的 narrative，三词中文不再撞车 |
