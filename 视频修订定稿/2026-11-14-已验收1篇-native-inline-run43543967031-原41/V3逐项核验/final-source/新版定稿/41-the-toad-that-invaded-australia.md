# EN: The Toad That Invaded Australia
# ZH: 入侵澳洲的毒蟾
## 英文
Greyback cane **beetles**, whose **larvae** **devour** roots, were **plaguing** **lucrative** sugar **plantations** in Queensland, north-eastern Australia. In June 1935 Queensland **imported** 102 cane toads, **bulky** **amphibians** **native to** the Americas, from Hawaii. That August about 2,400 of their young were **released**. **Biological** control, setting one **species** against a **pest**, had an **ambitious** new **recruit**.

The **hasty** plan met **opposition**. In November 1935 an insect expert, Walter Froggatt, **persuaded** officials to **halt** releases; after Queensland's **lobbying**, they **resumed** in September 1936. The **scheme** **flopped**: the toads barely **dented** beetle numbers, perhaps because the insects stayed high up on cane **stalks**.

The toads **thrived** instead. With few **foes** and **fertile** females **spawning** thousands of eggs, they **multiplied** **unchecked**. Their **invasion** **frontier**, creeping about ten kilometres a year at first, **accelerated** to about 50 a year in the **tropical** north. In February 2009 the first known toad **breached** Western Australia's border.

**Toxin** **glands** in their skin make them a deadly meal. Numbers of goannas (large **lizards**) and northern quolls (small **marsupial** hunters) have **slumped** in places; some local quoll populations were **wiped out**. The toads have **wreaked** **havoc**, but the damage is **uneven**; some species have **prospered**.

Biological control can **triumph**. From 1926 the cactus **moth**, Cactoblastis, cleared most **prickly** pear, an **invasive** **cactus**, from Queensland and New South Wales within about a decade. Tested first, its **larvae** **starved** rather than eat fifteen other plants; the toads faced no such test. Under an October 1984 law, recruits now face **stringent** **scrutiny**. The **blunder** was born of **haste** and sealed by **lobbying** that **overrode** a warning: an **untested** **ally**, once **loose**, may never be **recalled**.

- 【T2/T3】1935年6月，Reginald Mungomery（制糖业科学家，正文不点名）在夏威夷捉得102只甘蔗蟾蜍，6月22日运抵 Gordonvale 附近的 Meringa 试验站，仅1只途中死亡；8月19日放归约2,400只后代。PestSmart：https://pestsmart.org.au/toolkit-resource/how-did-the-cane-toad-arrive-in-australia ；Australian Geographic（2023年3月）：https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/history-culture/2023/03/defining-moments-in-australian-history-introduction-of-cane-toads/ 。两站均被代理拦截，只核到搜索摘要。不用“3,000只”。
- 【T4/T5·慎用】1935年11月，退休的新州政府昆虫学家 Walter Froggatt 游说联邦卫生官员，放归被叫停；昆州州长（premier）Forgan Smith 向总理 Lyons 施压后，1936年9月恢复。多源转述（含 Wikipedia），NMA 被拦截，只核到摘要；正文以“昆士兰游说”泛述，不写引语（Froggatt “as great a pest as the rabbit” 为二手转引，不用）。
- 【T6·慎用】蟾蜍未能显著减少甲虫：Tweed fact sheet（同上）。“甲虫在茎秆高处、蟾蜍够不着”只是通俗解释，原始研究未直核，正文只写 perhaps because。
- 【T7·慎用】天敌少、一次产卵数千：fact sheet 单源（“数万”不写）。
- 【T8】入侵前锋早年约每年10公里，进入北领地热带后约每年50–55公里：Phillips, Shine 等，Austral Ecology 32:169–176（2007）。https://wiki.pestinfo.org/wiki/Austral_Ecology_(2007)_32,_169-176
- 【T9】2009年2月27日，检疫官员在 Kununurra 以东约40公里的西澳–北领地边界发现首只已知越境蟾蜍：西澳政府2009年2月28日新闻稿 https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Barnett%20Liberal%20National%20Government/Cane-toads-arrive-in-Western-Australia-20090228 。正文写 first known，不写“占领金伯利”。
- 【T10/T11】皮肤毒腺使吞食者中毒；巨蜥、淡水鳄、北袋鼬在部分地区骤减，局部袋鼬种群消失；影响因地而异，部分物种反而受益：Shine, Quarterly Review of Biology 85(3)（2010）。Macquarie 大学页面：https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/the-ecological-impact-of-invasive-cane-toads-ibufo-marinusi-in-au/ （被拦截，只核到摘要）。不写种群总数（“2亿只”来源不明，不用）。
- 【T12】2010年6月，O'Donnell, Webb & Shine 在 Journal of Applied Ecology 47(3):558–565 报告：幼年北袋鼬吃了掺致呕药 thiabendazole 的蟾蜍肉后学会回避蟾蜍，放归后存活更好。UTS：https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/handle/10453/32477 。不混入后来的“香肠”项目。
- 【T13】仙人掌螟 Cactoblastis 1926年起释放，约十年内清除昆州、新州大部分刺梨：CSIROpedia https://csiropedia.csiro.au/Prickly-pear-control 。释放跨1926–27年，正文写“From 1926 … within about a decade”（主编裁定：只写年份须配相对时间）。
- 【T14·后半句慎用】Biological Control Act 1984（No. 139，1984年10月25日 as made），正文写 passed，不写生效：https://legislation.gov.au/C2004A03003/asmade/1984-10-25/text/original/pdf 。“如今须经严格审查才能放归”为通行说法，具体流程与时长未直核，正文不写年限、不配机构（主编裁定删去 years of）。
- 【T13补】仙人掌螟放出前做过饥饿试验：初孵幼虫分别置于无花果、桉树、甘蔗、香蕉、棉花、玉米等15种植物上，每组20只，48小时内全部死亡且未取食；对照组在仙人掌上正常取食发育，委员会据此建议批准野外释放。NAA：https://www.naa.gov.au/learn/learning-resources/learning-resource-themes/environment-and-nature/plants/use-insects-destroy-prickly-pear 。正文写 offered fifteen other plants and starved rather than eat them；蟾蜍"no such trial"为对照泛述（放归前无寄主/效果检验，T4–T6 一致）。
