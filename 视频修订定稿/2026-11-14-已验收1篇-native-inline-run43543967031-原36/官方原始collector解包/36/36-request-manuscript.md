# EN: The Company That Invented Its Own Killer
# ZH: 发明了数码相机，却被它打败

## 英文
In 1975 Kodak engineer Steven Sasson built the first **digital camera**. The **toaster**-sized **gadget**, a **cumbersome** **heavyweight** of about eight pounds, was hardly **portable**, **let alone** **handheld**. Its first **rudimentary** picture held 10,000 black-and-white **pixels**. Recording it on a **cassette** took 23 seconds, and a **playback** unit showed it on a television.

Kodak's **revenues** showed its **dependence on** film, the **profitable** **pillar** of its business. Technical **staffers** were **appreciative of** the camera, but it needed no film. By Sasson's **recollection**, his **superiors** at **headquarters** called it cute and urged him to **keep it hushed**. A **patent** followed in 1978. Production never did.

The **advent** of digital photography proved **disruptive**. In June 2009 Kodak said it would stop making Kodachrome, its **analogue** film, citing **lessened** demand in the digital **era**. Kodak's **manpower** had reached an **apex** of about 145,000 in the 1980s. By 2012, **layoffs** since 2003 had **slashed** 47,000 jobs, alongside the **shutdown** of 13 factories and some 130 labs.

On 19 January 2012 the 131-year-old firm sought Chapter 11 protection from its **creditors**, **sapped** by **pension** costs and years of **slowness** in going digital. Citigroup, a **lender**, provided $950 million for its **restructuring**. A **portfolio** of 1,100 digital imaging patents fetched about $525 million from a **consortium** of twelve **buyers**, including the **giants** Apple and Google.

After 19 months of **reorganisation**, the **once-mighty** Kodak left bankruptcy in September 2013, **downsized**, **streamlined**, and **refocused as** a commercial **printer** for business **clients**. In November 2009 President Obama had **decorated** Sasson with national **laurels**, **commending** his digital camera. That **medal**'s year also saw Kodak announce **the end of** Kodachrome.

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】1975年，柯达工程师史蒂文·萨松在纽约州罗切斯特造出第一台数码相机，重约8磅，烤面包机大小；1975年12月拍下第一张照片（拍摄对象为技术员乔伊·马歇尔，正文不写），黑白，100×100像素即1万像素；录到盒式磁带上用时23秒，经回放装置在电视上显示。萨松当时年龄不写。S1
- 【T2】技术人员喜欢它；它不用胶卷；管理层的反应大意是"挺可爱，但别声张"，正文与中文均只转述；柯达的生意依赖卖胶卷；1978年获美国专利；从未投产。"收入显示出依赖"是对业务结构的概括；"让他把这件事捂住"转述"别声张"。S2
- 【T3】2012年1月19日，有131年历史的柯达申请第11章破产保护，原因是养老金负担和多年迟迟不转向数码；员工约1.9万人，80年代顶峰约14.5万；自2003年起裁员4.7万、关闭13家工厂和约130个冲印室；花旗提供9.5亿美元融资。英文以 By 2012 标明统计截至2012年申请破产时。S3
- 【T4】2012年12月，约1,100项数字成像专利以约5.25亿美元出售或授权给12家公司组成的集团，包括苹果、谷歌、三星、微软、脸书和亚马逊；正文只写苹果和谷歌，交易月份不写。S4
- 【T5】2009年6月柯达宣布停产 Kodachrome，理由是数码时代需求下降；冲印于2010年12月结束（正文不写）。S5
- 【T6】2009年11月17日，奥巴马总统在白宫向萨松颁发国家技术与创新奖章，表彰他发明数码相机。英文写作 national laurels，中文按主编要求补出奖章全称。S6
- 【T7】2013年9月3日，柯达经过19个月走出破产，规模缩小，专注商业印刷，已剥离相机、消费胶卷和照片冲印业务。S7
- 【补】结尾依据：2009年6月柯达宣布停产 Kodachrome，同年11月萨松获奖章，"同一年"成立。
