# EN: A Change of Anthem in the Deep
# ZH: 鲸歌易帜
## 英文

Each winter, male humpback whales, and only males, fill their **breeding grounds** with a **haunting** **melody**. Sound units **constitute** phrases, and phrases build themes in an **elaborate** **hierarchy**. Each **rendition** lasts minutes, and the song can **recur** for hours, probably for **courtship**. All the males in a population sing an **identical** version, which **evolves** **subtly** each year.

That is the rule. Off eastern Australia, it was broken. From 1995 to 1998, **researchers** used hydrophones, or underwater **microphones**, to record humpbacks on their **seasonal** **migration** along the **coastline**. In the mid-1990s, **merely** two of 82 singers **emitted** a **distinctive** new song, **utterly** unlike the **reigning** **chorus**.

Within about two years, **virtually** every singer had **converted**, and by 1998 the old song had been **displaced** **wholesale**. A study published in Nature in November 2000 found that the new song **resembled** that of the Indian Ocean population off western Australia, apparently **ferried** east by a **handful** of visiting **performers**.

Nor was it a **freak**. In April 2011, a study in Current Biology **analysed** over a decade of **recordings** from six South Pacific populations. Song types **primarily** arose off eastern Australia, then **rippled** **eastward** through **successive** populations to French Polynesia, **generally** crossing the **region** within about two years.

Such rapid change, too fast for genetic **evolution**, points to **imitation** rather than **inheritance**, a **persuasive** case of **cultural** **transmission**. Why males sing, and why they **overhaul** their songs, remain **controversial**. One idea is that **novelty** itself spurs change. Some **scholars** are **reluctant** to **define** this as culture in the human sense. Whatever the label, a few **outsiders** off eastern Australia **reshaped** an entire population's music.

## 中文

每年冬天，雄性座头鲸——而且只有雄性——会让它们的**繁殖地**(breeding grounds)回荡着一段**萦绕不散的**(haunting)**旋律**(melody)。若干声音单元**构成**(constitute)乐句，乐句又组成主题，形成一个**精巧的**(elaborate)**层级结构**(hierarchy)。每一**遍演唱**(rendition)持续数分钟，整首歌则**反复出现**(recur)数小时，很可能是为了**求偶**(courtship)。同一种群的所有雄鲸都唱**完全相同的**(identical)版本，而这个版本每年都在**演变**(evolves)，只是变化**细微**(subtly)。

这是常规。可在澳大利亚东部海域，常规被打破。1995年至1998年间，**研究人员**(researchers)用水听器，即水下**麦克风**(microphones)，录下了沿**海岸线**(coastline)进行**季节性**(seasonal)**迁徙**(migration)的座头鲸。20世纪90年代中期，82只歌者中**仅仅**(merely)有两只**唱出**(emitted)了一首**与众不同的**(distinctive)新歌，与**当时盛行的**(reigning)**合唱**(chorus)**截然**(utterly)不同。

大约两年之内，**几乎**(virtually)每只歌者都已**改唱**(converted)新歌；到1998年，旧歌已被**全盘**(wholesale)**取代**(displaced)。2000年11月发表于《自然》（Nature）的一项研究发现，这首新歌与澳大利亚西部海域印度洋种群的歌**相似**(resembled)，似乎是由**一小批**(handful)来访的**歌手**(performers)向东**带来**(ferried)的。

这也并非**偶然的怪事**(freak)。2011年4月，《当代生物学》（Current Biology）上的一项研究**分析**(analysed)了南太平洋六个种群十余年的**录音**(recordings)。歌型**主要**(primarily)发端于澳大利亚东部海域，随后经由一个个**相继的**(successive)种群**如涟漪般扩散**(rippled)，**向东**(eastward)传至法属波利尼西亚，**通常**(generally)在大约两年内横贯整个**海区**(region)。

如此迅速的变化，快得远非基因**演化**(evolution)所能及，指向的是**模仿**(imitation)而非**遗传**(inheritance)，是**文化**(cultural)**传递**(transmission)的一个**有说服力的**(persuasive)例证。雄鲸为何歌唱、又为何**彻底改换**(overhaul)歌曲，至今仍**有争议**(controversial)。一种观点认为，是**新奇**(novelty)本身推动了变化。一些**学者**(scholars)**不愿**(reluctant)把这种现象**界定**(define)为人类意义上的文化。无论如何称呼，一小批**外来者**(outsiders)曾在澳大利亚东部海域**重塑**(reshaped)了整个种群的音乐。
