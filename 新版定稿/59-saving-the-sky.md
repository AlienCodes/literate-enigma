# EN: Saving the Sky
# ZH: 拯救天空
## 英文
A **layer** of **ozone** **shields** Earth **from** much of the sun's **ultraviolet** **radiation**. In June 1974 Mario Molina and Sherwood Rowland, chemists in California, warned in Nature of a **menace** in fridges and **spray** cans. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) are so **inert** that they drift **intact** into the stratosphere, high in the **atmosphere**, where they can **liberate** **chlorine**, a **catalyst** that destroys ozone **molecule** after molecule. The pair foresaw **gradual** **erosion**.

The **alarm**, when it sounded, was **abrupt**. In May 1985 Joseph Farman and colleagues at the British **Antarctic** Survey reported that **ozone** over Halley Bay, their Antarctic station, had **plummeted** each southern spring since the mid-1970s. Their **humble** Dobson spectrophotometer **gauged** ozone from the ground. The staff were **persistent**, reading it in a **painstaking** **routine** of **manual** **observation**.

Yet NASA's Nimbus-7 **satellite**, a **sophisticated** eye **in orbit**, had recorded the same lows. Its software **flagged** them **as** **dubious**, since ozone that low had never been reliably measured. NASA says it was still **verifying** them when Farman published. The **sensor** had worked; **expectation** had not.

**Remarkably**, the response was **swift**. In September 1987 **diplomats** signed the Montreal **Protocol**, a **binding** **accord** to **curb** CFCs. **Amendments** **tightened** it: in June 1990 **affluent** countries **pledged** to **phase out** CFCs by 2000. In September 2009 the Protocol and its parent Vienna Convention became the UN's first **treaties** to win **universal** **ratification**.

In January 2023 a UN-backed **assessment** **forecast** **recovery** to 1980 levels around 2066 over **Antarctica**, 2045 over the **Arctic**, and 2040 elsewhere. **Healing** will take decades. The sky's **rescue** began with **diligent** observers who trusted that a **baffling** number was no **glitch**, and published it.

然而，美国国家航空航天局（NASA）的雨云7号**卫星**(satellite)，这只**精密的**(sophisticated)眼睛**在轨道上运行**(in orbit)，也记录到了同样的低值。（a sophisticated eye in orbit：同位语，补充说明卫星）（lows：名词复数，低值）它的软件**把**(flagged as)这些读数**标记为**(flagged as)**可疑**(dubious)，因为这么低的臭氧此前从没有被可靠地测到过。（flag A as B：把 A 标记为 B）（that low：that 作副词，这么低，ozone that low 即这么低的臭氧）据NASA的说法，它还在**核实**(verifying)这些读数，就在法曼发表论文的时候。（NASA says…：注明是 NASA 一方的说法；when Farman published：时间状语从句）**传感器**(sensor)并没有出问题；**预期**(expectation)却出了问题。（had not：省略了 worked，即 expectation had not worked）

**引人注目的是**(Remarkably)，各国的反应十分**迅速**(swift)。1987年9月，**外交官**(diplomats)们签署了《蒙特利尔**议定书**(Protocol)》，这是一项具有**约束力的**(binding)**协定**(accord)，用来**限制**(curb)CFCs。（a binding accord…：同位语，补充说明议定书；to curb CFCs：不定式作后置定语，修饰 accord）此后的**修正案**(Amendments)又**收紧了**(tightened)它的管控：1990年6月，**富裕**(affluent)国家**承诺**(pledged)在2000年前**逐步淘汰**(phase out)CFCs。（pledge to do：承诺做……；phase out：逐步淘汰）2009年9月，该议定书与其所依据的《维也纳公约》一道，成为联合国首批获得**普遍**(universal)**批准**(ratification)的**条约**(treaties)。（its parent Vienna Convention：议定书所依据的母公约《维也纳公约》）（to win…：不定式作后置定语，修饰 treaties）

2023年1月，一份由联合国支持的**评估**(assessment)报告**预测**(forecast)，臭氧层将**恢复**(recovery)到1980年的水平：约2066年在**南极**(Antarctica)上空，2045年在**北极**(Arctic)上空，其他地区则在2040年。（UN-backed：由联合国支持的；forecast：过去式与原形同形）（recovery to 1980 levels：恢复到1980年的水平）臭氧层的**愈合**(Healing)还需要数十年。天空得以**拯救**(rescue)，始于一群**勤勉的**(diligent)观测者，他们相信，一个**令人费解的**(baffling)数字并不是**小故障**(glitch)，于是把它发表了出来。（who…：定语从句，修饰 observers；was no glitch：并不是小故障；and published it：与 trusted 并列，it 指那个数字）（简述：1. 预警（1974年）科学家发现，咱们冰箱和喷雾罐里用的“氟利昂”（CFCs），飘到天上后会破坏臭氧层（臭氧层是替地球挡紫外线的保护伞）。2. 发现与乌龙（1985年）法曼团队在南极发现，天上的臭氧居然破了个大洞！最搞笑的是，NASA的卫星其实早就拍到了这个异常，但电脑软件觉得数据太离谱，把它当成“机器故障”给自动屏蔽了。3. 人类反击（1987年起）各国政府一看，天塌了，赶紧签协议（《蒙特利尔议定书》），全面禁用这种化学物质。4. 奇迹般的好结局（现在）因为全球行动，2023年评估说，臭氧层估计再过几十年就能长好、恢复原样了。）

## 史实与来源
- 1974年6月（28 June 1974），加州大学欧文分校化学家莫利纳与罗兰在《自然》发表论文，提出氯氟烃升至平流层后释放氯，氯原子催化破坏臭氧——理论预测缓慢、全球性变薄，并非“发现臭氧空洞”。Nature 249:810–812（只核到检索摘要）：https://www.nature.com/articles/249810a0 ；正文不写“证明”，不写氯原子破坏臭氧分子的具体倍数。
- 臭氧层吸收太阳大部分紫外辐射：通识（NOAA CSL 臭氧评估执行摘要同此口径，只核到摘要）：https://csl.noaa.gov/assessments/ozone/2022/
