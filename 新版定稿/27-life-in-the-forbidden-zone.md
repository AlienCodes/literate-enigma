# EN: Life in the Forbidden Zone
# ZH: 禁区里的生机
## 英文
Chernobyl is **synonymous with** **disaster**, yet it holds a **startling** amount of **wildlife**. Early on 26 April 1986, one of its **reactors** exploded in Soviet Ukraine, **dispersing** **radioactive** **fallout** across Europe. On 27 April, nearby Pripyat **was emptied of** about 45,000 **inhabitants** within hours. A zone some 30 kilometres in **radius** became **off-limits**, and Belarus **fenced off** **adjoining** **terrain**.

In October 2015, a study in **Current Biology** combined **aerial** surveys with **footprint** counts on the Belarusian side. **Elk**, **roe deer**, red deer, and wild **boar** were **comparable** in **density** **to** those in four **reserves** free of **contamination**, and wolves were over seven times as **numerous**. Earlier **helicopter** counts showed **elk**, **roe deer**, and **boar** **surging** in the **post-disaster** decade.

Later **fieldwork** **bolstered** these findings. In April 2016, camera traps **snapped** wolves, boar, and raccoon dogs near the most **hazardous** spots, with no sign of **suppressed** numbers. The **probable** reason is **mundane**: the **absence** of **agriculture**, **forestry**, and hunting.

Smaller **organisms** fared worse in some studies. One, in March 2007, found fewer **nesting** birds, and fewer species, where radiation was more **intense**. Another, in March 2009, found **pollinators** and spiders less **plentiful** where **exposure** was greater. A third, in March 2014, found leaves **decomposing** about 40 per cent less at the most **polluted** sites, **impeding** the return of nutrients to the soil.

Counting heads cannot **guarantee** the health of **individual** animals, and **how much damage** low but **continual** doses **inflict** remains **contentious**, as studies **clash over** methods. The **burgeoning** **herds** may **trumpet** the **dividend** of human **departure**, but the **sluggish** **rot** of fallen leaves keeps a quieter **ledger** of radiation's cost.

清点头数无法**保证**(guarantee)动物**个体**(individual)的健康；而低但**持续**(continual)的剂量会**造成**(inflict)**多大损害**(how much damage)，目前仍然存在**争议**(contentious)，因为各项研究**在**(clash over)方法**上存在冲突**(clash over)。（count heads：清点头数，这里指统计动物数量）（how much damage…inflict：主语从句，damage 是 inflict 的宾语，提到了前面；remain + 形容词：仍然……）（clash over…：在……上冲突）**迅速增长的**(burgeoning)**兽群**(herds)或许在**宣扬**(trumpet)人类**离开**(departure)所带来的**红利**(dividend)，但落叶**迟缓的**(sluggish)**腐烂**(rot)，记着一笔更安静的**账**(ledger)：辐射的代价。（trumpet：大肆宣扬，与后面的 quieter 对比；the dividend of…：……带来的红利）（keep a ledger of…：记下……的账，这里比喻记录代价）（简述：切尔诺贝利是灾难的代名词，这里却有大量野生动物。1986年4月26日凌晨，一座反应堆爆炸，放射性沉降物飘散到欧洲；第二天，普里皮亚季约45,000名居民在数小时内撤空，周围半径约30公里成为禁区，白俄罗斯也围起了相邻地带。2015年的研究发现，白俄罗斯一侧驼鹿、狍、马鹿和野猪的密度与未受污染的保护区相当，狼的数量是后者的七倍多；2016年，相机陷阱在危险最大的地点附近也拍到了狼、野猪和貉，可能的原因是这里没有农耕、伐木和狩猎。不过，2007、2009、2014年的研究发现，辐射越强的地方，筑巢的鸟、传粉昆虫和蜘蛛越少，落叶分解也慢了约40%。动物数量多不能保证个体健康，低剂量持续辐射的危害至今仍有争议。）
