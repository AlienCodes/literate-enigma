# EN: Apollo 13: The Successful Failure
# ZH: 阿波罗13号：成功的失败
## 英文
On April 11th 1970 NASA **launched** Apollo 13, meant for the third **crewed** **lunar landing**. On April 13th, some 200,000 miles out, an **oxygen tank** **exploded** in the **service module**, home to most of the ship's oxygen and power. The **blast** **crippled** the **spacecraft**; the landing was **aborted**.

The **astronauts** **took** **refuge** **in** the **lunar module**, Aquarius, built to support two men for about two days. Using its **engine**, the crew **steered** the **craft** **onto** a **trajectory** around the Moon, whose gravity **swung** it home. They **rationed** water, **conserved power**, and **shivered** in a **bitterly** cold **cabin**.

A quieter **hazard** **emerged**: with a third man aboard, **carbon dioxide** was **accumulating**. The command module, the crew's **capsule**, used square **filter** **cartridges**; Aquarius's **sockets** were round. From plastic bags, **cardboard**, and **tape** on board, ground engineers **devised** an **adapter**, then **talked** the crew **through** **assembling** it. On April 17th the capsule **splashed down** in the Pacific.

Yet the **fault** had been **built in** on Earth. A NASA **review board** **traced** the **explosion** **to** the tank's **thermostat** **switches**, designed for the ship's 28-**volt** supply. In 1965 the spacecraft's **builder** **rewrote** the **specification** to let the **heaters** **run on** 65-volt launch-site power; the switches were never **upgraded**, a **mismatch** neither that **contractor** nor NASA **detected**. Weeks before launch, to **drain** the **stubborn** tank, a **heater** ran about eight hours. The switches probably **malfunctioned**; **unchecked** heat **scorched** the **insulation** on the tank's **wiring**; in flight, that insulation **ignited**.

NASA called Apollo 13 a "successful failure". The **paradox** holds: a flight **undone by** a five-year-old engineering **oversight** was **salvaged** within four days by **ingenuity** on the ground.

- 【T1】1970年4月11日发射阿波罗13号，原定为第三次载人登月（阿波罗11、12号已登月，为通识）。NASA 历史摘要（被代理拦截，只核到检索摘要）：https://www.nasa.gov/mission/apollo-13/ ；Britannica：https://www.britannica.com/topic/Apollo-13 （只核到摘要）。
