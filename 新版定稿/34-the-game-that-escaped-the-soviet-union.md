# EN: The Game That Escaped the Soviet Union
# ZH: 走出苏联的俄罗斯方块

## 英文
In June 1984 Alexey Pajitnov, a 29-year-old **Moscow** **coder**, grew **engrossed in** a **diversion** he had built. His **primitive** Elektronika 60 had a **purely** **textual** display, so every **geometric** piece of four **tiles** was **assembled from** **characters**. The result proved **addictive**. He called it **Tetris**, a **fusion** of the **Greek** tetra- and tennis, his favourite **pastime**.

In the Soviet Union, the state, not its **salaried** **designer**, was the **proprietor** of this **intellectual property**, and Pajitnov held no **entitlement** to it. Copies **mushroomed** **freely** on **floppy disks** across the **Eastern bloc**.

In 1986 Robert Stein, an **enterprising** British **software** **vendor**, **stumbled upon** **Tetris** in **Hungary**. **In** his **ignorance** **of** Elorg, the **state bureau** **regulating** Soviet **software** exports, he **hastily** **hawked** rights he did not yet **legitimately** hold. The rights were **shuffled** **onward** through a **chaotic** **muddle** of **overlapping** deals among Western firms, one owned by the **magnate** Robert Maxwell.

In February 1989 Henk Rogers, a **resourceful** Dutch-born **publisher**, reached **Moscow** on a tourist **visa** and **found his way to** Elorg. Stein and Kevin Maxwell, the magnate's son, were in **Moscow** that same **hectic** week of **negotiations**, each a **bidder**. Pajitnov felt a **fondness** for Rogers, and **Nintendo** **scooped** the handheld rights, then worldwide **console** rights.

**Nintendo** **bundled** **Tetris** with its **pocket-sized** Game Boy, and that **version** shipped over 35 million copies. Pajitnov **reaped** none of the **spoils** for years. His **earnings** began only after his **arrangement**'s **expiry** around 1995. In 1996 he and Rogers **founded** The Tetris Company to manage the rights.

The man whose **puzzle** once spread for nothing later helped decide who could sell it.

## 史实与来源
- 【T1】1984年6月，29岁的计算机工程师阿列克谢·帕基特诺夫在莫斯科苏联科学院多罗德尼岑计算中心，用一台只能显示文字的 Elektronika 60 完成俄罗斯方块核心玩法，方块由字符组成；名字由希腊前缀 tetra-（每块四格）与他最爱的运动网球（tennis）合成。英文 coder、pastime 为对"工程师""运动"的通俗说法；fusion 指名字的合成。S1
- 【T2】他是国家雇员，游戏归国家所有；软盘拷贝在莫斯科和东欧免费流传。英文 Copies mushroomed freely 为对"免费流传"的生动化表述。S2
- 【T3】1986年，英国软件商罗伯特·斯坦因在匈牙利看到俄罗斯方块，想获得授权；他从未听说过管理苏联软件出口的国家机构 Elorg（英文以 in his ignorance 表述；hastily 是对"未获授权先行出售"的概括），在拿到授权前就把版权卖了出去；随后版权在 Mirrorsoft（属媒体大亨罗伯特·马克斯韦尔）、Spectrum HoloByte、雅达利旗下 Tengen、罗杰斯的 Bullet-Proof Software 等西方公司间层层转卖转授，英文概括为 chaotic muddle。公司名正文不逐一列出。S3
- 【T4】1989年2月，荷兰出生的发行商亨克·罗杰斯持旅游签证到莫斯科，2月21日找到 Elorg（主管别利科夫，姓名正文不写）；同一周斯坦因和凯文·马克斯韦尔也在莫斯科争夺版权（英文以 hectic 描写）；帕基特诺夫喜欢罗杰斯；Elorg 把掌机版权、后又把全球主机版权给了任天堂（英文 scooped 对应 granted）。resourceful 为对其独闯莫斯科的概括。S4
- 【T5】任天堂把俄罗斯方块与新出的 Game Boy 捆绑销售（1989年4月日本、7月北美，正文不写）；Game Boy 版出货超过3,500万份。S5
- 【T6】帕基特诺夫多年分文未得；约1995年协议到期后开始拿到版税（他1991年已移居美国，故英文删去"when he moved to America"，中文也不写移居）；1996年他与罗杰斯成立俄罗斯方块公司（The Tetris Company）管理版权。结尾"后来参与决定谁能卖它"即据此，字面成立。S6
