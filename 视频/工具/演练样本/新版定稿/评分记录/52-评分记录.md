## 逐轮扣分明细
### 首评
- **英文总编辑 93**："triple the first figure" 把时间窗不同的两数直接相比（史实 −1）；"The real villain" 与结尾 "among the chief offenders" 口径不一、P4 提前点名煤火使结尾重复（论证 −2）；captive/imprisoned 隐喻堆叠、标题夸张（语言 −2）；comfort/response 偏基础（−1）；中文"讨厌事"口语（−1）。
- **语言学教授 91.5**：blamed 太基础不应占名额；速查表 combat 只给语境义"治理"；"被迫取消"误译（实为中场中止）；"当作讨厌事来容忍"欧化；grates 译"炉排"（锅炉术语）；the grudging response 指代后置；Contemporary officials 易误读为"当代"；Sadler's Wells、royal assent 无交代；reassessed the mortality 搭配不当；"顶住…直到"句子悬空。
- **英文母语读者 88.5**：标题；and 丢转折、ensuing deaths 指代不清；reassessed the mortality；comfort so habitual 修饰不当；grudging response 指代倒置；blanket 与 lid 混合比喻；withstood calls 少见；beloved in British homes 不地道；Sadler's Wells、Smithfield Show、royal assent 无交代；结尾重复 P4；"肉牛"增义；"炉排"。
- **总编辑本人 97**：villain 口径；P1/P4 重复；隐喻堆叠；"自满"不准；"顶住…直到"欧化。——**未发现标题问题与 triple 时间窗问题，由其他评审查出。**

### 首评后的修改（总编辑本人据四份评审改英文，语言学家同步中文）
新标题；warmer air pressed down like a lid（去 blanket）；smoke was trapped（去 imprisoned）；a choking smog；the Sadler's Wells theatre；the Smithfield agricultural show；The smog dissipated；Officials at the time … but a 1954 … report blamed later deaths on influenza；2001 研究 reassessed the evidence and cast doubt on that explanation（删 triple）；P4 改为 "For decades Londoners had tolerated the smoke as a nuisance. One villain flickered in their own homes…"（不在 P4 点名煤火，留到结尾）；the government's grudging response. It withstood pressure for an inquiry…；Reform proved protracted, slowed by official inertia；became law；municipal councils；beloved of British families。重点词去 blanket、imprisoned、contemporary、blamed、mortality、triple、grates、ensuing，增 choking、agricultural、dissipated、explanation、inertia、municipal 等。中文：演到幕间休息被迫中止、麻烦、麻痹大意、才松口、纯种牛、成为法律。

### 会签后按评审原文落实
pedigree → prize（不加粗，重点词 50 个，中文「获奖的牛」，速查表删 pedigree）；"One villain flickered in their own grates, a source of warmth and comfort so habitual that few thought to accuse it."（中文「就在他们自家的炉栅里摇曳」）；结尾 "had, all along, been among the chief offenders."；中文「把煤烟看成一种麻烦，容忍了下来」「从源头上遏制煤烟」（速查表 combat 增「遏制」）。母语读者"在 P4 点明煤火"的建议与英文总编辑"把揭晓留到结尾"的意见相左，两位总编辑取后者。改后 270 词、50 重点词，draft_check 硬性全过。
